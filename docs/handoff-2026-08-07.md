**1. A MILLION SEATS CONVERGE.** `docs/scale-1m-2026-08-07.md`.

**2. FRONT 3 IS ANSWERED** — `docs/front3-descent-2026-08-06.md`. The N=5000
plateau was never a contest-cost or capacity problem: at the plateau **100.0%
of pass-0 descents offer exactly ONE candidate** (the child-row head, the only
child a parent hears first-hand), `meanHops` is exactly 12.00, and 11,259 free
cells sit unreached one column over. FOUR fixes are refuted with numbers — do
not rebuild any of them. T7 (spread-after-NOROOM) fixes it and is OFF because
it mints 18 duplicate seats at N=50000 (non-monotonic; 100k is clean) and costs
tree compactness.

**3. V5 IS SETTLED AS A DIAGNOSIS AND OPEN AS A FIX — THREE ATTEMPTS REFUTED.**
Q2 compaction makes one seat 5x hotter than the floor (15.16 vs 3.13
framesPerTick_max at N=20000). The scale audit said that was the probes, **and
the audit was RIGHT** — a per-message-type instrument (branch
`v5-mt-instrument`) shows FIND is 79.4% of that seat's traffic (72,188 frames),
the ON-OFF delta is FIND TO THE FRAME at every N, and FIND is the only type
that tracks N. I claimed the opposite mid-session; that was an
apples-to-oranges error (a cumulative counter against a run that never passed
COMPACT_SETTLE) and it is corrected in the audit.

Why the seat: POSITION. `up({pc,r,0}) = {parent,r,lastDigit}` maps every
child-section "0" onto Section-1 COLUMN-0 cells, and "only a row head decides"
makes row-mates forward everything to the head (mates 12-17k, head 72k ~= their
sum). It does ZERO work for them — cAdmits 412->412, cMoves 209->209.

THREE REFUTED FIXES, do not rebuild any: (a) exponential backoff on misses — no
effect; (b) NOROOMUP walk-down + node suppression (Nathan's design, my broken
implementation — the non-head branch never set the breadcrumb, so suppression
never armed deep) — a NET LOSS; (c) gating the probe on `rootDig.dmin` — zero
effect, because the settled room really does have frontier cells at depth 2, so
"is anything shallower than me free?" is TRUE FOR ALMOST EVERY LEAF.

**THE REFRAME:** V5 is a CONTENTION problem, not an information problem. Every
probe is rational; there are simply ~10,000 leaves competing for 3-31 cells. The
fourth design — admission control on the probe, proportional to frontier size —
is named, unbuilt, and must be MEASURED like the three before it.

## THE TWO DECISIONS — ANSWERED, WITH WORK ATTACHED

**1. Compaction stays ON.** Nathan's call, 2026-08-07. My recommendation, not
yet built: **gate it on ROOM SIZE.** p50 is flat and the funnel only
concentrates as N grows, so below a few thousand seats compaction is nearly free
and does its job, while above that it is pure cost doing nothing (cAdmits
412->412, cMoves 209->209 in a settled window). A size gate keeps the benefit
where rooms actually live, removes the O(N) growth where it would bite, and does
NOT red `repro-compaction` (which asserts ON beats OFF in the shrink scenario).
**NATHAN HAS ANOTHER IDEA HE WANTS TO DISCUSS FIRST — do not build anything
until that conversation happens.**

**RETRY NOTES FOR THE NOROOMUP DESIGN (Nathan's), because it was NEVER FAIRLY
TESTED.** What I measured was my BROKEN implementation: the non-head forwarding
branch never set `lastProbeFrom`, so the answer died at the first Section-1
non-head and suppression never armed anywhere deep (MTDUMP: NOROOMUP ~= FIND at
S1 non-heads, ~0 deep). Four things a fair retry needs:
  - breadcrumb on EVERY forwarding hop, including the non-head branch;
  - arm suppression at S1 row heads on ORIGINATION;
  - leaf backoff >= COMPACT_PERIOD — mine set `compactAt = TICK + 6..12`, which
    retries SOONER than the normal 90-180, the opposite of its own comment;
  - **size the window to the SUBTREE, not to a guess.** I shortened it 10s -> 3s
    when lone rows grew; that was probably backwards. A probe that REACHES an S1
    head has already cost the frame we are paying for, so suppression only pays
    if it arms BELOW S1 — and for a node to be armed when its subtree's next
    probe arrives, the stamp must outlive roughly `135 / L` ticks, where L is the
    leaves beneath it (a 5-leaf node needs ~27 ticks; a 25-leaf node ~5). At 3s
    almost nothing armed except right under the apex, where it is already too
    late to help.
