## What this release is

**The link release.** A link can now arrive with the first move already made:
`gifos.app/?run=anyroad&go.at=36.0640,-112.1400&go.fly=1` lands a stranger in
the Grand Canyon with the wings out, and Anyroad's ☰ sheet will produce that
link for you. GifOS shows what the link is asking for, in the app's own words,
attributes it to the sender rather than the app, and does nothing until you say
yes. Also: Ask AI keeps every conversation (searchable History, deletion only
through the trash behind a confirm), answers stream word by word including from
on-device brains, a second and much better offline voice (KittenTTS Nano), an
EXPERIMENTAL/slow warning ahead of the 1.75 GB brain downloads, a progress bar
while an app copies across the mesh, and the staged-feed freeze is gone.

## The one red the gate found, and what it actually was

`e2e-chess-mp` came up **RED TWICE** in the pre-flight gate — the same suite the
0.9.6 handoff records as a former blocker, and it had been GREEN in the 0.9.6
gate twice on this same box in the same tier context. That looks exactly like a
regression, and it is not one.

The failure is `frame.click: Timeout 30000ms exceeded` on the FIRST move — the
click is never dispatched at all. The app rebuilds `#fBoard` wholesale on its
`HB_MS=3000` presence beat (by design, ~2 rebuilds per 3s), so every `.sq` node
is replaced on a timer, and Playwright will not click an element until it is
STABLE across two consecutive animation frames. On a busy box the rebuilds and
the frames interleave so that gate never opens.

**Measured, interleaved on one box, HEAD against the shipped 0.9.6 tree:**

| box state | HEAD | 0.9.6 (`d907be3`) |
|---|---|---|
| idle | 2/2 GREEN | 2/2 GREEN |
| full-core synthetic load | 3/3 RED | 3/3 RED |

Identical in both directions, same assertion, same first move. Fixed in the
SUITE (`464caac`): the click retries once without the stability gate. Nothing
asserted changed — mutation-tested by aiming the destination click at the wrong
square, which still reds with `0 moves`.

It went **GREEN in 82s in the official gate, in real tier context, first try**.

- **`e2e-anyroad` should be SPLIT, not budgeted again** (carried from 0.9.6).
- The app-copy progress bar's only guard is a SOURCE SCAN

## Process notes paid for this session

- **Fan the gate across the farm.** Four boxes ran in parallel — the
  authoritative gate on the 8-core host, the behavior battery on clawbox, a
  second-architecture confirmation on the Pi, and the unit tier on pi-16gb. The
  second-box runs are free confidence and they cost nothing but ssh.
- **Never run `release.sh` on the raspberrypi.** Its `run_one` reaps browsers
  after EVERY suite, and only 3 of the Pi's 12 resident chromes carry
  `GIFOS_RESIDENT=1`, so the gate would cut the MonitorBot's session. Run the
  non-browser tiers directly instead — that is what the second-box script does.
- **The gate refuses to start against someone else's stack.** Leftover
  `http.server 8099` / `relay-local` from earlier debugging made both the main
  gate and the behavior tier exit immediately with "ports already in use". That
  is the gate being right; clear your own stack first.
- **`scp` does not carry the executable bit** — four staged launchers all failed
  with "Permission denied" until chmod'd on each box.
- **A pre-flight gate is worth its wall-clock.** Running the whole thing an hour
  before the freeze turned the one red into something already fixed and verified
  by the time the official gate started.
