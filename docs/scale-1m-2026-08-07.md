# A MILLION SEATS CONVERGE — 2026-08-07

> **LIVE, and read the caveat before quoting the number.** This is the C++
> reference sim (`test/sim/mesh.cpp`), and it converges a million WITH T7
> `spreadon 1`, which is DEFAULT OFF — see docs/front3-descent-2026-08-06.md for
> why (a duplicate-seat burst at N=50000, and the compaction trade). So the
> claim this doc supports is **"the seating protocol converges a million"**, not
> "GifOS holds a million people today". The browser twin, real WebRTC, real
> relays and real devices are all still ahead of it.

Box: pi-16gb (Raspberry Pi 5 class, aarch64, 4 cores, 16 GB) with its resident
inference stack stopped. Wall clock 22,447 s (~6h14m), single-threaded
(`--threads>1` is refused as unsound — docs/sim-threads-2026-08-06.md).
**Peak RSS 7,077 MB** — about 7.1 KB per seat, which is the number to plan by.

## The curve (det, seed 20260714, T7 on, every rung CHECK PASS, dups=0)

| N | ticks to converge | peak RSS |
|---|---|---|
| 3,000 | 2,624 | — |
| 5,000 | 3,200 | — |
| 10,000 | 3,776 | — |
| 20,000 | 4,480 | — |
| 50,000 | 8,512 | 339 MB |
| 100,000 | 6,848 | 679 MB |
| 200,000 | 5,888 | 1,351 MB |
| **1,000,000** | **8,128** | **7,077 MB** |

333x the seats for 3.1x the time. Sub-linear, close to logarithmic — which is
what the law asks for. At the canonical 500 ms/tick, 8,128 ticks is ~68 minutes
of simulated time to seat a million.

Memory is linear and modest: ~7.1 KB/seat, so a million fits in 8 GB. That is a
SIM cost (every seat's maps live in one process), not a per-participant cost —
the law's per-node budget is the O(C²) occ map, measured flat at occ_max 30-31
from N=500 all the way to N=20000.

**N=50,000 is the outlier and it is not noise:** 8,512 ticks and, at that rung
only, `seated=49986/50000 DUPS 18 CHECK FAIL` on the earlier ladder — the T7
duplicate burst. Non-monotonic (100k is clean), so a race whose window depends
on topology rather than a threshold. It is the single reason T7 is off, and it
is documented in front3-descent-2026-08-06.md.

## What this does and does not establish

ESTABLISHED (measured): the seating/healing protocol — admission, the tree,
the healing laws, dedup — converges a million seats from a single join storm
with zero duplicates, zero stranded seats and zero teleports, and its
convergence time grows sub-linearly.

NOT established: anything about transports. The sim's fabric models loss,
severance, subnets, latency and quality, but NOT the ESTABLISHMENT of a link —
"can these two reach each other", never "have these two finished negotiating".
Every expensive bug this month lived in that gap (test/README.md, "The gap that
remains"). A million browsers is a different question from a million seats.

Also not established: the per-node MESSAGE budget at that scale. `DIGGAUGE`
measures it and it is flat in N for the digest, but the V5 compaction funnel
(docs/scale-audit-2026-08-06.md) is an open O(N) violation at the hottest node,
still being measured.

## Reproducing

    g++ -O2 -std=c++17 -o /tmp/mesh test/sim/mesh.cpp
    printf 'det on\nspreadon 1\ninit 1000000\nconverge 400000\nstate\ndups\ncheck\n' \
      | /tmp/mesh --service

Needs ~8 GB and about six hours on a 4-core ARM box. N=200,000 is the same
shape in 43 minutes and 1.4 GB if you want the answer sooner.
