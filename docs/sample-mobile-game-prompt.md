# $30M Game Prompt

> Sample prompt for generating high-fidelity mobile game gameplay sequences.
> Source: ernestosoftware.com

## Prompt

Generate an exceptionally high-fidelity, meticulously detailed, vertically oriented 9:16 mobile game gameplay sequence that flawlessly mimics the hyper-polished, top-grossing aesthetic of a multi-million dollar revenue hyper-casual runner advertisement. The camera must be positioned in a smooth, perfectly stabilized third-person tracking perspective, hovering just behind and slightly above the player character group, maintaining a dynamic but locked focal distance that emphasizes constant forward momentum. The environment is a breathtaking, ancient desert canyon corridor, flanked on both sides by impossibly tall, sheer sandstone cliffs exhibiting photorealistic geological strata, deep terracotta and burnt orange hues, and intricate weather-worn texturing. Massive, ancient architectural wonders, including colossal stone brick archways with crumbling edges and ornate, time-eroded carvings, periodically bridge the gap between the canyon walls, casting majestic, ray-traced volumetric shadows across the golden, sun-baked sand of the linear pathway below. The atmospheric lighting is set to a cinematic golden hour, with brilliant, warm sunlight filtering down through the dust-motes in the air, creating god-rays that illuminate the playing field with a premium, high-contrast, perfectly balanced exposure.

At the center of this visually stunning corridor runs a tight, disciplined squad of stylized, futuristic soldiers. These protagonists are clad in high-gloss, premium cyan-blue sci-fi armor with smooth, rounded tactical helmets featuring glowing visor slits. They carry sleek, futuristic energy blasters that pulse with a soft neon luminescence. As they run forward with a synchronized, heavy-stepping, deeply satisfying tactical jogging animation, their boots kick up subtle, physics-based puffs of desert dust. The squad approaches a massive, glowing, semi-transparent forcefield gate that completely bisects the canyon path. This gate is rendered as a shimmering, holographic barrier of crackling cyan energy, featuring a slight glass-like refraction effect that distorts the environment behind it, and is boldly emblazoned with a massive, brilliantly illuminated white typography that reads "x2". The instant the blue squad breaches the threshold of this ethereal gate, a visually spectacular and flawlessly smooth multiplication mechanic occurs. The characters instantly duplicate in a fluid, pop-in-free animation, physically expanding their formation outward into a massive, organized battalion of identical blue soldiers, instantly doubling their firepower.

Immediately following this dopamine-inducing multiplication, the newly expanded squad engages in high-octane, relentless forward combat. They unleash a ceaseless, synchronized barrage of glowing, neon-blue laser projectiles from their blasters. These brilliant energy bolts streak down the sandy corridor toward a massive, incoming horde of enemy forces. The antagonists are identical in silhouette to the protagonists but are clad in an aggressive, dark crimson-red armor, creating a striking and instantly readable color contrast against the blue squad and the orange canyon walls. The red enemies charge recklessly toward the player's formation, but as the relentless storm of blue laser fire makes impact, the enemies do not simply fall; they are obliterated in an immensely satisfying, physics-driven explosion of premium visual effects.

This gauntlet of destruction culminates in an epic, screen-filling boss encounter beneath the largest and most imposing stone archway yet. A colossal, terrifying, heavily armored golden scorpion dominates the pathway, its massive scale dwarfing the human soldiers. The creature is a masterpiece of creature design, featuring thick, interlocking, metallic gold chitinous plates that gleam under the harsh desert sun, massive articulated pincers that snap menacingly, and a towering, segmented tail tipped with a stinger that radiates a menacing, ethereal orange energy. As the combined might of the massive blue squad focuses their continuous laser fire entirely upon this towering arachnid behemoth, the creature's armor begins to crack with glowing internal light before violently detonating. The climax of the sequence sees the scorpion and the remaining red enemies shattering not into gore, but into a spectacular, screen-shaking cascade of thousands of glittering, physics-enabled gold coins and shimmering loot. These coins explode outward in a perfect parabolic arc, raining down upon the sand with hyper-realistic metallic reflections and bouncing with satisfying weight, perfectly encapsulating the irresistible, highly rewarding visual feedback loop designed to maximize player retention and conversion.
