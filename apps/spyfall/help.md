# Spyfall

One of you is the spy. The rest of you are at the same place, each with a job there. The spy does not know the place. Ask questions out loud. Catch the spy — or, if you are the spy, guess where you are.

## Two ways to play

**Pass this phone** — one device, around the table. Add at least three names, pick 6 / 8 / 10 minutes, then **Deal**. Each person taps **their own name**, looks at their card, and taps **Hide this card**. Hide never opens the next card. Hand the phone on. When everyone has looked, **Start the round**.

**Play with friends** — everyone on their own phone. This button opens the room. It does **not** send the link. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app, then send that link. Whoever opens it is in the same game. There is no lobby code and no game server. Need three people to deal.

## Roles

- Everyone but one person sees the **place** (a bank, a pirate ship, a circus tent, …) and a **job** there.
- The **spy** sees neither. They listen, bluff, and try to name the place before time runs out.
- One person is told they ask the **first question**. After that, questions bounce around the room.

Ask things that sound ordinary if you belong there, and odd if you do not. Do not name the place out loud unless you are accusing.

## During a round

- Tap the timer to pause (host only, in a live game). Tap again to resume.
- Tap a place on the list to cross it off. **Marks stay on this phone.** They are not votes and they are not published.
- Tap a player name the same way, as a private note of who you suspect.
- In a live game, **Your role · tap to show** hides your card from shoulder-surfers. Tap again to hide it.
- On one phone, **Look at a card** is how you peek at your own card again. Only tap your own name.

When the timer hits zero, or the host taps **End round**, the place and the spy are revealed. Say who you think it was, then deal again.

## Invite and Save

**Invite** in the bar above the app is the room. Each person writes only their own row. Your card stays on your device. Location marks stay on your device.

**Save** keeps the app. It does not keep a round, a name list, or those marks. Next open, you start from the home screen.

An unofficial port of [Spyfall](https://github.com/tannerkrewson/spyfall) by tannerkrewson. The party game was designed by Alexandr Ushan; this is a digital dealer of that idea, not their product.
