# Ludo

A race around the board. Four colours, four tokens each. First to get **all four home** wins.

This is generic Ludo, not a branded product.

## Rules

- Each colour sits a corner and has **four tokens** in the yard.
- **Roll a 6** to move a token out onto its start square.
- Tokens run **clockwise** around the 52 squares, then up their own coloured home stretch (six squares, exact count).
- **56 steps** from leaving the yard to home.
- Land on an opponent who is **not** sitting their start square and they go back to the yard. You stay.
- A token may not land on one of its own. Two tokens on one square sit side by side, not on top of each other.
- A **6** is another roll. **Three sixes in a row** skip the turn.
- If no token can use the roll, the turn passes.

## Controls

Tap **Roll** (or the die), then tap a **lit** token. Only legal tokens light up.

On a phone it is the same tap. There is no drag.

**New game** starts again with the same colours.

## This device or a friend

- **Play on this device** — 2, 3, or 4 colours. Pass the phone. Red starts. **Continue** resumes a game saved in this file.
- **Play with friends**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app. Seats fill as people open the link: Red, Green, Yellow, Blue. You only roll and move on your colour. Empty colours are skipped. Extras watch. There is no lobby.

## What is saved

A local game in progress lives in this file. A friend’s board lives in the room, not in your private file.
