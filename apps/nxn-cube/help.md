# NxN Cube

Turn the faces of a colourful cube until each side is one colour. Start at 3×3, or pick any size from a pocket **2×2** up to **10×10**.

## Drag

There are no keyboard turns. A mouse and a finger do the same thing on the cube:

- **Drag a face** to twist that layer (it snaps to a quarter turn).
- **Drag the empty space** around the cube to spin the whole toy, so you can see the other sides.

One finger at a time. The chrome around the cube still scrolls; only the cube itself captures the drag.

## Buttons

- **Scramble** shuffles the cube and zeros your move count.
- **Restore** puts it back to solved, on your device, and zeros the count. That is a restart, not a win.
- **Size** rebuilds a solved cube at that order. Your last size stays on this device.

Moves in the corner count each snap that actually turned a layer. A drag that falls back does not count.

## Race a friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. The link is the room. Then someone presses **Scramble** — everyone who is in gets that **same** shuffle.

First to a solved cube wins. The side list shows moves and who finished. **Restore** on your phone only solves *your* cube and takes you off that scramble (you cannot win it until someone scrambles again). Changing size while a race is live shuffles a new one for everyone.

Invite is in that bar, not inside this game.

## What is saved

Your preferred size. The cube in front of you is not — close it, and you get a solved cube again. A live race lives on the invite.

An unofficial port of [rubiks-cube](https://github.com/pengfeiw/rubiks-cube). This toy is NxN Cube, not a trademarked puzzle.
