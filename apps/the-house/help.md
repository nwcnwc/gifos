# The House

You are in a strange house. You do not remember coming in. Tap or click things to walk over and look at them. Find the way out.

This is an unofficial port of Artur Kot's short point-and-click walking-sim. The rooms, the art, and the sounds are his.

## How to look

- **Tap** or **click** a hotspot (a door, a window, a note, a plant). The figure walks there, then looks.
- Some views are larger than the screen. **Drag** the room to pan. A tap is not a drag — you have to slide.
- The **speaker** in the corner mutes and unmutes.
- **reset game** (the gear) starts from the first room and forgets what you picked up.
- **Back** closes a close-up, a dialogue, the tray, or the gear. It does not quit the house.

There is no jump and no run. It is walking, looking, and using what you found. There is no Flash plugin.

## Inventory

Things you pick up sit in the **tray** at the edge of the screen. Open it, then use an item on the world. A used item leaves the tray.

## What is saved

The room you reached, the things you are carrying, and which scenes have already played stay in this copy of the app. Close it and come back: you are still in that corridor, still holding the note — your progress is saved.

This is a short game. It is meant to be finished in one sitting, but the file will wait if you do not.
