# Underrun

The year is 2718. The facility went dark. Walk the halls, shoot what comes for you, and reboot every computer on the floor. When the last one comes back, you hop to the next.

## Objective

Three floors. Spiders in the corridors, turrets around corners, health packs on the ground. Walk into a dead computer to reboot it. Reboot all of them to jump. Die and you restore from backup on the same floor.

## Controls

**Computer**

- Move: **WASD** or the arrow keys
- Aim and shoot: mouse. Hold the button to fire
- Click (or tap) the title screen to deploy
- **Tab** shows the soldier list when friends are in

**Phone**

- Left thumb walks. Right thumb aims and fires — push the stick out to shoot
- The sticks appear after you touch the screen during play

## Live friends

On your own it is the original corridor. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app to drop extra soldiers into the same halls.

- You see them. Their shots hit the things on **your** floor.
- A computer they walk into comes back here too.
- The spiders themselves stay local — each of you fights your own swarm, aimed at the nearest soldier they can see.
- Friends on another floor do not appear until you meet there.

Tap the soldier count (or Tab) for health and floor.

## What is saved

Your **best floor** lives in this file. Close it and come back — that number is still there. A live run is not saved; dying restores the current floor.
