Press Invite (GifOS menu) to send a friend the link. You both walk in the same world. Change it — they see it.

    Draw the floor. Draw a friend. Write what they say. Play when you want to walk.

    Arrows or a swipe to walk. Talk to whoever is there. Make a world of your own, or open one of the ones already here.

    Unofficial port of Bitsy (MIT). Playing alone is a tiny world that stays here. A shared room is the same writing — nobody writes on anybody else’s row.
