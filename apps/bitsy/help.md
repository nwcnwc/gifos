# Bitsy

You walk a tiny pixel room, talk to whoever is there, pick things up, and — if you want — draw a world of your own.

## Walk

- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD. Space, Enter, or any other key talks, or continues the words on screen.
- **Phone:** swipe on the room to walk. Tap the room, or **talk**, to speak. The pad under the room (▲ ◀ ▶ ▼) is the same as arrows.
- **Restart** puts you back at the start of this world. On a keyboard, Ctrl+R (or Cmd+R) does that too.

Walls block you. Walking onto a thing picks it up. Walking into a friend talks to them.

## Worlds already here

Chips under the room:

- **the example** — Adam le Doux’s cat-and-tea room
- **a blank room** — an empty box, for making
- **the porch** — evening, a plant, a note
- **next door** — two rooms, and a garden through the doorway

Your last world is remembered in this file. Close the app and open it again: the writing is still here. Your footsteps are not — you start at the usual square.

## Make

**Make** opens a small editor. This is not the full Bitsy studio.

- Paint the **16×16** floor with the selected tile, friend, or thing. **erase** clears a square.
- Paint the picture itself on the **8×8** grid. Tap a pixel to flip it.
- **New floor / New friend / New thing** add drawings. Tick **This drawing is a wall** so you cannot walk through that floor tile.
- **What they say** is the talk for the selected friend or thing.
- Room / floor / people colours sit under the paint.
- **The world as writing** is the whole game as text. Paste a Bitsy world there and press **Use this writing**.
- **Play** walks what you just made.

Tunes, extra rooms, and exits that already live in the writing are kept. There is no screen here for music, extra rooms, or doorways — type those in the writing, or paste a world that has them.

## A live friend

This app is ready to share as soon as it opens. Press **Invite** in the bar above this app to send the link. Everyone who opens it loads the **same writing**. They do **not** see your character walk: each person has their own footsteps in the same rooms. When you pick a world, press Play, or Use this writing, they get that world too.

**← Solo** stops sharing and keeps you on this device.

The host holds the room. If they leave and nobody chose to keep the room alive on Invite, the square empties.

## What is saved

This file stores the world writing you last played or made. It does not store where you were standing.
