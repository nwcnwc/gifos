# Air Hockey

Drag your paddle. Put the puck in the far goal. The other end is a computer — or a friend on their own phone.

## The table

A low ice table, one paddle on your side, one on theirs, **one puck**. Your paddle stays on **your half** — it cannot cross the centre line. There is no keyboard: the paddle follows a finger or the mouse on the ice.

- **Phone:** put a finger on the table and drag. The page does not scroll while you hold it.
- **Computer:** move the mouse over the table. You do not need to click and hold.

♪ in the corner turns the hits off. If the original sounds will not play, you hear a short beep instead.

## Scoring

A puck in the far goal is a point for you; a puck in yours is a point for them. There is **no match length** — the numbers just climb. After a goal the puck is served again.

## On your own

The far paddle is a computer that chases the puck. Its skill is fixed; there is no difficulty slider.

## A live friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. A friend sits at the **other end**. Their camera faces you, so their paddle is the near one on their screen. The score resets when they join.

You are this end if you opened the app; they are the far end. The puck is the same one both of you see. A third person who opens the link can **watch**; they do not get a paddle.

If they leave, the computer takes the far paddle back.

The person who opened the app simulates the puck. A delayed paddle can look like it hit a puck that already missed. This is table ice with a friend over a link, not a tournament.

## What is saved

Only whether sound is on. The score is not kept. Sharing the file does not share a match.
