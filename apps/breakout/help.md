# Breakout

Bounce the ball off the paddle and knock out every brick. Clear the wall to go up a level. You have **three lives**.

## Controls

**Keyboard:** ← → or **A** **D** to move. **Space** or Enter starts the game, and launches the ball if it is sitting on the paddle. **Esc** goes back to the menu. In the menu, ↑ ↓ pick a level.

**Mouse or finger:** drag on the court. The paddle follows.

**Phone:** drag the paddle, or hold ◀ / ▶ under the court. Those arrows sit in the letterbox so they never cover the bricks.

The ♪ button mutes the beeps.

## Rules

Each brick is worth more the higher it sits. The ball speeds up a little every time it breaks one, so a long rally gets sharp. Miss the ball and you lose a life. Lose the last life and it is game over — space to try again.

There are many walls. Pick one from the menu before you start, or just play: clearing a wall moves you to the next.

## A second paddle

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. A second paddle appears on the **same brick wall** — you are orange on the left, they are cyan on the right. You both keep the ball up. You both score on the same bricks. Lives are shared. After that you can both move anywhere.

The person who opened the app runs the ball. A third person who opens the link can watch; they do not get a paddle.

## What is saved

Your **high score**, the last level you picked, and whether sound is on. A game in progress is not kept. Sharing the file shares the high score.
