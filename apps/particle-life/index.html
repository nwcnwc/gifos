Press Invite (GifOS menu) to send a friend the link. You both get the same mix. Tap the jar to stir — they see your poke.

    Tap or drag to stir. Push shoves them away; switch to Pull to gather them. New mix pours a fresh set of rules.

    Unofficial port of Particle Life by hunar4321 (MIT). Playing alone is the original toy. A shared jar is the same mix, each person poking on their own — nobody writes on anybody else’s.
