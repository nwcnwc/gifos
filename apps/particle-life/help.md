# Particle Life

A jar of tiny coloured specks. Each colour is a **species**. Each species likes some species and shies from others. From that one table they clump into worms, cells, crystals — things that look alive, for a while.

There is no score and no win. You pour a mix, watch it, and stir.

## Species and attraction

A mix is a random table of **likes and shies**.

- Green might chase red and run from itself. Orange might huddle. Cyan might ignore everyone.
- The number is hidden. You do not tune each pair here — you pour a **New mix** until the jar does something you like.
- **Colours** (2–7) is how many species. More colours, wilder tables.
- **Crowd** is how many specks per colour. A packed jar is busier; a sparse one is easier to read.

**Reset** pours the **same** likes and shies again, from new starting spots. **New mix** rolls a fresh table.

The number at the top (`mix …`) is this recipe. Same number, same likes, same opening spots.

## How to play with the jar

- **Mouse:** click the jar to **Push** (specks flee your pointer). Hold **Shift** or **right-click** to **Pull** (they gather). Drag to keep stirring.
- **Phone / touch:** tap or drag. The **Push / Pull** button stands in for Shift — tap it to switch. A ring flashes where you poked.
- **Keyboard:** `r` pours a new mix. `o` resets the same mix.

Push is a shove. Pull is a gathering. Neither paints; they only stir whatever the specks are already doing.

## Solo vs a live friend

Alone, this is the original toy. The last mix (recipe, colour count, crowd) lives **in this file**. Close it, open it later, you get that jar again — not a screenshot of the last frame.

Press **Share the jar**, then press **Invite** in the bar **above** the app and send the link. Invite is not inside this page.

What a friend gets:

- The **same mix** — same likes, same shies, same opening spots.
- **Your poke**, where you touched, as push or pull. They poke on their own. Nobody writes on anybody else’s row.
- **New mix** and the colour/crowd knobs start the next round for everyone.

What Invite does **not** share: a lockstep film of every speck. Each device runs the jar itself, so after a while two jars of the same mix **drift**. A poke still lands in the same place, on whatever is happening there. That is honest, not a bug.

A friend who goes quiet for a few seconds drops off the list. **← Solo** leaves the room.
