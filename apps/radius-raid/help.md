# Radius Raid

You fly a small white ship in a dark arena. Enemies come in from the edges. Shoot them before they chew through the hull. The longer you last, the denser it gets.

## Controls

**Computer**

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys
- **Aim and fire:** mouse (hold to shoot)
- **F** — autofire on or off
- **P** — pause
- **M** — mute

**Phone**

Left thumb moves. Right thumb aims, and fires while you hold it out. **❚❚** pauses. The sticks appear after a finger has touched the screen during play.

The first round also flashes the original key list at the bottom of the arena. That copy is part of the game.

## The swarm

Thirteen kinds of enemy: straight-liners, homers, splitters, growers, stealth, towers, things that circle you. Touching one drains health. When health hits zero, the round is over — **PLAY AGAIN** or back to the menu.

About one kill in ten drops a power-up:

- **Health pack** — the hull repairs
- **Slow enemies**
- **Fast shot**
- **Triple shot**
- **Pierce shot** — bullets keep going through

Bars at the top of the arena show which ones are still running.

## Score

Each kill adds its value (tougher shapes are worth more). **SCORE** is this run; **BEST** is the highest this file has seen. **STATS** on the menu shows career totals — rounds, kills, shots, time. **CLEAR DATA** wipes them, and asks first.

## Alone and with a friend

Alone, this is the original game. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app to send a link. Extra ships drop into the same arena, firing on the same swarm. Homing enemies chase the nearest living ship.

In a shared round the score on screen is the host’s — one number for the room. If you blow up while a friend is still flying, you drop back in. If the person running the swarm leaves, the remaining ships keep firing at a frozen wave until someone starts a new round.

## Saved

Mute, autofire, high score, and career stats live in this file. Sharing the GIF shares those. A live friend’s ship is only in the room, not in the file.
