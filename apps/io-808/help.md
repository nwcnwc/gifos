# iO-808

A silver 808 on the table. Sixteen yellow steps, twelve voices, orange knobs. No samples — every hit is synthesised here.

## Make a beat

1. Tap an instrument on the strip (Bass Drum, Snare, hats, and so on).
2. Tap a **step** to put a hit on it; tap again to take it off. Yellow means on.
3. Press **Start**. The lights walk. Press again to **Stop**.
4. Twist **Level** and the extra knobs (tone, decay, snappy, tuning) on that voice.

You open on a short house pattern: four-on-the-floor bass drum, snare on 2 and 4, closed hats on the eights, clap with the snare.

**Clear** wipes the pattern you are on, not the others.

## The sequencer

- **16 steps.** One bar. The numbers 1–16 are the pads; the small 1–4 mark the quarter-notes.
- **16 patterns.** The row of pattern buttons is sixteen slots. Tap one to switch. Programming on pattern 3 does not change pattern 1.
- **A / AB / B.** Two versions of the same pattern. **A** plays A, **B** plays B, **AB** flips A then B each time the bar restarts.
- **1st / 2nd.** Two bars you can program. **This copy always plays 1st at 16 steps.** 2nd is there to edit, but it never runs — there is no length control to chain it.
- **Tempo** (40–300), **Fine** (±10), **Volume**.

## Voices

Accent, Bass Drum, Snare, Low/Mid/Hi Tom (or Conga), Rim (or Claves), Clap (or Maracas), Cowbell, Cymbal, Open Hat, Closed Hat. Toms, rim and clap have a switch for the other sound.

## Play and share

Playing alone, the kit stays on this device. Close it, come back, the steps and knobs are where you left them — all sixteen patterns.

Press **Share a pattern**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. A friend who opens it gets **this** pattern: the same steps, knobs, tempo and variation. Either of you can change it; you both hear the same beat. **Keep it here** leaves the room and restores the kit you had before you shared.

Invite is not a button on the 808. It lives in the bar above.

Audio starts on the first Start (or the first tap that needs sound). Turn the ringer up.
