# Piano Trainer

Practice scales, chords, fifths, and a short quiz on a two-octave keyboard. Tap the keys, or use the **home row**. Your last key, mode, and quiz score stay in this copy of the app.

## Keyboard

White keys, left to right: **A S D F G H J K L ; '** (C3 up). Black keys sit on **W E T Y U O P**. A real MIDI keyboard is optional — if this device has one, notes from it count the same as taps. On a phone, hold several keys at once for a chord; drag to move.

**Hear** plays the next notes so you can match them by ear.

## Modes

- **Scales** — play the notes of the chosen scale up, then down. Major, natural minor, melodic minor, and harmonic minor.
- **Chords** — play the triad (1–3–5) on each degree. The Roman numeral (I, ii, V…) is in the prompt.
- **Sevenths** — play the four-note seventh on each degree.
- **Fifths** — walk the circle: each scale degree, then its fifth. Tap a wedge to change key.
- **Quiz** — a fifth above a note, a key signature, the relative minor, the name of a chord, or play a named note. Tap an answer; MIDI is not required.

**Hard** hides the next-note highlight. **Shuffle** starts each round on a random degree.

Pick the tonic from the key list (C, G, D, … and the minors).

## Practice together

Press **Practice together**. A friend who opens the link sees the same mode and the same prompt. You each play on your own keyboard.

## What is saved

Your last key, mode, hard/shuffle flags, quiz score, streak, and finished rounds stay in this copy of the app.
