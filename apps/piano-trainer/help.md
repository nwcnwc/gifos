# Piano Trainer

Practice scales, chords, fifths, and a short quiz on a two-octave keyboard. Tap the keys, or use the **home row**.

## Keyboard

White keys, left to right: **A S D F G H J K L ; '** (C3 up). Black keys sit on **W E T Y U O P**. A real MIDI keyboard is optional — if this device has one, notes from it count the same as taps.

## Modes

- **Scales** — play the notes of the chosen scale up, then down. Major, natural minor, and melodic minor.
- **Chords** — play the triad (1–3–5) on each degree.
- **Sevenths** — play the four-note seventh on each degree.
- **Fifths** — play a note, then its fifth in that key.
- **Quiz** — pick the named key, or the fifth of a named key, from four choices.

**Hard** hides the next-note highlight. **Shuffle** starts each round on a random degree.

Pick the tonic from the key list (C, G, D, … and the minors).

## Practice together

Press **Practice together**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app. A friend who opens the link sees the same mode and the same prompt. You each play on your own keyboard.

## What is saved

Your last key, mode, hard/shuffle flags, and quiz score stay in this copy of the app.
