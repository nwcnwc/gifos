# Tower Defense

Creatures walk from one corner of the map to the other. You place guns in their way. If they reach the far corner, you lose a life.

## Objective

Start with **$500** and **100** life. Waves never stop. Every so often a clear wave gives a little life back. Keep them out as long as you can.

You cannot close the path entirely — they always need a way from the entrance (top-left) to the exit (bottom-right). The game refuses a wall that would trap them.

## Towers

Pick one, then tap a square.

- **Cannon** ($300) — hits hard, middling range
- **LMG** ($100) — longer range, lighter hits
- **HMG** ($800) — heavy hits, short range
- **Laser** ($2000) — never misses, expensive
- **Wall** ($5) — only a wall. They cannot walk through it

Tap a gun you already placed to **Upgrade** (about three-quarters of what you have put into it) or **Sell** (about half back). On a shared map you only upgrade or sell a tower you placed.

## Controls

**Computer** — click a gun on the right-hand panel, then click a square. The same panel has Start, Pause, Upgrade, and Sell. Hover a gun for what it does.

**Phone** — the strip above the map is the same palette, sized for a thumb: pick a gun, then tap a square. **Pause**, **Upgrade**, and **Sell** are there too. **Start** and **Restart** still live on the original panel to the right of the map — tap Start once your first gun is down, or the waves never come.

## Solo vs a friend

Playing alone is the original game.

Press **Share the map**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. Everyone who opens it places towers on the same map. Each person puts down their own. The creatures keep coming. **← Solo** leaves the shared map.

## Scoring

Money from kills. Score climbs as you hold. Life falls when something gets through. Your best wave and score are remembered in the file — they do not show on the board.

## What is saved

Best wave and score stay on this device. The live map is not. Restarting a shared game clears the towers.

An unofficial port of [HTML5 Tower Defense](https://github.com/oldj/html5-tower-defense). This copy adds the shared map.
