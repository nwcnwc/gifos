# JS Paint

Classic MS Paint on this device. Pencil, brush, eraser, fill, airbrush, text, select, and shapes. Nothing is uploaded.

## Draw

Pick a tool on the left, then draw on the canvas.

- **Free-Form Select** and **Select** — grab a region to cut, copy, move, or stretch.
- **Eraser** — wipe to the background colour. Right-drag erases only the foreground colour.
- **Fill** — flood a connected region.
- **Pick Color** — click the canvas to set the current colour.
- **Magnifier** — click to zoom in, right-click to zoom out.
- **Pencil**, **Brush**, **Airbrush** — freehand. Brush and airbrush have size/shape options under the toolbox.
- **Text** — click, type, pick a font. Finish the box before you draw again.
- **Line**, **Curve**, **Rectangle**, **Polygon**, **Ellipse**, **Rounded Rectangle** — click and drag. Hold **Shift** for a straight line, square, or circle.

Left-click paints with the **foreground** colour. Right-click paints with the **background**. The two swatches sit in the colour box; click a palette chip to change the foreground, right-click to change the background. **Edit Colors** adds a custom chip.

## File

- **New** — a blank picture. Unsaved work is lost.
- **Open** — a PNG, GIF, JPEG, BMP, or similar file on this device.
- **Save** / **Save As** — PNG, GIF, JPEG, or BMP onto this device. That is how you take a copy with you.
- **Undo** (`Ctrl+Z`) and **Repeat** (`Ctrl+Y`) — every branch of history, until you close the app.
- **View → Zoom** — normal, large, extra large, or a custom amount. **Show Grid** at 4× and up. **View Bitmap** fills the window with the picture.

## Keyboard

- `Ctrl+N` new, `Ctrl+O` open, `Ctrl+S` save, `Ctrl+Shift+S` save as
- `Ctrl+Z` undo, `Ctrl+Y` or `Ctrl+Shift+Z` redo
- `Ctrl+A` select all, `Delete` clear the selection
- `Ctrl++` / `Ctrl+-` zoom; `Ctrl+0` normal size
- Arrow keys nudge a selection. Hold **Shift** while drawing to constrain.

## Phone

Your finger is the mouse: tap a tool, then drag on the canvas. There is no separate pan gesture — zoom with the **Magnifier** or **View → Zoom**, then scroll the canvas. The toolbox and colour box are the same as on a computer; they are small, so zoom the UI from **View → Enlarge UI** if the hits are tight. There is no right-click: the background colour is the second swatch; tap it to swap.

## What is saved

The current picture auto-saves inside this app. Close it, open it later, the canvas is where you left it. Theme (Classic Light or Classic Dark) is remembered. Undo history is **not** kept across launches — same as MS Paint.

## Private, not shared

This is a local editor. There is no live shared canvas. **Invite** in the bar above the app shares a copy of the app, not a drawing session. Your picture stays on this device unless you use **Save As** or hand someone the app file.

## Not in this copy

No Load from URL, no Upload to Imgur, no multi-user session, no speech, no eye tracking. Themes here are Classic Light and Classic Dark. **Help → Help Topics** is the old Paint help viewer, which this copy does not ship — use **Help** in the bar above instead.
