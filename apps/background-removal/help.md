# Background Removal

Cut a person or a product out of a photo, on this device. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account and no watermark.

## Start

- **Take photo** — a still, not a live camera.
- **Choose a picture** — JPEG, PNG, WebP, anything this browser can show.
- Drop a file onto the stage.
- **Try a sample** if you just want to see the cut.

The first cut for a model downloads that model (about 44, 88 or 176 MB) once. After that it lives on this device and works without a connection.

## Backgrounds

The cut is the subject on a transparent PNG. You can also paint a new background without cutting again:

- Transparent (the checkerboard)
- White, black, studio grey, green, blue
- A custom colour
- Another picture

**Edge softness** feathers the outline. **Soft shadow** sits the subject on a colour or a picture. **Keep the background, drop the subject** inverts the mask — useful for a texture you want without the person in it.

Hold the picture to see the original.

## Download

- **Download PNG** keeps transparency.
- **Download JPEG** is smaller; a transparent cut is flattened onto white.

## Models

- **Small** — fastest, sometimes a little jagged.
- **Medium** — the usual choice.
- **Large** — full precision, slowest to fetch and to run.

A device with a graphics chip is faster. The line under the title says which this one is using.

## What is saved

The last picture, the last cut, and your settings stay on this device, inside this app. Sharing the file shares those too. There is no Invite — this is a private tool.

Unofficial port of [@imgly/background-removal](https://github.com/imgly/background-removal-js) by IMG.LY.
