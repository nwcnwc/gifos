# Guitar Bro

Notes fall down **one string** of a guitar neck. When a note arrives at the nut, play that pitch — on a real guitar, or by tapping the fret.

## Menu

Pick a **song**, a **string**, and a **BPM**. Slow the beat while you learn; speed it up when you are ready.

Strings, thickest to thinnest in reverse:

- **1** thin E · **2** B · **3** G · **4** D · **5** A · **6** thick E · **7** a low metal B

Modes:

- **Survival** — five hearts. A miss costs one; a hit can give one back. Hit zero and the song ends.
- **Practice** — keeps going until you pause.

**Start** begins. **Pause** (or **Space**) opens the menu again.

## Play

The fret row is the strip along the **bottom** of the neck.

- **Tap or click** a fret as the matching note arrives. The right fret is the **column the note is falling in**.
- **Hear it** plays the target note as a tone so you know what to match.
- **Listen** records a short clip on this device and names the note. Play the guitar, then stop. The song **pauses while it listens**, then scores if the pitch matches.

A hit scores **10**. A miss costs **10** and a heart. There is no keyboard chart — Space is pause, not a note.

## Phone

The neck is the whole screen. Use a thumb on the fret row. Listen still works if this device may use the microphone; tap still works with the mic off.

## A live friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the **bar above the app**. You both get the same song, string, speed, and chart. You play at the same time on your own necks. Highest score when the song ends wins.

**Play again** starts the next round on that chart. **← Solo** leaves the race. Pause is off during a race. There is no game server.

## What is saved

Your last song, string, BPM, and mode stay in the file. Scores do not.
