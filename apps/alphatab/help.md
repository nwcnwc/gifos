# alphaTab

Open a guitar tab and play it. The last song comes back the next time you open the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Open a song

A public-domain **Greensleeves** is already here the first time. Press **Open** to pick a Guitar Pro file (`.gp`, `.gp5`, `.gpx`), MusicXML, Capella, or an alphaTex `.tex` file. Drag a file onto the page. Files of about 8 MB or less stay in the app; larger ones open for this session only.

## Play

Press **Play** (or the space bar). A cursor walks the notation and the tab, and you hear the notes. **Stop** goes back to the start. **Speed** slows or speeds the song without changing pitch. **Click** turns a metronome on.

**Page** stacks the music down the screen. **Scroll** lays it out in a horizontal strip, the way a phone tab site does.

**Both** shows standard notation above the tab. **Tab** hides the stave. **Score** hides the tab.

On a **phone**, Play sits under your thumb. Flick the page to scroll; the cursor keeps the current bar in view while it plays.

## Tracks

A song with more than one instrument lists them. Tap a name to show that part.

## A live friend

Someone who joins the room sees the same song and the same beat. When you press Play, their cursor moves with yours. A guest who opens a different file sees it only on their own device.

The host can switch the room between *everyone drives the playhead* and *only the host drives* using the control in the bar above.

## What is saved

The last song (when it fits), where you were, the speed, and the layout. Sharing the app file shares that saved song too.
