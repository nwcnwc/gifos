# Aim and Shoot

You are the black figure. The others are robots run by neural nets. Kill them all before they kill you. Each time you clear a wave they breed — the next generation is better.

## Controls

**Computer**

- Move: `W` `A` `S` `D` or the arrow keys
- Aim: mouse
- Fire: click and hold
- Start: click the title art

**Phone**

The first finger on the screen shows two sticks and two fire buttons. Left stick moves. Right stick aims. The small circles fire. Tap the title to start.

## Rules

A red bar is health. Empty is death. A green bar is the gun's cool-down — empty means you cannot fire yet. **The walls hurt.** Bounce a robot into a wall if you can; do not walk into one yourself.

The number in the corner is the **generation**. Higher means the bots have had more time to learn.

## A friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Each of you plays your own arena. The roster shows the generation you have reached. Nobody aims for you.

## What is saved

Your **best generation** stays in the file. A round in progress is not kept.
