WORD MASTER

How to play

  • You have 6 guesses to find the word.
  • Each guess can be any valid word.
  • After a guess, letters turn green, yellow, or gray.

W Correct letter, correct spot

W Correct letter, wrong spot

Unofficial port of Word Master by octokatherine. Play as many rounds as you like. To play a friend, use Invite in the bar above the app.
