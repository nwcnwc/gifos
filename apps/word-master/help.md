# Word Master

Six guesses at a five-letter word. Finish one, play another — there is no once-a-day limit.

## How to play

Type a five-letter word and press **Enter**. Letters change colour:

- **Green** — right letter, right place
- **Yellow** — right letter, somewhere else in the word
- **Gray** — not in the word

On a computer, type on the keyboard; Backspace deletes. On a phone, use the on-screen keys. **ENTER** submits; the wide key on the right deletes.

A guess must be a real word (in **Normal**). **Easy** accepts any five letters. **Hard** also demands you use every green and yellow hint you already have. Change that under the gear.

## Scoring

There is no points table. On your own you keep a **streak** of wins in a row, plus the longest streak and the average guesses in the current streak.

## Live friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Two of you get the **same secret word**. You never see each other’s letters — only how many guesses the other person has used, and a tick or a miss.

First to find it wins. If you both get there, fewer guesses wins; a tie on guesses goes to whoever finished first. Versus always asks for a valid word (Easy and Hard wait until you are solo again).

**Play Again** starts the next round once you are both done.

## What is saved

Dark mode, difficulty, streaks, and the solo game in progress live in this file. A live match is the room, not the file.

Unofficial port of [Word Master](https://github.com/octokatherine/word-master) by octokatherine.
