# Backdooms

A tiny first-person shooter in yellow halls. Things walk toward you. Shoot them. Last as long as you can.

## Objective

You start in a buzzing corridor. The walls go on forever. Dark figures close in. A hit takes health. Empty the clip into one and it falls — then more arrive. Your **score** is how many you put down. **Ammo** trickles back if you keep moving.

## Computer

- **WASD** walk (strafe with A/D). Arrow keys turn if the pointer is not locked.
- **Mouse** looks once the pointer is locked (click the window). Esc hands the pointer back; click **Click to look** to resume.
- If the pointer will not lock, drag with the mouse to look.
- **Click** or **Space** fires. You have a short clip; it refills slowly while you move.
- **Enter** or **Space** on the start card to begin.

## Phone

- Left thumb **walks** (the pad in the corner).
- Drag on the right half of the screen to **look**.
- **FIRE** under the right thumb, or a short tap on the look side.

The game fills the screen. Turn the phone sideways if you can.

## Continue

Close it and come back: your **best score** is still in this file. **Play** starts a new run. The halls are generated from a seed — a new run is a new maze unless a friend is already walking one.

## Solo vs friends

On your own this is the original corridor.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Extra people appear in the same halls as pale figures. You walk the same maze. You can see them, and a shot in the chest counts. This is not a tournament and there is no game server. The room is the link.

## What is saved

Best score and look speed stay in this file. A live fight, health, and ammo do not.
