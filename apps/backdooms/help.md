# Backdooms

A first-person shooter in yellow office halls that go on forever. Things walk toward you. Shoot them. Last as long as you can.

## Objective

You wake on damp carpet under a line of fluorescents. The corridors run out of sight in every direction. Things the colour of dried blood come down them at you — you will usually hear one before the lights find it. Your **score** is how many you put down.

Nothing can touch you for the first couple of seconds. After that, a hit takes health, and when the bar is empty the run is over. **Shells** come back on their own, and faster while you are moving, so standing still to fight is the fastest way to run dry.

## The shotgun

It is a pump gun, not a rifle. One trigger pull is seven pellets: one straight down the crosshair and six spread around it. Up close the whole load lands and that is usually enough; at ten metres most of it is elsewhere. A solid hit staggers what it hits, which is how you buy room when more than one has found you. Pellets stop at walls — you cannot shoot through the mass.

## Computer

- **WASD** walks, A and D strafe. Arrow keys turn if the pointer is not locked.
- **Mouse** looks once the pointer is locked; click the window to lock it. Esc hands it back and pauses; click **Click to look** to carry on.
- If the pointer will not lock at all, hold the button and drag to look.
- **Click** or **Space** fires.
- **Enter** or **Space** on the start card begins.

## Phone

- The **left side of the screen is the stick**. Put your thumb down anywhere on that side and the stick appears under it — you never have to find a pad.
- Drag anywhere else to **look**. A short tap there fires.
- **FIRE** is under the right thumb, and it keeps firing while you hold it.
- Turn the phone sideways. The halls are wide, and portrait shows you a lot of ceiling.

When something bites you, the screen flares red **on the side it came from**. Turn that way.

## Continue

Close it and come back: your **best score** is still in this file, along with your look speed. **Play** starts a fresh run. The halls are generated from a seed, so a new run is a new maze — unless a friend is already walking one.

## Solo vs friends

On your own this is the corridor and whatever is in it.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Whoever opens that link wakes in the same maze, from the same seed, and appears down the hall as a **pale figure** — cold and lit, so you can always tell one from a thing. You can watch them walk. You can shoot them, and they can shoot you.

## What is saved

Best score and look speed live in this file. A run in progress — health, shells, where you are standing — does not.
