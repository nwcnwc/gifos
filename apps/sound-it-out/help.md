# Sound It Out

Calm videos that build words from their sounds, in a voice the child already knows. Made for a boy with Down syndrome learning to read, and for every family like his.

There are no cartoons, no points, no streaks. Colour on screen means exactly one thing: **whatever is being said right now**.

## What a lesson is

A lesson is a **list** plus a **video**.

On **Sentences**, add a name, a word, or a whole line. Tick what belongs in this video. Press **Play on this screen**, or **Save as a video file** for a USB stick.

The video does the teaching:

1. Each word is built from its sounds, the letters lighting as they play.
2. The gaps shrink until the sounds become the word.
3. A sentence grows word by word, then ends with your own read of the line — the highlight following your voice.

A single letter plays its sound and stops. A single word never does a line-read. Words the letter rules would say wrong (`the`, `said`, `one`) are shown and spoken **whole**. Magic-e words build the way phonics taught them: `c` + `ase`.

## What the child does

They watch. They hear the same line again. They are not asked to tap, spell, or win anything. Loop the video on a TV or tablet.

## What you record

**Record** on a list entry walks you through its words, then the whole line. Say it the way you would to your child — not chopped into letters unless the prompt asks for a sound.

For a consonant, do not add an “uh” on the end (`t`, not `tuh`). The app checks as you speak and will ask you to try that one again.

Words go into a **shared bank**, so the next sentence that uses “the” or a name is already cheaper.

Until you record, a **real human starter voice** covers the letter sounds and the shipped packs. Your take always wins. There is no computer voice: a word whose sounds and whole-word clip are different voices is left out of the video rather than faked.

## Sound Bank and Settings

**Sound Bank** is the 42 letter sounds, optional letter teams, the word bank to listen back, and a **sight words** list (names, “Chase”, anything you never want broken into sounds).

**Settings** is almost empty on purpose. **Save a backup copy of this app** writes one GIF with your list and recordings inside it. Drop that GIF on any Home Screen to get them back.

Starter packs (Paw Patrol lines, VeggieTales, faith lines, around home, letter sounds, sounding-out practice, letter teams, first sentences) are just entries on the list. Remove any you like.

**Sounding out** (how many times through the letters) and **How it looks** only change the video you make next. They stay on this device.

## Invite a friend

**Invite** is in the bar **above** the app. Family who open the link see the list live, hear the recordings, and can play the video at their house. They can **add a sentence** for the child.

**Recording stays with you.** The microphone follows the owner, never the link. A guest who taps Record is told so. A small badge shows who is here.

## What is saved

The list, ticks, recordings, sight words, and look-and-pacing choices stay **inside this app’s icon** on this device — not in the copy anyone else downloads from the store. A backup GIF is the copy worth keeping. A saved video file is WebM; making it plays the video once in real time, so a six-minute video takes six minutes. Leave the app open.
