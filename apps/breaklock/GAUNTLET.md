## Bars

- **ONE** — https://maxwellito.github.io/breaklock/ (the original). Same 3×3, same pegs, same three modes, same snark. "As good as" is losing.
- **TWO** — GifOS: offline, stats inside the GIF, invite = I set a pattern / you crack it. No account, no install, no server.

## Rounds

1. **Vendor** the pattern model, SVG lock, greydient, English quotes. Drop webpack, SCSS, webfont, i18n, service worker, social share. Classic scripts only.
2. **Touch** — one pointer stream (the original split mouse/touch and dropped `mouseleave` on end). Magnet widened from 6 to 12 SVG units so a finger actually hits a dot.
3. **ICON** — 16 frames of a 4-dot reverse-C being drawn, then Mastermind pegs. Transparent sticker, dark outline, reads at 64px.
4. **STORE ART** — 1200×720 mid-game: four tries in the history with pegs, the live lock mid-swipe.
5. **LISTING** — leads with the invite (you set, they crack) and the file-as-save. Author is maxwellito.
6. **Stats** — wins/losses, streak, best streak, fewest tries per difficulty, plus a practice/challenge in progress. Countdown is not kept.
7. **Invite** — setter draws a secret the UI never shows; cracker publishes attempts; setter watches the history. YOUR TURN_ passes the next secret.

## Remaining gap

The original's airport-display title and the OLED "deep black" easter egg are lighter here (airport is on, deep-black is not — there is no localStorage). Multiplayer is 1 setter + N crackers, not a simultaneous race on a *random* secret; that is the point of bar TWO.

## Win

The original needs a tab and forgets you; this one is a GIF you hand someone with a lock already drawn in it, or an invite that makes them crack yours.
