# Stolen Sword

Someone took the sword and ran into the bamboo. You get one finger: **press, pull back, and let go** — you fly, and the blade goes with you. Grey marks wait in the air. Some throw fire. Some hide behind a shell.

The hint at the bottom is the original: `press · pull · let go`. It hides after the first press.

## How to fly

There is only one move, on a computer and on a phone.

1. Press and hold (mouse button, or a finger on the picture).
2. Pull **away** from where you want to go. A faint line shows the path.
3. Let go. You dash along that line.

Pull farther for a faster dash. A tiny pull does nothing. You have **two dashes**; landing on ground, a platform, or a wall gives them back. Hitting a mark in the air with the blade cuts it down.

On a phone it is the same press you use on a computer. There is nothing else to learn. The page does not scroll under your thumb.

## Survive

You can take **two hits**. Fire, a shell you bounce off the wrong way, or a mark you run into all count. After a hit you flash and get a moment of safety. A third hit and that land starts over.

The first time through the grove, a ghostly drag-track shows the move. After that, the game remembers you have seen it.

## Four lands

Grove, bamboo, mountain, river — pale to dark. Clear the marks in a land and the thief is still ahead; the next land loads. At the end of the river the chase loops. There is no score. Getting farther **is** the score.

## Solo vs friends

Solo is the original campaign. Progress through the lands is yours.

**Invite** in the bar above the app puts extra swordsmen in the same grove, flying their own lines. They are **ghosts**: tinted, see-through, only drawn if they are on the same land. Everyone fights their own grove. You cannot cut a friend, and they cannot cut your marks. Tap the sword-count chip for who is where.

## What is saved

The land you reached and whether you have seen the tutorial stay in this app. A live room is not. Next open, you continue the chase from that land.
