This app serves your OS

Offline Text to Speech is a Provider app: instead of doing a job on its own screen, it gives GifOS an ability — the Text → speech AI type. Every app that asks the computer to speak (like Reader in the Tools folder) will use it.

  1. Keep its icon in the Providers folder on your Home Screen — a provider only works from there (anywhere else it wears a red ✕).
  2. Open Settings → AI models and set Text → speech to 📦 Offline Text to Speech.
  3. That's it. Apps that speak now speak with this voice, offline.
