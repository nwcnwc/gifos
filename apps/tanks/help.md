# Tanks

A top-down tank in a walled yard. Drive, aim, shoot. Three hits and you come back.

## Objective

Last tank standing is not a mode — there is no round clock. Kills and deaths sit on the tally. A hit takes one heart. At zero you explode, then respawn in a couple of seconds with three again.

On your own, two drone tanks patrol and shoot back. Send the invite and the drones leave: the other people are the fight.

## Controls

**Computer**

- **W A S D** or the arrows to drive
- Mouse to aim the turret
- Click or **Space** to shoot
- **Tab** shows the tally

**Phone**

- Left thumb drives (the stick)
- Right thumb aims; holding that side fires
- **FIRE** also shoots
- Tap the tally to open scores

Shells bounce off nothing — walls stop them. You cannot drive through walls or other tanks.

## Play with friends

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. Everyone who opens it lands in the same yard, each a different colour. There is no game server and no lobby code. The room is the people who still have the link open.

You write only your own tank. Hits you land are claimed on your row; the other tank applies the wound to itself. This is a game with friends.

## What is saved

Nothing lasting. Close it and you start again, three hearts, empty tally.

An unofficial port of [Realtime Multiplayer in HTML5](https://github.com/ruby0x1/realtime-multiplayer-in-html5) by Sven Bergström. The original needs a Node process and socket.io. This copy does not: the meeting is the arena.
