# Tanks

A top-down tank in a walled yard. Drive, aim, shoot. Three hits and you come back.

## Objective

There is no round clock. Kills and deaths sit on the tally. A hit takes one heart. At zero you explode, then respawn in a couple of seconds with three again and a short shield so you are not shot the instant you appear.

On your own, two drone tanks patrol and shoot back. With a friend in the room the drones leave: the other people are the fight.

## Controls

**Computer**

- **W A S D** or the arrows to drive
- Mouse to aim the turret
- Click or **Space** to shoot
- **Tab** (or tap the tally) shows scores

**Phone**

- Left thumb drives (MOVE)
- Right thumb aims (AIM); holding that stick also fires
- **FIRE** also shoots

Shells stop on walls. You cannot drive through walls or other tanks.

## A friend

Everyone who opens the same room lands in the yard, each a different colour. You write only your own tank. Hits you land are claimed on your row; the other tank applies the wound to itself. This is a game with friends.

## What is saved

Career kills and deaths on this copy of the app. A fight in progress is not kept — close it and you start again with three hearts, but the career line on the tally is still there.
