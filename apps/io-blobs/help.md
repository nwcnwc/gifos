# IO Blobs

You are a small coloured blob in a big open arena. Swallow the smaller ones. Do not get swallowed.

## How to grow

Dots of food sit all over the field. Run over them. You have to be **clearly bigger** than another blob to eat it — a near-tie bounces you off. Spiked balls knock a chunk off you if you have grown large enough to cover one; small, they just shove you aside.

You spawn again a moment after you are eaten. Size resets. The name on the scoreboard is you.

## Controls

**Computer**

- Point the **mouse** — you drift toward the cursor
- or **WASD** / arrow keys
- **Space** or **Shift** — Boost (a burst of speed that **costs size**; it does nothing until you have eaten enough)
- **Tab** — show or hide the Largest list

**Phone**

- Put a thumb down and **drag** — a stick appears under that thumb
- **Boost** is the same burst as Space

There is no pinch-zoom. The view follows you and pulls back a little as you get huge.

## Solo or a friend

On your own, a few wandering blobs keep you company. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link: everyone who opens it drops into the **same** arena. The wanderers scatter the moment another person arrives — the others you see are people.

Invite is not a button inside this game.

This is not a ranked ladder. A room of friends over a link. Remote blobs are drawn a fraction of a second behind so they glide.

## What is saved

Your **best size** on this device. The current blob is not: close the app and you spawn small again. A live room is the link, not a file.
