# Webamp

Winamp 2.9: a main window, a graphic equalizer, and a playlist. It opens already playing a short original demo. Drop MP3s on it. The playlist and the EQ stay in this file.

## Play something

- First open greets with **GifOS — Intro**, **Green LED**, and **On a Plane**. Those three are synthesized in this file (not a downloaded MP3, not the llama).
- **Drop** MP3s (or WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A) anywhere on the player, or tap **+ MP3s**.
- Press **Play** on the main window, or **X** on a keyboard.
- Double-click a row in the playlist to jump to it.

Nothing is fetched. If you want a skin that is not the one it opens with, drop a `.wsz` file, or tap **Skin**.

## The three windows

- **Main** — time, vis, volume, seek, and the transport buttons (previous, play, pause, stop, next).
- **Equalizer** — ten bands plus preamp. On/Off, Auto. Double-click a slider to reset it.
- **Playlist** — the list, add/remove, shade it to a thin bar. Resize from the bottom-right.

Drag a window by its title bar. The shade button (top right, next to close) collapses it. Close on the main window hides the player; tap **Show player** to bring it back.

## Keyboard

**Z** previous, **X** play, **C** pause, **V** stop, **B** next. **L** opens files. **Alt+W** / **Alt+E** / **Alt+P** toggle the three windows. **Ctrl+D** double-size. **R** repeat, **S** shuffle.

## With a friend

A live friend sees the same playlist titles and the same EQ. Twist a band and they see it. Each person plays files that are on **their** copy; handing someone this GIF is how the songs themselves travel.

## What is saved

Your library, the playlist order, the EQ, volume, shuffle/repeat, window layout, and any skin you dropped. Close it, open it later, you are still there.
