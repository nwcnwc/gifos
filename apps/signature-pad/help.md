# Signature Pad

A cream sheet you sign with a finger or a mouse. The line thickens and thins the way a pen does. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account.

## Sign

Draw on the paper. One stroke is one line of ink.

- **Finger or stylus** — press and drag on the pad. The page does not scroll while you sign.
- **Mouse** — click and drag.
- **Black** or **blue** ink — the two dots on the toolbar.

**Undo** takes back the last stroke. **Clear** wipes the whole pad. Neither can be undone after you leave.

## Save a picture

**Save PNG** writes `signature.png` onto this device. That file is yours to keep or send. The pad itself is unchanged.

If the pad is empty, it asks you to sign first.

## What stays in the file

The last signature on this computer stays **inside the app**. Close it, open it later — the strokes and the ink colour are still there.

A PNG you saved is a separate picture. It does not update when you sign again.

## Pass the pad

Playing alone is one signature. Press **Pass the pad**, then **Invite** in the bar **above** the app (not inside it). Everyone who opens the link gets **their own line** on the same sheet.

You sign yours. You cannot sign theirs. Each name sits on its own row. Waiting people show as empty lines until they write.

**Save PNG** in a meeting writes the **whole sheet** as `signature-sheet.png` — every line, labelled.

**← Solo** leaves the sheet and puts your own signature back.

## Tips

- Sign slowly. The pen tracks speed, so a hurried scribble looks thinner.
- A long flourish is fine. If a line looks wrong, Undo once and try that stroke again.
- The paper is cream. The PNG keeps that colour.
