# The Evolution of Trust

An interactive essay about why people trust each other, and how that trust can fall apart. You play the Game of Trust — put a coin in, or keep it — first against one stranger, then in a tournament of many personalities, then in a world that evolves.

Playing time is about **30 minutes**. There is no score to beat. The point is to see what happens.

## How to play

- Tap **the next button** on each page. Some pages ask you to **cooperate** or **cheat** first.
- Along the bottom, the **dots** jump to a chapter you have already reached (or any chapter, once the essay has started).
- **ON / OFF** in the corner is sound. The music starts when you press play on the title.
- **Back** on a phone steps one chapter, or closes the footnotes.

On a phone the whole stage shrinks so the words and the games still fit. Drag the sliders in the last chapter with a finger.

## The characters

You will meet **Copycat**, **Always Cheat**, **Always Cooperate**, **Grudger**, **Detective**, and later **Copykitten**, **Simpleton**, and **Random**. Hover or tap a name to remember who they are.

## Watching with a friend

Whoever opens the link watches **your** tournament: the same chapter, the same mix of characters, the same Start / Step / Reset. They do not turn the pages — you do. Sound stays theirs.

## What is saved

The **chapter you were on**, how far you got, and whether sound is off. Close it, come back, you are still there. Sharing the file shares that place. A tournament in motion is not kept; press Start again.

## Credits and footnotes

The last chapter has links to Nicky Case's other work, the book this is based on, and **footnotes**. The footnotes open here, as a page you can scroll. The original is dedicated to the public domain.
