# Excalidraw

A hand-drawn whiteboard for boxes, arrows, handwriting, and diagrams. The rough ink is the point. What you draw is saved on this device, inside the app. There is no account and no cloud.

## The jobs

Sketch a flowchart, a UI, a map, or a scribble. Drop a `.excalidraw` file on the canvas to open it. Export a **PNG**, an **SVG**, or the open `.excalidraw` file when you want a copy in your hands.

## Tools

The left toolbar is the usual Excalidraw set:

- **Selection (V)** — move, resize, restyle.
- **Rectangle (R)**, **Diamond (D)**, **Ellipse (O)**.
- **Arrow (A)** and **Line (L)**.
- **Draw (P)** — freehand.
- **Text (T)** — click to type; double-click a shape to label it.
- **Image** — paste or pick a picture; it stays in the drawing.
- **Eraser (E)**, **Laser**, **Frame**, **Hand (H)**.

Colours, stroke, fill, and font sit in the top bar once something is selected. **Cmd/Ctrl+Z** undoes, **Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z** redoes. **Delete** removes the selection.

## Zoom and pan

- **Scroll** or **pinch** to zoom. **Cmd/Ctrl +** and **−**, or the zoom readout.
- **Hand (H)** or **hold Space and drag** to pan.
- Two fingers pan on a phone.

## Menu and boards

The hamburger is **Load**, **Save**, **Export**, **Save as image**, **Boards on this device…**, **Clear canvas**, **Toggle theme**, **Canvas background**.

The **pill** in the top-left is the current board’s name. Tap it for the list: rename, open another, **New board**, or delete (you must keep at least one). Several boards can live in the same app.

## Invite

**Invite** is in the bar **above** the app. It does **not** share this drawing. A guest who opens it gets their **own** empty board. There is no live room, no share-link, and no sticker library from the internet. To show someone your work, **export**.

Live collaboration, Magic / AI frames, web embeds, and the public library browser are not in this copy.

## What is saved

Every board, its viewport and theme, images you paste, and items you add to **your** library auto-save **inside this app** on this device. Close it, open it later: the canvas is where you left it.

A few photos are fine. A board full of photographs makes the app heavy.

Export still writes PNG / SVG / `.excalidraw`. Hand-drawn Latin faces (Virgil and friends) are in the app. Chinese, Japanese, and Korean fall back to the **system font**.
