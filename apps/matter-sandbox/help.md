# Matter Sandbox

A 2D physics toy box. Stacks fall, balls bounce, ragdolls flop, and a slingshot knocks the pile over. There is no score and no win — you build a mess, then do it again.

## Hands

- **Grab** (default): drag any piece and throw it. On a phone, one finger is enough.
- **Box / Ball / Ragdoll**: tap the room to drop one. Ragdolls are a little person with floppy joints.
- **Sling**: pull the rock back and let go. A new rock loads after each shot.
- **Stack**: drop another pyramid of boxes.
- **Pause** freezes the pile so you can place things. **Reset** puts the slingshot and pyramids back.

**Gravity** is the slider under the room. `1.0` is ordinary. `0` is space. Below zero, everything falls at the ceiling.

Keyboard: `1–5` pick tools, `R` resets, `Space` pauses.

## Solo vs a live friend

Alone, this is the original toy box. The pile lives **in this file**. Close it, open it later, the boxes are where you left them.

Press **Share the room**, then press **Invite** in the bar **above** the app and send the link. Invite is not inside this page.

A friend who opens the link lands in the **same pile**. They drop, grab, and sling too. The person who opened the app first runs the physics; everyone else’s hands still work.

## What is saved

The scene — every box, ball, ragdoll, the slingshot, and the gravity you picked. Sharing the file shares the pile.
