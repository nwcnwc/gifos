Unofficial port of Nonogram by HandsomeOne. Paint from the numbers. A finger can drag a line.

How to play

  • The numbers say how the filled squares run in each row and column, in order, with gaps between runs.
  • Tap Fill, then paint. Tap Cross to mark a square empty. Drag to paint a whole line.
  • A green number means that line is right. Right-click (or Cross) to mark empties.
  • When every line is right, the picture is finished.

To play a friend, use Invite in the bar above the app. Same puzzle — first to finish wins.
