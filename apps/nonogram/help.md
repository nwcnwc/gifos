# Nonogram

Picross. Numbers along the edge tell you how the filled squares run. Paint until the picture appears.

## The numbers

Each row and each column has a list of numbers. Those are the **runs** of filled squares, in order, with at least one empty square between runs.

A `5` on a five-wide row means the whole row is filled. `1 1` means two filled squares with a gap between them — you still have to work out where. A green number means that line already matches.

When every line is green, you have finished.

## Fill and Cross

Squares start blank.

- **Fill** paints a square (part of the picture).
- **Cross** marks a square empty, so you remember not to paint it.

Tap the buttons under the board to pick the brush, then tap a square. Drag to paint a whole row or column in a straight line. Tap a painted square with the same brush to clear it back to blank.

**Mouse:** left-drag uses the current brush. Right-click (or right-drag) uses the other one. The small control on the board also flips Fill and Cross.

**Phone:** there is no right-click. Use **Fill** and **Cross**, then drag with a finger. The board does not scroll while you paint.

There are no keyboard keys for the grid.

## Size, timer, undo

Pick **5 × 5** up to **15 × 15**. Early puzzles are pictures; later ones are generated. **New game** deals another.

The timer counts up. Tap it to pause; tap ▶ to continue. Switching away also pauses.

**Undo** steps back. **Hint** fills or crosses one square that belongs.

## Alone or a race

On your own, the puzzle in progress is saved in this file. Close it and pick up where you left off. Last size is remembered.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app to race. The link is the room. You both get the **same** puzzle. Live times and fill bars sit at the top; first to finish wins. Size is locked while you race. **Play again** deals a new shared puzzle.

Invite is in that bar, not inside this game.

An unofficial port of [Nonogram](https://github.com/HandsomeOne/Nonogram).
