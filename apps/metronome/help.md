# Metronome

A **click that keeps time**. Set a tempo, press Start. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no microphone.

## Play

1. Set **Tempo** with the slider, the **− / +** buttons, or **Tap tempo** in time a few times.
2. Pick a **signature**: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, or 6/8. In 6/8 the click is the eighth note.
3. Optionally pick a **subdivision** (beat, 8ths, triplets, 16ths).
4. Press **Start**. The first beat of the bar is a higher click. The pendulum swings with the beat.

**Sound** is Click (a wood tick) or Beep (the original oscillator). **Volume** is this device’s click, not a shared speaker.

Tempo marks (Largo, Adagio, Andante, Moderato, Allegro, Presto) jump to a printed speed.

On a keyboard: **Space** starts and stops. Arrow keys nudge the tempo (Shift for ±5).

## Phone

− / + and Start are thumb-sized. Hold − or + to roll the tempo. Tap tempo is the second button.

## What is saved

Tempo, signature, subdivision, sound, and volume stay in this file on this device. They come back the next time you open it. Playback does not start on its own.

Guests in a meeting see the host’s tempo and signature, read-only. Each person hears the click on **their** device — there is no shared speaker. Volume stays local.

Unofficial port of [cwilso/metronome](https://github.com/cwilso/metronome) by Chris Wilson.
