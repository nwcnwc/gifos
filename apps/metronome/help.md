# Metronome

A **click that keeps time**. Set a tempo and a time signature. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no microphone.

## Play

1. Drag **Tempo** (30–240 BPM), or tap **Tap tempo** in time a few times.
2. Pick a **signature**: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, or 6/8.
3. Press **Start**. The first beat of the bar is a higher click.

**Volume** is this device’s click, not a shared speaker. Stop before you leave.

The lights under the dial walk the beats of the bar.

## What is saved

Tempo, signature, and volume stay in this file on this device. They come back the next time you open it. Playback does not start on its own.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app to show the same numbers in a meeting. Each person hears the click on **their** device — there is no shared speaker. Guests see the host’s tempo and signature, read-only.

Unofficial port of [cwilso/metronome](https://github.com/cwilso/metronome) by Chris Wilson.
