# InvaderZ

Keep the swarm out of the atmosphere. They change shape as you play: each body is how it moves, and a miss teaches the next wave.

## Objective

You have one cannon on the ground. Shoot the invaders before they drop through the line. **Five through and the sky is lost.** You get **one shot at a time** — wait for it to hit or leave the top before you fire again.

There are six bodies in a wave. When the last one dies, a new **generation** is born from the ones that got furthest. Every seventh generation the elite is cloned instead of mixed. The number at the top is how far the swarm has evolved, not a score.

## Controls

**Computer**

- Left / right: arrow keys, or `A` / `D`
- Fire: space
- Restart after Game Over: space or Enter
- With a friend: Tab shows or hides the kill table

**Phone**

Buttons sit **under** the board, never on the cannon.

- **LEFT** / **RIGHT** — hold to move
- **FIRE** — tap to shoot; after Game Over, tap FIRE to start a new wave

There is no up or down. The cannon stays on the ground.

## Reading the board

- **Gen** — the current generation
- **Through n/5** — how many invaders have already crossed the line, not lives you have left
- **Kills** — bodies you personally shot this run
- **Best** — your furthest generation on this device
- **Elite** — a tiny picture of the best shape from the last wave

## Solo or a friend

On your own you are the only cannon. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link: extra cannons appear on the same ground, each person aiming their own, everyone shooting the same wave. There is no lobby. The host of the room runs the swarm; if they leave, the wave waits.

Invite is not a button inside this game.

## What is saved

Your **best generation** stays on this device, inside the app. The current wave is not saved — close it and you start generation 1 again. A live room is the link, not a file.
