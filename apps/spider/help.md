# Spider

**Spider patience.** Ten piles. Build descending runs **king down to ace**. A finished king-to-ace run of 13 *in the same suit* leaves the tableau. Clear **eight** runs to win.

Score starts at **500**. Each move and each deal costs a point. Each finished run is **+100**. Undo puts the cards and the score back.

## Suits

- **1 suit** — every card is a spade. Any descending run can move. The easy game.
- **2 suits** — spades and hearts. You may drop a card on the next-higher rank of *either* suit, but you can only pick up a run that is all one suit.
- **4 suits** — the full game. Same rules, four suits.

The deal is honest: first four piles get six cards, the other six get five. Fifty cards remain in the stock, in five rows of ten.

## Controls

- **Tap** a face-up card to pick up the descending same-suit run from there. Tap a pile that will take it (empty, or whose top card is one rank higher). Tap the same card again to send it to the best pile.
- **Drag** the run onto a pile. Works with a finger or a mouse.
- **Tap the stock** (top right) to deal one card onto every pile. Every pile must already have a card, and the stock must not be empty.
- **Undo** takes back the last move or deal. **Hint** lights a useful move. **New** starts a fresh deal.
- Keyboard: `U` undo, `H` hint, `D` or space deal, `N` new, `1`/`2`/`4` change suits, `Esc` drop the selection.

## What is saved

The tableau, the stock, the score, the suit count, and your win/loss record come back with this copy of the app.
