# Spider

**One-suit Spider.** Ten piles. Build descending runs from king down to ace. A finished king-to-ace run of 13 leaves the tableau (a foundation). Clear **eight** runs to win.

Score starts at **500**. Each move and each deal costs a point. Each finished run is **+100**.

## Controls

- **Tap** a face-up card to select the descending run from that card down. Tap a destination pile to drop it. A pile takes a run if it is empty, or if its top card is one rank higher than the moving card.
- **Tap the stock** (bottom-right) to deal one card onto every pile. Every pile must have at least one card, and the stock must not be empty.
- **Undo** takes back the last move or deal.
- **Hint** lights a useful move. **New** starts a fresh deal.

## What is saved

The tableau, the stock, the score, and your win/loss counts come back with this copy of the app.
