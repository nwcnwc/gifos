# fend

A notepad calculator that keeps units. Type a line, press **Enter**. The pad stays in this file. Nothing is uploaded.

## Try these

- `1 ft to cm`
- `5 kg in lb`
- `100 C to F`
- `sqrt(2)`
- `0xFF`
- `roll 4d6` (a roll; `4d6` on its own is the distribution)
- `sin(pi / 2)`
- `1/3 + 1/6`

Mix units in one line. Variables work: `a = 3` then `a * 2`. `_` is the last answer.

A line that does not calculate says why, in a sentence. Currency rates are not in this file, so those lines will say they are not available.

## The pad

The history is the notepad: each line you typed, then the answer under it. Tap a line to put it back in the prompt. **Up / Down** walks previous lines. **Clear pad** starts a new page (the old one is gone).

On a phone a keypad sits under the prompt — digits, operators, and chips for **ft**, **cm**, **to**, and the rest — so you can convert without a keyboard. **abc** opens the device keyboard for names and variables.

## What is saved

The whole pad (every line and answer) lives in this file on this device. It comes back the next time you open it.

Unofficial port of [fend](https://github.com/printfn/fend) by printfn.
