# Super Sudoku

Fill every square so each **row**, **column**, and **3×3 box** contains the digits 1–9 once.

## How to play

1. Select a square.
2. Enter a digit.
3. Printed (given) digits cannot change.
4. You finish when the grid is full and no digit repeats in a row, column, or box.

Tap **New game** for another puzzle at the current difficulty (Easy through Evil). The puzzle number is in the header.

## Controls

**Phone / touch**

- Tap a square, then tap 1–9 on the pad.
- **Notes** turns pencil marks on. Tap a digit to add or remove it; tap **Notes** again to fill for real.
- **Erase** clears the selected square. **Undo** steps back. **Hint** fills the selected square with the correct digit.
- Tap the timer to pause. Tap the play overlay to continue.

**Mouse**

- Click a square, then click a pad digit or type.
- Right-click a square to select it in notes mode.

**Keyboard**

- Arrow keys move. `1`–`9` fill. Backspace, Delete, or `-` erase.
- `N` notes. `H` hint. Escape pauses.
- Ctrl/⌘ Z undo, Y redo.
- Ctrl/⌘ C copies notes from the selected square; V pastes them onto another.

## Settings

**Settings** keeps:

- **Show auto notes** — possible digits on empty squares (until you pencil your own)
- **Highlight wrong entries** — a digit that cannot be the solution
- **Highlight conflicts** — two of the same digit in a row, column, or box
- **Show how many of each number** — counts on the pad
- **Dark mode**

Leaving the tab pauses the timer.

## Scoring

There is no score. The timer is elapsed seconds while you play. **Hint** counts; in a race, friends can see how many you used. Hints do not disqualify you.

## Solo vs a live friend

On your own, this is a normal sudoku. Close the app and pick up the same puzzle.

**Invite** (in the bar above the app) seats a friend in the room. You both get the **same** puzzle. First to finish wins. Live times and fill progress sit in the race strip. Difficulty is locked while you race. **Play again** deals a new shared puzzle.

Invite is OS chrome — this app does not draw a share button.

## What is saved

Your current solo puzzle, notes, time, hint count, and settings stay in this file. A race is not written over that save. When the friend leaves, you return to your own puzzle.

Thousands of puzzles ship inside the app. Nothing is uploaded.
