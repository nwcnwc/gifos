# Pixel It

Turn a **photo** into **pixel art** on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

This is a converter, not a drawing app. You start from a picture. You do not paint pixels by hand.

## Load a picture

- **Drop** a JPEG, PNG or WebP onto the dashed box, or tap the box to pick a file.
- **Take photo** opens the camera for a still. Press it, take the shot, and the conversion runs. There is no live viewfinder inside the app — you get one picture at a time.

A small sample landscape is there when you first open, so you can try the sliders before bringing your own photo.

## Convert

- **Block size** — 1 is almost the original; 50 is huge chunks. The original demo starts at 8.
- **Greyscale** — drop the colour, keep the blocks.
- **Use palette** — snap every block to the chosen palette. Palettes are the ones from Pixel It: retro, dusk, game-boy-ish, candy, and more.
- Pick a palette from the row of colour chips.

The canvas updates as you change those. On a large photo it can take a moment.

## Save

**Download PNG** writes `pxArt.png` to this device. That file is yours.

The last picture and the last settings stay **in this app file**. Close it, open it later, they are still there. They do not leave this device.

## Phone

A thumb works on the sliders and the palette chips. Take photo is the easy path on a phone; drop is easier on a laptop.
