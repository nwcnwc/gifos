# Tower Game

A crane swings a floor out. Drop it on the tower. Stack them. Miss three times and it is over.

## Objective

Land each floor on the one below. Climb. The crane gets meaner the higher you go. A perfect stack — the new floor sits flush — scores more, and a streak of perfects more still. Three misses (the hearts) end the run.

On your own it is the original game. Send the link and it becomes a race: everyone builds their own tower. Live heights as you go. Tallest when the hearts run out wins.

## Controls

**Phone** — tap the screen to drop the floor that is swinging.

**Computer** — click, or press **Space** or **Enter**. The same keys start a run from the title, and try again from game over.

The first floors show a small hand and an arrow. That is the original hint. After that, tap when you mean it.

## Rules

You start with three hearts. A miss costs one and resets the perfect streak. A land that hangs off the edge stays, but the next floor has a smaller target. Go far enough off-screen and the crane starts playing harder.

Sound plays with the drops. There is no mute in the app.

## Scoring

Each landed floor scores. A perfect adds a bonus, and each perfect in a row adds another. The number on game over is that run. **Best** is your highest on this device.

In a race the HUD along the top shows each person's floors. Tallest tower wins. A tie on floors is broken by score. You each see your own tower — not theirs.

## Solo vs a friend

Playing alone never waits on a room. Tap Start and stack.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. Two or more people in the room are racing, even if you already started. First to run out of hearts waits to see who stacks higher. **Try again** is locked until everyone is out; then it is a new round.

## What is saved

Your best score stays in the file. A run in progress does not. The race lives only while the link is open.
