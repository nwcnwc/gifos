# Offline Captions (Whisper)

This app is an **ear for your whole computer**. It turns speech into text **on this device** — no account, no key, nothing sent away.

It is a **Provider**: instead of doing a job only on its own screen, it gives every app the **Speech → text** ability. The **meeting's live captions** are the first to use it: in a meeting, open **Settings → Captions** and choose **Whisper on this device**.

## Setting it up

1. Keep its icon in the **Providers** folder on your Home Screen — a provider only works from there. An App Store install puts it there for you.
2. Open **Settings → AI models** and set **Speech → text** to **📦 Offline Captions (Whisper)**. A meeting's Captions settings can do this step for you.
3. That's it. Apps that listen now listen with Whisper, offline.

## First-run wait

The first time you use it, the model downloads once and stays on this device: **tiny (41 MB)** on a phone, **base (77 MB)** on a computer. While that happens the computer shows a progress pill. After that the app never needs the network.

## What it does that the browser's own captions cannot

- **Any language, found by itself.** Whisper hears 99 languages and picks the one being spoken. You can also fix the language in the meeting's Captions settings.
- **Real sentences.** Punctuation and capital letters, instead of two-word lines.
- **Your own words.** Give it a short list of names and product words (in the meeting's Captions settings) and it spells them your way.
- **Write English.** Turn on *Write everything in English* and every language comes out as English text.

## It thinks, then it writes

It runs on the processor. A sentence appears **a few seconds after it was said** on a computer, and **longer on a phone**, where it also costs battery. That delay is work, not a hang. If you need instant captions in one language on a weak device, the browser's own engine is still there under **Settings → Captions**.

## Try it here

The **Try** box on this app's page transcribes an audio file you choose (a recording, a voice note). Nothing you pick leaves this device.
