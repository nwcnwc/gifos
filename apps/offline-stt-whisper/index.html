🎧 Offline Captions — WhisperPROVIDER

Speech to text for your whole computer, in 99 languages, entirely on this device. No account, no key, nothing sent anywhere.

This app serves your OS

It is a Provider app: instead of doing a job on its own screen, it gives GifOS an ability — the Speech → text AI type. The meeting's live captions use it first; any app that asks the computer to listen gets the same ear.

  1. Keep its icon in the Providers folder on your Home Screen — a provider only works from there.
  2. Open Settings → AI models and set Speech → text to 📦 Offline Captions (Whisper). A meeting's Settings → Captions can do this for you.
  3. In a meeting, choose Whisper on this device under Captions.

What it does that the browser's captions cannot

  • Any language, found by itself. 99 of them; it picks the one being spoken.
  • Real sentences, with punctuation and capitals.
  • Your own words. A short list of names and product words, spelled your way.
  • Write English from any language spoken.

It runs on the processor and thinks before it writes: a sentence appears a few seconds after it was said on a computer, longer on a phone. The model downloads once: tiny, 41 MB on a phone; base, 77 MB on a computer.
