# Periodic Table

All **118** confirmed elements live in this file. Tap a cell, quiz yourself, or race a friend. Nothing is downloaded.

## Table

The grid is the map. Tap an element: a card fills with name, number, mass, family, electrons, state, and year. Use **‹** and **›** to step through the list.

- Filter by family (Alkali, Earth, Noble, La, Ac, …). **All** shows every cell.
- Search by name, symbol, or number (`Gold`, `Au`, `79`). A match opens that card.
- **Bigger** enlarges the cells so you can read the map and pan. **Fit** puts the whole table back on screen. On a phone, Bigger is how you actually read the grid; the card is the reading surface.

118 is **Oganesson** (Og). Ununennium would be 119 and is not here.

## Quiz

Ten questions, four choices, one right. Nearby wrong answers, not random.

Kinds you will see: symbol for a name, number for a symbol, name for a symbol, family, “which of these is a noble gas?”, period.

Misses show as chips at the end. **Play again** starts a new set. Your **best** score stays on this device.

## A friend

Open **A friend**, then press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. You both get the **same** questions and race to **ten right**. First done wins; the next race starts on its own. No account.

Until someone opens the link, you wait. **← Leave** drops you out of the race. Invite shares only the live quiz and scores — not your saved best.

## Private vs Invite

Solo quiz scores stay on this device. Invite shares the race in progress, not the table data (that is already in the app) and not your best score.
