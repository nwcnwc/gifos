# Star Battle

You fly a little yellow ship across a field of stars. Red saucers come in from the right. Rocks take two hits. Fuel leaks away one point a second — catch a falling bottle or the run is over.

Tap **Start**. On a phone held upright, tap **Start** on the gate first; the sky then turns so you fly toward the **top** of the screen.

## Controls

**Computer**

- `W` `A` `S` `D` fly. Space fires (tap, do not hold — each press is one shot).
- `P` pauses. `M` mutes.
- `Tab` shows the extra-ship board when friends are in the sky.
- The `+` / `−` icons change the HUD type size. The speaker and pause icons match `M` and `P`.

**Phone**

- Left thumb is a stick. Right thumb **FIRE** — hold it to keep shooting.
- **PAUSE** sits above fire.
- The first real finger on the glass reveals the pads. A laptop with a touchscreen does not get them until you actually touch.

Mouse does not fly the ship. Use the keys, or a finger.

## Scoring

- Red ship **+5**. Rock **+10** (two hits). A friendly yellow ship **−10** — they look like you, do not shoot them.
- Fuel starts at 15, max 30. It falls by 1 every second. A bottle is **+15**. Getting hit is **−15**.
- Time and shot-count sit next to the score. When fuel hits 0 the run ends. Type a name and **Continue** to land on the local rank list.

Stars in the background are scenery. They do not score.

## Solo vs friends

Solo is the original game: one ship, one wave, a rank list on this device.

**Invite** in the bar above the app puts extra ships in the **same** sky. Everyone shoots the same wave. There is no lobby and no game server.

- The host (the person who sent the invite) runs the wave. Guests wait until that first ship is in the sky.
- Your fuel is your own. A hit you land is yours.
- If you run out of fuel while friends are still up, you sit out — **You're out. Extra ships still flying.**
- Tap the ship-count chip (or `Tab`) for names, scores, and fuel.

Friends' ships look like yours. Do not shoot them.

## What is saved

The rank list and mute stay in this app. A live room is not. Next open, you are on the start screen with your old ranks.
