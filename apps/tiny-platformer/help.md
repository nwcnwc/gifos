# Tiny Platformer

A yellow square in a brick cave. Collect the gold. Jump on the grey blocks. Do not walk into them.

## Objective

Clear the cave. Gold pulses in place — touch it to take it. Grey blocks patrol the platforms; land on one from above to stomp it, or it sends you back to the start.

There is one cave. When you have the gold and the grey ones are gone, you can keep running around — there is no exit door.

## Controls

**Keyboard:** **Left** and **Right** arrows to run. **Space** to jump. You only jump from the ground, not in the air.

**Phone:** **◀** and **▶** sit under your left thumb, **JUMP** under your right. The pad appears on a phone, or after a finger lands.

## Scoring

Each gold brick is **1 coin**. Each stomp is **1**. Hitting a grey block from the side or below resets you to the start — coins and stomps you already took stay taken.

## What is saved

Your **best** coins and stomps on this copy of the app. The cave itself is not saved mid-run. Close it, come back: the best line is still in the corner.
