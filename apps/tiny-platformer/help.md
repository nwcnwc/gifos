# Tiny Platformer

A yellow square in a brick cave. Collect the gold. Jump on the grey blocks. Do not walk into them.

## Objective

Clear the cave. Gold pulses in place — touch it to take it. Grey blocks patrol the platforms; land on one from above to stomp it, or it sends you back to the start.

There is one cave and eight gold bricks. When every gold brick is taken and every grey block is stomped, the cave is **cleared**. You can keep running around, or start a fresh run.

## Controls

**Keyboard:** **Left** / **Right** arrows or **A** / **D** to run. **Space**, **Up**, or **W** to jump. You jump from the ground (a tap just before you land still counts). **R** starts the cave over.

**Phone:** **◀** and **▶** sit under your left thumb, **JUMP** under your right, **RESTART** above jump. The pad appears on a phone-sized screen, or after a finger lands.

## Scoring

Each gold brick is **1**. Each stomp is **1**. Hitting a grey block from the side or below resets you to the start — gold and stomps you already took stay taken.

## What is saved

Your **best** gold count (and stomps on a tie) on this copy of the app. The cave itself is not saved mid-run. Close it, come back: the best line is still in the corner.
