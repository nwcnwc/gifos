# One Stroke

Draw **one line**. Lift. Send it. That is the whole turn. This is not a whiteboard — nobody may scribble freely. The picture only exists because people take turns.

## Objective

Stack strokes into a doodle. On your own, you are the whole table. With friends, each person adds exactly one line per turn. After a round you **name the picture**, then it plays back as a loop of the strokes arriving, in order.

## Solo

You land on the page. A ghost line draws itself until you do.

1. Pick a colour and a thickness.
2. Draw one line. Lift your finger or mouse.
3. **Undo** if that line is wrong (it only clears the line you have not sent).
4. Press **That’s my stroke**. It lands, then the next line is yours.

**Play loop** replays the stack arriving. **New page** wipes the doodle. There is no score.

## With friends

Press **With friends**, then press **Invite** in the bar **above** the app and send the link. Invite is not inside this page.

- Seats appear around the paper. Whose turn it is sits on the page.
- You cannot draw when it is not your turn.
- After everyone has drawn once, you vote on a title (pick a suggestion or write one). Then the loop plays.
- **Another round** keeps the picture and starts a new turn. **New page** starts a blank one.
- Up to eight seats. Extra people watch.
- A friend who leaves mid-turn is skipped.

Need two people before turns start. While you wait, send the Invite.

## Controls

- **Mouse:** click and drag on the paper, then release. Left button only.
- **Phone / touch:** one finger draws until you lift. That lift ends the stroke. The page will not scroll while you draw.
- **Keyboard:** there is none for drawing. Colour and thickness are the dots under the paper.
- **Undo** only throws away the stroke you have not sent. Sent lines stay.

## What is saved

Your solo doodle lives **in this file**. Close it, open it later, the lines are where you left them.

A live round lives in the shared room. Leaving with **← Solo** puts you back on your private page. The friend’s picture does not overwrite your doodle.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no lobby code and no game server. The room is the link.
