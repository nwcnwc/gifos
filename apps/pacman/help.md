# Pac-Man

You are the yellow one. Eat every pellet in the maze before the ghosts catch you. There are **twelve mazes** and **five lives**.

## Controls

**Computer**

- Move: arrow keys or `W` `A` `S` `D`
- Pause / continue: `Space` or `Enter`
- Title and game-over: `Space` or `Enter` to start again

**Phone**

The first time you put a finger on the screen, a pad appears: four directions and **PAUSE**. A mouse never shows the pad.

You cannot tap the maze itself. The yellow one only turns at a corridor.

## Rules

Pellets are 10 points. A power pellet (the big ones in the corners) makes the ghosts blue for a while — touch one then and it is 10 points and it runs home. Clear the maze and the next one loads. Five lives; a ghost that is not blue costs one.

The four ghosts hunt together. They are not random.

## A friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Everyone who opens the link is on the same cabinet. Each of you plays your own maze; the roster shows name, score, and lives. Nobody drives your yellow one but you.

## What is saved

Your **best score** stays in the file. A game in progress is not kept. Close the app and you start from the title, with the high score in the corner.
