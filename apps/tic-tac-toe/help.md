# Tic-Tac-Toe

Three in a row, on this device or from one link.

## Objective

A three-by-three grid. One of you is **X**, the other is **O**. Take turns putting a mark down. First to three in a line — across, down, or diagonal — wins. A full board with no line is a draw. **X** always goes first.

## Play here

**Computer** — a perfect opponent on this device. It does not lose. A clean game is a draw; a slip is a loss. You can be X or O.

**Two here** — pass the device. X, then O.

Tap or click an empty square. **Undo** takes back your last mark (and the computer's reply, if it just played). **Play again** starts a fresh board. **Change game** returns to the setup.

## Play a friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. There is no game server. First to sit is X, second is O. Extra people watch.

After a win or a draw, the next game starts in a few seconds. If someone leaves mid-game, the remaining player wins.

You cannot undo a mark on a shared board. Wait for their turn; the board will not move until they play.

## Scoring

Wins, draws, and losses against the computer or a friend are counted on this device. Passing the phone (Two here) is not counted.

## What is saved

The tally stays in the file. A local game in progress comes back when you reopen it. A finished board does not. A live friend's board lives only while the link is open.

An unofficial port of [Colyseus Tic-Tac-Toe](https://github.com/colyseus/demo-tic-tac-toe). The original needs a game server. This copy does not.
