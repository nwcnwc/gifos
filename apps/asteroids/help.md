# Asteroids

A little triangle against a field of rocks. Turn, thrust, shoot. Extra ships appear when you send the link.

## How to start

**Press space** (or tap the field, or **FIRE** on a phone). Until then the game is waiting.

Sound starts **muted**. Press **M** to hear the shots.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** arrows or **WASD** to turn and thrust. **Space** fires. There is no reverse and no hyperspace — down does nothing.
- **P** pauses. **M** mutes. **Tab** holds the scoreboard (only once someone else is in the room).
- **Phone:** **THRUST**, **LEFT**, **RIGHT**, and **FIRE** sit under your thumbs. The overlay appears on a phone, or after a finger lands.

The field wraps. Fly off one edge, you come in the opposite.

## Lives and score

You have **three ships** (the one you are flying, plus two extras drawn in the corner). Hit a rock, a saucer, or a saucer shot and you lose one. When they are gone, game over — wait a few seconds, then space again.

A big rock is 20 points and splits into three mediums; those are 60 and split into pebbles at 180. A saucer is 200. The next wave is one rock heavier, up to twelve. A saucer wanders through if you linger.

Your high score sits under the current one. There is no extra ship at 10,000.

## Extra ships

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. Extra ships appear in the **same field** — everyone flies their own, everyone shoots the same rocks. There is no in-app share button.

The person who opened the app flies the rocks and the saucer. Friendly fire is **off** unless they turn it on (**F**, or the button). Then a shot can kill another ship; the person who was hit still decides they died.

Tap the “N ships” tally (or hold Tab) for the scoreboard.

A third person who opens the link gets a ship too. This is friends in one field, not a ladder.

## What is saved

Your **high score** and whether sound is muted. The current wave is not kept. Sharing the file does not share a game in progress.
