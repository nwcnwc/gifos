# Battleboat

Hide a fleet of five ships on a **10×10** grid. Sink theirs before they sink yours. First to sink all five wins.

The five ships: **Carrier** (5), **Battleship** (4), **Destroyer** (3), **Submarine** (3), **Patrol** (2). They may not overlap or hang off the board.

## New game

**Play the computer** hunts on this device. **Play a friend** is a second person on another phone or computer — press **Invite** in the bar above the app to send the link.

## Place your ships

1. Tap a ship in the roster (or leave **Carrier** selected).
2. Tap your map to drop it. **Rotate** switches down / across. A ghost shows whether the drop fits.
3. **Scatter ships** places all five at random if you do not want to think.
4. When every ship is down, press **Start** (computer) or **Ready** (a friend).

On a phone, while you hunt, **their** map sits on top and yours shrinks underneath. That is the layout, not a broken zoom.

## Hunt

Tap a cell on the **enemy** map. Miss, hit, or sunk. You cannot fire the same cell twice.

Against the computer, it fires back at once (a short pause, then its shot). It hunts by where a remaining ship is most likely to sit, then finishes a hit.

Against a friend, you take turns. The person with the earlier id shoots first when both are ready. Hits and misses show on both screens. A pip bar lists which of their ships are still afloat — not where they are.

There is no keyboard aiming. Mouse click and finger tap are the same.

## A friend

Each of you hides a fleet on your own device. **Ship positions stay on this device.** Only shots and the revealed cells of your own fleet are shared. If they leave, press **Invite** again to bring them back, or **Leave**.

**Play again** starts a new round with new placement.

## What is saved

Games against the computer keep a win / hit tally on this device. A friend game keeps your hidden ships for the current round so a reload does not dump your fleet. Nothing else is stored.
