A programming language

How to play

  • A programming language is hiding. Guess a letter.
  • Right letters fill in. Six wrong ones and the drawing is finished.
  • The words are the original fourteen: Python, Ruby, Java, and the rest.

To play a friend, use Invite in the bar above. Same word.

  • Race — each of you has your own rope. First to finish it wins. You never see their letters, only how many wrong they have used.
  • Share — one rope. Every letter anyone tries is on it. You sink or swim together.

Unofficial port of Vanilla Javascript Hangman Game by simonjsuh.
