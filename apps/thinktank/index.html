How to play

Destroy the house in the other tinted corner. You may not leave your own pieces in harm's way.

On your turn — do one thing

  1. Place — tap a piece below, then a gold square around your home.
  2. Move — tap a piece already out, then a gold square.
  3. Turn a tank — tap the tank, then a facing. It shoots the way it points. Cyan marks a tank you can turn.
