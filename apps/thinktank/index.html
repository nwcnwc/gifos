How to play

Two sides, red and blue. Each has a home (the tinted corner) and a base inside it. Destroy the other base to win.

On your turn, do one thing: place a piece from your hand into your spawn (the ring around your home), move a piece already on the board, or turn a tank to face a new way.

You cannot step into either home except with your own base, and you cannot put your own pieces in harm's way.

Pieces

  • Shield — stops the other side's shots. Friendly tanks fire through it. Moves one space any way. Can be stolen, not shot.
  • Tank — fires in a straight line from its face to the edge. Moves one space across or down. Turn it by picking a facing in the tray, then tapping the tank.
  • Infiltrator + — steals a tank or shield next to it. Moves one space across or down. Cannot be stolen.
  • Infiltrator × — same steal, moves one space on the diagonal.
  • Mine — blows up pieces next to it (except a shield), including itself. Jumps two spaces, over others. Do not set it where it would hit your own.
  • Base — stays in its home. If it is destroyed, that side loses.

Shot and stolen pieces go back to their owner's hand, except a steal keeps the piece on the board and changes who owns it.
