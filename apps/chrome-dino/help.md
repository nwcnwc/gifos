# Chrome Dino

The Chrome dinosaur. Jump the cacti. Duck the birds. There is no network — that is the whole joke.

## Objective

Run as far as you can without hitting anything. One hit and the run is over.

## Controls

**Keyboard:** **Space** or **Up** jumps. **Down** ducks (or drops faster if you are already in the air). After a crash, **Space** or **Enter** starts again.

**Mouse:** click the desert to jump.

**Phone:** **JUMP** under your right thumb, **DUCK** under your left. Tap the desert to jump, the same as Chrome. The buttons appear on a phone, or after a finger lands.

Hold **DUCK** to stay low under a pterodactyl. A tap is only a jump.

## The desert

The first seconds are empty so you can start. Then cacti. Later, pterodactyls at three heights — jump the low ones, duck the high ones. The ground speeds up the further you go. Every **700** the sky flips to night and back.

## Scoring

Distance is the score, in the Chrome units you already know (a hundred is a chirp). The high score sits to the left of the current one, marked **HI**.

## Solo vs a live friend

Alone this is chrome://dino. Close it, come back: the high score is still here.

To race: press **Invite** in the bar above this app and send the link. There is no extra button inside the game. When someone is in the room:

- You both run the **same desert** (the cacti and birds match).
- The other dino is a **ghost** — tinted, a little ahead or behind.
- First to hit is losing; the one who runs **farthest** wins. A tie is a tie.
- If you crash first, jump again to chase — they may still be running.

The race bar along the top only appears when a friend is actually here.

## What is saved

Your **high score** on this copy of the app. A live race is only while the room is open. The current run is not saved.
