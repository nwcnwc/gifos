# Catch the Cat

A black cat sits in the middle of a honeycomb. You turn dots into walls. After every wall the cat takes one step, always toward a way out. Close every path and you win. Let it reach the rim and it runs.

## The board

**11×11** dots on a table you can pick up and turn. Eight walls are already scattered, so the first board is never empty. The outer ring is the **rim** — it is drawn in red, and a cat that reaches it is gone. You cannot wall a cat's own hex, and you cannot tap a wall twice.

## Turning the board

The board is a real 3D table, not a picture of one.

- **Drag** with one finger (or the mouse) to turn it — sideways spins it, up and down tilts it.
- **Pinch**, or scroll the wheel, to move in. It is a proper dolly, so the closer you get the deeper the perspective.
- **Two fingers** together also twist and shove the board around.
- **Flat** looks straight down at it, which is how the game was originally drawn. **Reset view** puts everything back.

A tap and a drag are told apart on release: anything that travelled more than a few pixels turns the board and never walls a hex. So a shaky thumb cannot cost you a move.

## Controls

**Tap an open dot.** That is the whole game. There is no keyboard.

- **Undo** takes back your last wall and the cat's last step.
- **New board** scatters a new set of starting walls.

The cat takes about six tenths of a second to walk a hex, and you can watch it do it — that is the point of the animation. Tapping again while it walks does not skip the step; the cat breaks into a run and catches up.

## Scoring

Your **tap count** is how many walls you added — not counting the eight that were already there. Lower is better. There are no lives.

## Playing with other people

Press **Invite** in the bar above this app. Once someone joins, a **Race / Team** switch appears. Either of you can flip it; the whole room follows, because the mode belongs to the round and not to a player.

### Race

Everyone gets the **same starting walls** and plays their own private copy. Your walls are yours — they never appear on anybody else's board. Fewest taps to pen the cat takes the round. If the cat gets away from you, you are out of that round. If it gets away from everyone, nobody takes it.

The list under the board is the running score:

- **#** — where you stand right now.
- **wins** — rounds taken so far. The ♛ marks whoever is ahead.
- **this board** — how the round in front of each player is going. "got away" means the cat beat them.
- **🔥3** — three rounds in a row.

When your round ends your board stops taking taps — the race is still on for everyone else, and a stray tap would only start you a private chase. You can still turn and zoom the board and watch. As soon as the last player finishes, every screen calls the same result. A tie splits the round. If everybody else leaves before it ends, it is called off and nobody scores: the last one left has not beaten anyone.

### Team

**One board, one cat each, and nobody takes turns.** Your taps move only your cat, whenever you make them. Everyone's cat has its own colour, and its name floats above it.

The board is shared, and that is the whole game: **the wall you lay pens everyone's cat, not just yours.**

- You **clear** the round when every cat is walled in.
- You **lose** it the moment **any one** cat reaches the rim — it does not wait for the others. One cat out is already the answer.

Your own cat being penned does not end your round. Keep walling: your walls are what pens everyone else's.

Cats do not block each other. Two can stand on the same hex, and walls are the only thing that stops any of them.

Up to seven cats have their own starting seat. Beyond that, seats are shared.

## What is saved

Nothing in the file. Close the app and the board is gone. The standings last as long as the room does.

Nobody can play your taps for you.
