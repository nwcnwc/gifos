# Catch the Cat

A black cat sits in the middle of a honeycomb. You turn dots into walls. After every wall the cat takes one step, always toward a way out. Close every path and you win. Let it reach the rim and it runs.

## The board

**11×11** dots, hex-style. Eight walls are already scattered, so the first board is never empty. You cannot wall the cat’s own hex, and you cannot tap a wall twice.

## Controls

**Phone, mouse, or finger:** tap an open dot. That is the whole game. There is no keyboard. On a phone, **pinch with two fingers** to zoom the board if the dots run small; one finger always stays a tap.

- **Undo** takes back your last wall and the cat’s last step. You cannot undo after the cat is caught or has run.
- **New board** scatters a new set of starting walls.

Playing alone, tapping a finished honeycomb starts another chase. Prefer **New board** when you mean a fresh start. In a race the finished board stops taking taps — see below.

## Scoring

Your **tap count** is how many walls you added — not counting the eight that were already there. Lower is better. There are no lives.

## Racing a friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above this app. Everyone who opens the link gets the **same starting walls**. You each play your own copy — same cat, same first eight — and the fewest taps to pen the cat takes the round. If the cat gets away from you, you are out of that round. If it gets away from everyone, nobody takes it.

The list under the board is the running score:

- **wins** — rounds taken so far. The ♛ marks whoever is ahead.
- **this board** — how the round in front of each player is going right now. "got away" means the cat beat them.
- **🔥3** — three rounds in a row.

When your round ends, your board stops taking taps: the race is still on for everyone else, and a stray tap would only start you a private chase. As soon as the last player finishes, every screen calls the same result. Press **Next round** and the whole room gets a new board.

A tie splits the round — everyone on the fewest taps takes it. If everybody else leaves before the round ends, it is called off and nobody scores; the last one left has not beaten anyone.

Until a friend joins, you just play the honeycomb in front of you.

Nobody can play your taps for you.

## What is saved

Nothing in the file. Close the app and the board is gone. The standings last as long as the room does.
