# Catch the Cat

A black cat sits in the middle of a honeycomb. You turn dots into walls. After every wall the cat takes one step, always toward a way out. Close every path and you win. Let it reach the rim and it runs.

## The board

**11×11** dots, hex-style. Eight walls are already scattered, so the first board is never empty. You cannot wall the cat’s own hex, and you cannot tap a wall twice.

## Controls

**Phone, mouse, or finger:** tap an open dot. That is the whole game. There is no keyboard, no zoom, and no pan.

- **Undo** takes back your last wall and the cat’s last step. You cannot undo after the cat is caught or has run.
- **New board** scatters a new set of starting walls.

If a round is already over, tapping the honeycomb starts another chase. Prefer **New board** when you mean a fresh start.

## Scoring

Your **tap count** is how many walls you added — not counting the eight that were already there. Lower is better. There are no lives.

## A live friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above this app. Everyone who opens the link gets the **same starting walls**. You each play your own copy — same cat, same first eight walls. Fewest taps to pen the cat wins. If the cat gets away, you are out of that round.

A list of names appears when someone else is in the room. **New board** hands everyone a new shared start. Until a friend joins, you just play the honeycomb in front of you.

There is no game server. Nobody can play your taps for you.

## What is saved

Nothing in the file. Close the app and the board is gone. A race only lasts while the room does.
