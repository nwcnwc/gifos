# Thumb Sprint

Mash the pad. Your sticker sprints. First across the tape wins.

## How to play

1. Mash (or press space) to start a countdown.
2. Wait for **GO**. Mash before that and you **false-start**: you lurch, you go back, and you stall until a moment after GO.
3. Mash as fast as you can. Each tap moves the sticker a fixed step.
4. First sticker to the tape wins. The last second **loops** so you can see whose nose broke it.
5. Mash again (if you started the race) for another go.

There is no steering and no jump. Just mash.

## Controls

**Phone / touch** — the pad is most of the lower half. Tap it.

**Mouse** — click the pad.

**Keyboard** — **space**. Holding space does not keep running; press it again for each step.

A buzz on the phone marks a tap, and a longer buzz marks a false start.

## Solo vs a live friend

On your own, pick **Ghost** or **Computer** in the header:

- **Computer** — a fair cadence. Beatable if you mash, not asleep if you don't.
- **Ghost** — a replay of your best time in the next lane. The first finished run sets a time; later runs race that ghost.

**Invite** (in the bar above the app) puts a friend in the next lane. Two to four lanes. The person who opened the app starts the gun; guests wait for GO on the pad. A fifth person watches.

Invite is OS chrome — this app does not draw a share button.

Jumping the countdown is visible to everyone in the race.

## Scoring

Time is seconds from GO to the tape, shown to two decimal places. Earliest finish wins. A false start does not add a penalty — you just go back and wait.

## What is saved

Your **best solo time** (and the ghost recording) stay in this file. Close the app and they are still here. Versus races do not replace that personal best. Ghost vs computer is remembered as a preference.
