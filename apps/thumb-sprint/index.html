Thumb Sprint

Mash to sprint.

How to play

  • Mash the pad (or space). First sticker across the tape wins.
  • Mash before go and you go back — false start. Lanes sit side by side so everyone sees it.
  • On your own: a ghost of your best time, or a computer with a fair cadence.
  • A friend in the next lane: press Invite in the bar above the app and send the link. Two to four lanes. No race server.
  • The last second loops so you can see the nose.
