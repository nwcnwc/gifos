# Blackjack

Beat the dealer to 21 without going over. **Toy chips. No cash.** Nothing is wagered and nothing is paid out.

Face cards are 10. Aces count 11 until that would bust, then 1. The dealer hits a 16 and stands on 17. A natural 21 on the deal is blackjack.

Stake is **10** chips. Start with **200**. Bust, lose, or a dealer blackjack costs the stake. A win pays even money. A player blackjack pays **3:2**. A push returns the stake.

## Controls

- **Deal** starts a hand.
- **Hit** takes another card.
- **Stand** locks your total; the dealer plays.

## Play with friends

Press **Invite** in the bar. Each person is a seat. The host (the first person) deals. Hit and stand on **your** hand. When every seat has stood or busted, the dealer plays in the host's browser.

## What is saved

Your chip pile comes back with this copy of the app. A live table lives in the room while people are connected.
