# Sandspiel

Pour elements onto a little world and watch them run. Sand piles. Water finds a way down. Lava and water make stone. Fire eats wood and dies in water. Plants drink. Paint with a finger or a mouse.

## Elements

Sand, water, wall, gas, cloner, fire, wood, lava, ice, plant, acid, stone, dust, mite, oil, rocket, fungus, seed. **Erase** clears a cell. Tap one, then paint.

## Brush, pause, undo

Five brush sizes, from a dot to a splash. The middle size is the usual pour.

**Pause** freezes the world so you can look or paint still. **Undo** takes back the last stroke (a short stack). **Reset** wipes the world empty.

## Private saves

The last board stays in this copy of the app. Close it, come back, it is still there. Type a name and press **Keep** to hold extra boards on this device. Tap a name to load it. The trash removes that named copy only.

## Play together — a wall of boards

Playing alone is the original toy. Want a public wall of worlds? Press **Play together**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. Friends who open it land on the wall.

**Put on the wall** pins your current board as a small card (a tiny picture, not the whole pour). Tap a card to open that world; it pauses so you can look. Each person only writes their own “here” mark. Cards are shared.

**← Solo** leaves the wall. Your private saves stay on this device.
