# Sandspiel

Pour elements onto a little world and watch them run. The world lives in this file: close the app, come back, the dunes are still here.

## Pour

Tap or drag on the world. **Sand** piles. **Water** finds a way down. **Lava** and water make stone. **Fire** eats wood and dies in water. **Plants** drink. There is also wall, gas, cloner, wood, ice, acid, stone, dust, mite, oil, rocket, fungus, seed. **Erase** clears a cell. The name in the corner is what you are holding.

Five brush sizes, from a dot to a splash. The middle size is the usual pour.

**Pause** freezes the world so you can look or paint still. **Undo** takes back the last stroke (a short stack). **Reset** wipes the world empty.

On a phone, swipe the coloured chips sideways to reach every element. A first-open world is empty — tap to pour water.

## Private boards

The last world stays in this file. Type a name and press **Keep** to hold extra boards on this device. Tap a name to load it. The trash removes that named copy only.

## Play together — a wall of boards

Playing alone is the original toy. A public wall of worlds: press **Play together**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. Friends who open it land on the wall.

**Put on the wall** pins your current board as a small card (a tiny picture, not the whole pour). Tap a card to open that world; it pauses so you can look. Each person only writes their own “here” mark. Cards are shared.

**← Solo** leaves the wall. Your private saves stay on this device.
