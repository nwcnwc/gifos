# HexGL

A futuristic racing ship on a dawn city track. Three laps. Beat your best time — or the ghost of whoever sent you the link.

## Start

**Start** on the menu, then wait for the countdown: Get ready, 3, 2, 1, Go. Quality is **Low** on a phone (a smaller picture that still runs) and **Mid** on a computer. You can tap the Quality line before you start.

Sound starts **on**. Mute from the menu.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** arrows or **WASD** to steer and accelerate. **Down / S** brakes. **Q** and **E** air-brake (slide).
- **Phone:** left thumb on the pad to steer, **GO** under the right thumb to thrust, **BRAKE** beside it. The overlay appears on a phone, or after a finger lands.
- **Esc** (or Back) returns to the menu from a race.

Boost pads on the track (red on the collision map, cyan on the ship) give a burst of speed. Walls cost shield. When the shield is gone, the ship is destroyed.

## Laps and times

Three laps. Checkpoints are automatic. Finish time and each lap show on the result card. Your **best time** is saved in this file and shown on the menu. If you have a best, a **ghost** of that run flies the same line while you race.

## Ghosts from a link

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. Extra ships appear as translucent ghosts on the same track — everyone races their own run, nobody collides with anybody else. Times show on the board. There is no in-app share button.

## What is saved

Your best time, the ghost of that run, quality, and whether sound is muted. A race in progress is not kept.
