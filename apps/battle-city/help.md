# Battle City

You are a yellow tank at the bottom of a brick city. Twenty enemy tanks come for the **eagle**. Destroy them all before they blow the eagle — or you — up. There are **35 stages**. This copy always starts at stage 1; there is no stage picker.

The picture is the old 256×240 screen. It scales up to fill the window. There is no zoom and no pan. The playfield is 13 tiles across.

## Controls

**Keyboard**

- Move: `W` `A` `S` `D` or the arrow keys
- Fire: `J`, `K`, `Space`, or `Enter`
- Pause: `P` or `Escape` (the host of the room pauses for everyone)
- Title: up/down to pick **1 PLAYER** or **2 PLAYERS**, then fire or `Enter`

**Phone**

The first time you put a finger on the screen, a pad appears: four directions on the left, **FIRE** on the right, and one button above the pad — **START** on the title screen, pause once the fight begins. A mouse never shows the pad, even on a laptop with a touchscreen.

A tap counts. You do not have to hold a direction down: a quick tap turns the tank and nudges it one step.

## Rules

Bricks chip. Steel bounces ordinary shots — only a fully upgraded gun breaks it. Rivers block you. Trees hide you (drawn on top of the tank). Snow is painted ice; you do not slide on it.

Four kinds of enemy: basic, fast, power, armour (armour takes four hits). A flashing red one is carrying a prize. Pick it up:

- **Star** — upgrades your gun (four steps). After the last step, extra stars are points.
- **Helmet** — brief shield
- **Grenade** — every enemy on screen blows up
- **Timer** — freezes the bots
- **Shovel** — steels the walls around the eagle for a while
- **Tank** — an extra life

You start with three lives. Another life every 10,000 points. Friendly fire from the green tank freezes you for a moment; it does not kill you. If the eagle breaks, the game is over.

Score: 100 / 200 / 300 / 400 by enemy type, 500 for a prize.

## A friend

Alone, it is you against the computer. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and a friend on another phone or computer is the green tank — same stage, same eagle, two guns. There is no game server. Pick **2 PLAYERS** on the title (it says **2 PLAYERS (INVITE)** until someone is in the room). If they join mid-stage they become green on the **next** stage.

The host of the room runs the stage. Guests send move and fire.

## What is saved

Only the **hi-score**. A game in progress is not kept. Close the app and you start again from the title.
