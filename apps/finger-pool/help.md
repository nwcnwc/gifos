# Finger Pool

A green table, fifteen coloured balls in a triangle, a white one waiting. Flick any ball. Knock colours into the six pockets.

## Objective

Pocket the coloured balls. There is **no stick**, **no stripes vs solids**, and **no 8-ball**. Any ball, including the white one, is legal. Faster flick is a harder shot.

## Controls

**Touch (phone)**

Put a finger on a ball, drag the way you want it to go, let go. A dashed line shows the direction while you pull. A power bar fills when the flick is hard enough.

**Mouse**

Same as a finger: click a ball, drag, release. Faster movement is more power, not a longer drag.

**Keyboard**

There are no keyboard shots. Use a finger or the mouse. **♪** mutes the click-and-pocket tones. **New rack** appears when the table is clear.

There is no zoom and no pan. The table is a 2:1 felt, letterboxed in the window.

## Rules

Wait until everything stops before the next flick. Coloured balls that drop stay down. If the **white** ball falls in, it comes back to its starting spot — that is not a foul, and it does not cost you the shot.

The rack is over when no colours are left. **New rack** sets them up again.

## Scoring

Each colour you pocket is **one point**. White does not score. On your own the total is just “how many went in.” With another person, **you keep the balls you pocket** — their pockets are theirs.

## Solo vs a live friend

**Play** is you, on this device, flick whenever the balls are still.

**Two on this device** is hot-seat: you flick, they flick, same screen. The banner names whose turn it is.

For a friend on **another** phone or computer: press **Invite** in the bar **above** this app (not inside the table), then send the link. You take turns, one flick at a time. The other person watches the balls roll. When they join, you start a **fresh rack**. If they leave, you land back on the menu.

## What is saved

Sound on/off stays on this device. Scores live only for this rack / this room. Closing the app does not keep the table.
