# Emoji Minesweeper

Minesweeper drawn in emoji. Clear every safe square. Step on a mine and the board blows.

## Objective

Open all the squares that are **not** mines. Numbers (1️⃣–8️⃣) tell you how many mines touch that square. The face at the top grins while you play, 😎 if you clear the board, 😵 if you hit a mine.

There is no points tally. **Time** and **Moves** are the score. **BOMBS** is how many flags you still owe.

## On a computer

- **Click** a square to step on it.
- **Right-click** to plant or remove a flag (🚧 in the default set).
- **Double-click** a number to open the squares around it — the same as the original. Flag the mines first, or you may step on one.
- **R** starts a new game (not while you are typing in Settings). **Escape** closes Settings.

## On a phone

- **Tap** a square to step on it.
- **Hold** (~half a second) to plant or remove a flag. The tap that follows a hold is ignored, so a flag is not also a step.
- **Double-tap** a number to open its neighbours — same as double-click. If the page zooms instead, pinch back; the board itself has no zoom.

## Settings (🔧)

Columns, rows, and how many mines, plus the emoji set (chicks, cake, flowers, circles, weather). **Save and restart** applies them. The grinning face also starts a new game.

Biggest board is 2,500 squares; you cannot place more mines than squares.

## Solo vs a live friend

On your own this is the original game: a **random** board, and the **first click is always safe** (if that square was a mine, the board is dealt again under you).

**Invite** is in the bar **above** the app, not inside it. When a friend opens it you both get the **same** board. First to clear it wins. A strip at the top shows each other’s time and how much of the board is open.

In a race the first click is **not** guaranteed safe — reshuffling would give you different boards. Anyone may deal a new shared board with the face button. Times ride with each player; you cannot write on theirs.

## What is saved

Your last board size, mine count, and emoji set stay **inside this app** on this device. The board you were mid-clearing is not saved. Race times live only while the room is open.
