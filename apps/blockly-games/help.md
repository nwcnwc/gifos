# Blockly Games

Three short games that teach programming by snapping colourful blocks together. No typing. Designed for someone who has never written a program.

## Puzzle

Match each animal. Drag the **picture** into the picture hole, pick how many **legs**, and stack the **traits** (feathers, whiskers, honey…) underneath. Press **Check**. Green means that piece is right; the highlighted one is not. There is only one puzzle.

## Maze

Walk the little person to the flag. Drag blocks from the tray on the left (on a phone it is still that tray — it is wide enough to grab) into the white space, then press **Run**.

- **move forward** — one square.
- **turn left / turn right** — a quarter turn.
- **repeat until** (the flag) — keep going until you arrive. Later levels add **if path** and **if / else**.

Some levels only let you use a few blocks. That is the point: a loop does the work of many moves. If you walk into a wall, press **Reset** and try again. Tap a level number to skip around. A filled circle is a level you have solved.

## Turtle

The turtle draws as it walks. **repeat 4 times** around **move forward** and **turn 90°** is a square. Later: colour, pen up (walk without drawing), nested loops, a star, a moon. Press **Run** and compare your ink to the faint picture underneath. Level 10 is open-ended — draw anything.

A slow/fast slider sits under the picture.

## A live friend

On your own it is the original games. A friend who joins this copy lands on the **same game and level**, with the **same blocks**. Whoever snaps last, both screens follow.

## What is saved

Which games and levels you have finished, which one you were on, and the blocks you left there. Close it, come back, you are still in the maze.
