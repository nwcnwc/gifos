# Chess Grandmaster

Play chess against the real engine, on this device. It never needs the network.

## Against the engine

On **New game** pick:

- **Play as** White, Black, or random. The board turns so you sit at the bottom.
- **Strength** — Beginner (~1320) up through Grandmaster (~2850), then **Stockfish (full)** with no handicap. The note under the list says what that rung does.
- **Engine thinks** — 0.3s, 0.8s, 2s, or 5s per move.
- **Coaching** — evaluation bar, win/draw/loss odds, the best line, and **Hint**. A hint appears only when you press Hint. Turn coaching off for a clean board.

The first open waits on **Loading engine…**. Start stays locked until it says **Stockfish ready**.

## Controls

**Phone and mouse:** tap a piece, then tap where it goes. Dots are empty landings; rings are captures. There is no drag, no keyboard, and no clocks.

A pawn that reaches the last rank asks **Promote to…** (queen, rook, bishop, knight).

- **Hint** — marks the engine’s choice for this position.
- **Take back** — undoes your last move **and** the engine’s reply, so it is your turn again.
- **Resign** / **New game**

Castling, en passant, and promotion all work. Check is named on the status line.

## How a game ends

Checkmate, stalemate, the fifty-move rule, or resign. There is no clock flag, and this app does not claim a draw by repetition.

## Game history

Every finished engine game is stored in this file. **Game history** lists them. Open one to step through the moves. A game in progress auto-saves: close the tab, come back, resume.

Friend games are the live board only — they do not land in that history list.

## A live friend

**Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar above this app. Two people take White and Black; everyone else waits in line. **Winner stays**, loser goes to the back. **Resign** is on when you are seated.

The engine is **not** a third player and gives no hints at this table. Either seat can tick **Commentary**: after a move the engine mutters a reaction for both of you. It never shows the best move.

Choose **keep the room alive** when you Invite if you want the table to survive when the person who started it leaves.

## What is saved

Your in-progress engine game, the strength and think-time for that game, and the history of finished engine games. A friend’s table lives in the room while the room does.
