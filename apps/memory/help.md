# Memory

Watch the coloured pads light up. Tap them back in the same order. Each round adds **one more step**. Miss, and the round is over.

## Controls

Tap a pad (or click it). That is the whole game. There is nothing to type and nothing to drag.

**Start** begins a round. After a miss, Start again.

The number on a pad during the demo is which step of the sequence it is.

## Rules

You begin with **four** pads. After **level 7** two more appear (six). After **level 14**, eight. The lights also get faster as you go.

Score is how many rounds you have cleared. A miss ends the round; the best score in the corner is the longest you have ever gone on this file.

## A friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Both of you get the **same** sequence. You never see their taps — only the level they have reached. Play your own pads. Higher score wins the round.

## What is saved

Your **best score** stays in the file. A round in progress is not kept.

Unofficial port of [Mnimi](https://github.com/sepandhaghighi/mnimi) by Sepand Haghighi.
