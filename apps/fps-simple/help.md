# FPS Simple

You start in a sunlit market street with a rifle. A garrison patrols it and hunts you. They take cover, work around your flank, throw grenades to move you, and go down when they are hit. Rounds travel, drop over distance, and punch through thin material — what you stand behind matters. Lean before you step out.

## Play

The first screen **builds the street**. Wait until **Play** lights up, then press it. That tap also grabs the mouse (on a computer) and starts the sound.

**Watch demo** is a looping combat screensaver — a shop-window loop, not a match. Tap it, or press **Esc**, to leave.

## Computer

- **WASD** move · **mouse** aim
- **Click** fire · **right-click** aim down sights
- **R** reload · **Shift** sprint · **Ctrl** crouch · **Space** jump
- **Q / E** lean around a corner
- **1 / 2** swap weapons · **G** grenade · **F** use · **V** melee
- **Tab** hold for scores · **Esc** pause (and always hands the pointer back)

Press a key that does nothing and a short reminder of these bindings flashes.

Pause is also where you change **sensitivity**, **field of view**, **invert-Y**, and **quality**. Quality picks itself from what this device can do; you can override it.

## Phone

- Left thumb on the pad to move. Push the pad to its **edge** to sprint (there is no sprint button).
- Drag anywhere on the **right** to aim. A short, still tap there fires.
- **FIRE**, **ADS**, **RELOAD**, **JUMP**, **CROUCH** sit under your right thumb.
- Pause is the **❚❚** button.

The phone layout only appears after a real finger touches the screen, so a laptop with a touchscreen keeps the mouse.

## A live friend

Press **Invite** in the **bar above the app** — not inside the game. Everyone who opens the link lands in the same street, same buildings, same sun. The garrison stands down; the other players are the opposition.

Comfortable up to about six people. Friends glide a fraction of a second behind you. This is a deathmatch over a link, not a ranked match.

**Alone you fight the garrison. In a room you fight each other — not both.**

## What is saved

Sensitivity, field of view, invert-Y, and a quality you actually chose stay on this device. Scores live for the room, then they are gone.
