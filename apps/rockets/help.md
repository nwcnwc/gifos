# Rockets

You are a little rocket in a field of stars. Fly, catch them, score. A round is one minute. Then the next one starts, and your best lives in this file.

## Controls

- **Phone:** a thumb anywhere is the stick. It appears under your finger.
- **Keyboard:** arrows or **WASD**.
- **Mouse:** point where you want to go (on a computer, and only while a finger is not flying).
- **Enter** or **Space** starts from the title.

The stick is a target speed, not a thruster. Release and you coast to a stop. The edges of the sky bounce you back.

## Stars

- Pale stars — **1** point
- Gold stars — **3**
- Blue comets (they drift) — **5**

Grab several in a row (within about a second and a half) and a combo bonus stacks on top. The field refills as you empty it. Overlapping another rocket bumps you both.

## Alone and with a friend

Alone you play against the clock. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and a friend lands in the same sky — live scores, the same stars, a bump if you cross. There is no lobby and no account. The room is the link.

The person who opened the room writes the starfield. If they leave, the stars freeze until someone starts a new room. Two rockets cannot collect the same star twice; first claim wins.

When a friend is in the sky, a small scoreboard lists names. After the minute, both of you see the round’s scores, then the next minute begins.

## Saved

Your best score lives in this file. A round’s live score does not. Sharing the GIF shares the best, not the current sky.

Unofficial port of [Rocket Universe](https://github.com/lauthieb/rocket-universe) by lauthieb. The original needed a game server; this copy does not.
