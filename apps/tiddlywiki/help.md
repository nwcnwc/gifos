# TiddlyWiki

A notebook of small pages called **tiddlers**. Close the app and they are still here. Send **Invite** in the bar above and the same wiki is live with whoever opens the link.

## Write a note

- The **plus** button (or **alt-N** on a keyboard) opens a new tiddler.
- Give it a title. Type the body. Click the tick (or **ctrl-Enter**) to keep it.
- Notes save themselves as you go. There is no download step.
- Tag a tiddler to group it. Search lives in the sidebar.
- Link with `[[Title of another tiddler]]`. Bold is `''like this''`, italic is `//like this//`.
- For Markdown, set the tiddler's type to `text/markdown` (the type field on the editor).

## Find your way

- The **sidebar** lists Open, Recent, and tags. On a phone it hides; open it from the control at the top left.
- Click a title in the river to edit it. The pencil and the tick sit on the tiddler’s toolbar.
- **ctrl-Enter** saves an edit. **Escape** cancels it.
- **Back** on a phone cancels an edit, or closes the tiddler you are reading, or hides the sidebar.

## Share the wiki

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Everyone who opens the link sees and edits the same tiddlers. Your open tabs stay yours. There is no account.

## What is saved

Every tiddler you write or change, plus the ones you had open, live in this file on this device. Sharing the GIF shares the notebook. A tiddler you delete stays gone.
