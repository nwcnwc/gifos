# QR Code

Type a **link, a phone number, a message, a contact, or any text**. A code appears that a phone can point at. Nothing is uploaded. This is a general maker — for a fridge card with the Wi-Fi name and password, use **WiFi Card** instead.

## Make a code

1. Pick **Text**, **Link**, **Phone**, **SMS**, **Email**, or **Contact**.
2. Fill the boxes. The picture updates as you type.
3. Hold the screen up, or press **Download PNG** / **Copy picture** / **Print**.

A **Link** gets `https://` if you leave the scheme off. A **Contact** becomes a vCard a phone can save.

## Looks

Under **Size and colours**:

- **Error correction** is how much of the picture can be scuffed and still read. **L** is smallest, **M** is the default, **Q** / **H** are tougher and denser.
- **Size** is only how large it draws on this screen.
- **Dark** and **Light** are the two colours of the squares. Keep the light colour light — phones need the quiet white border. Too-close colours get a warning.

## What is saved

The last code, its type, size, colours, and a short **Recent** list stay in this file on this device. They come back the next time you open it. Tap a recent row to restore it.

A shared GifOS link can open this app already filled in.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app to show the **same code** in a meeting. People who join see it and can point a phone at it. They do not type their own — the host’s code is the one shown.
