# Nullboard

A compact kanban. A column of notes is a **list**. A set of lists is a **board**. Everything you type stays in this file.

## Notes and lists

- **Click** (or tap) a note to edit. Click outside, or **Shift-Enter**, when you are done. **Escape** throws the edit away.
- **Ctrl-Enter** saves and adds a note after this one. **Ctrl-Shift-Enter** adds one before.
- **Drag** a note to another place, including another list.
- **≡** on a note (hover on a computer, tap on a phone): **X** deletes, **R** makes it a raw heading, **_** collapses it to the first line.
- **≡** on a list: delete, move, or **+ Note**.
- **≡** on the board (top of the page): **+ List**, undo, redo, or delete the board.

## Boards

The **≡** at the top right lists every board in this file, adds a new one, and exports or imports a `.nbx` save. Deleting a list can be undone; deleting a **board** cannot.

**Ctrl-Z** is undo (one revision back). **Ctrl-Y** or **Ctrl-Shift-Z** is redo. The last fifty revisions of a board are kept.

## Phone

Swipe the lists sideways. The bar at the bottom:

- **+ Note** — a new note on the last list
- **+ List** — a new list on this board
- **Undo** — one revision back
- **Boards** — the board list and UI preferences

Tap **≡** on a note or list to open the menu that hover would show on a computer.

## Links

`http://` and `https://` in a note pulse on hover. **CapsLock** or **Control** highlights every link so a left-click opens it.

## What is saved

Every board, list, and note in this file, plus the font and theme you picked. Close the app and come back; you are on the same board. A copy of the file carries that same board with it.

Unofficial port of [Nullboard](https://nullboard.io) by Alexander Pankratov.
