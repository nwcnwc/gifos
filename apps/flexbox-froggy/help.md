# Flexbox Froggy

Help Froggy and friends onto their lilypads by writing **CSS flexbox**. The pond on the right (on a phone: above) is a flex container. The code you type is the style.

## How a level works

1. Read the short lesson. Coloured words like `justify-content` are tappable — they open the list of values. Tap a value to drop it into the editor.
2. Type CSS in the grey box, between the `#pond {` that is already there and the closing `}`. One property per line, with a semicolon.
3. The frogs hop as you type. When every frog sits on the matching lilypad, **Next** lights up.
4. Press **Next** (or Enter, once the box is full) to hop to the next pad. The arrows beside **Level** skip around; tap the level number for the map of all 24.

There is no score. A green circle on the map means you have solved that level.

## Settings

**Beginner** shows the directions. **Intermediate** hides them. **Expert** also picks the next level at random.

**Colorblind mode** draws a shape on each frog and pad so colour is not the only clue.

**Reset** (in the level map) wipes solved levels and sends you back to 1.

## Keyboard

The editor is ordinary text. **Enter** adds a line until the box is full, then it presses Next. **Ctrl+Enter** / **⌘+Enter** checks and advances.

## A live friend

On your own it is the original game. A friend who joins this pond shares the CSS with you — whoever types, the frogs hop on both screens. Tiny named frogs along the water show who is here.

## What is saved

The level you are on, the CSS you typed, which levels you have solved, and your settings. Close it, come back, the frogs are where you left them.
