Anyroad

Drive any road on Earth. Search a place, drop in, go.

Street addresses work. Search runs once when you press Search, and the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's geocoder to be turned into coordinates — their policy allows one request a second, which is also why there is no type-ahead.
