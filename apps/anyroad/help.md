# Anyroad

Search for anywhere on Earth, drop in, and drive. The road under you is the real road, built from live map data as you go. Hills climb; a crest will launch you.

## Getting there

The first screen is a search. Street addresses work. Press **Search** once — there is no type-ahead. Or tap a preset (Paris, Manhattan, Stelvio Pass, and a few others) or a place you have been.

You land on the nearest road, facing along it. The world is empty for a few seconds while the ground and the tarmac arrive. A tiny map in the corner shows tiles still loading; tap it if you want the reasons.

☰ is Settings. 🌍 hops somewhere else.

## Driving

By default the car **cruises** at whatever that class of road is built for. Your job is steering and braking. Hold **BRAKE** at a standstill for a beat to reverse; the speedo says **R**.

- **Keyboard:** arrows or **WASD** to steer and to override the cruise. **Space** fires the blaster. **X** is the handbrake. **R** puts you back on the nearest road.
- **Phone — Wheel:** turn the on-screen wheel (or slide the left half of the picture). The brake is the only pedal you need.
- **Stick:** drag anywhere. Sideways steers; up/down trims a speed you then hold.
- **Tilt:** the phone's lean is the wheel. Set “straight ahead” by choosing Tilt.

A courtyard can trap you. After a couple of seconds of going nowhere, **Stuck? Back to the road** appears — or press **R**.

The ▲ on the dash takes off. **GO** climbs, **BRAKE** descends, steer to turn. Take the wings off and you fall; the landing cares how fast you were going down.

Tap the picture (or space) to fire. An animal you hit with it is off the road; traffic takes a few shots. Hitting a deer, a building, or another car cracks the windscreen. When it is wrecked, **Repair and carry on**.

## Wildlife and traffic

Deer, sheep, geese and the odd cow graze the verges and wander across. They are yours alone — a friend in the same place sees a different herd. Other cars use the same roads you do. Set both in Settings, or turn them off.

## A live friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Everyone who opens the link lands in the same place, in the same world, with live cars. 🔆 sends up a flare they can see.

**Race** (🏁) drops a flag at a distance you pick — 800 m, 2.5 km, 6 km, or 15 km — on a random bearing, then snaps it onto the nearest road. Everyone starts from where they are. First to the flag wins. This is not a course you draw.

Settings → **Copy a link to here** is different: it opens this spot (and the aeroplane, if you are flying) for someone who has never used the app. It is not the race room.

## What is saved

Your settings, recent places, and a **private** cache of roads you have already driven — coming back does not need the network. The car's place is not kept; you hop again. Map pictures stay on this device. An Invite shares live cars and the race, not your cache.
