Other income

A pension, an annuity, rent, part-time work. Tick rises with inflation for anything indexed — Social Security is; most company pensions are not, and that difference is worth tens of thousands over a retirement. Income that starts before you retire is added to what you save, not to what you live on.

Big one-offs and college

Anything big that lands on one year or runs for several: college, a wedding, a new roof, a few years of helping a parent, selling a house, an inheritance. Set for how many years to 4 and it bills every year for four years — one child, one row.
