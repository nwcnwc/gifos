# Bowling

Ten pins at the far end. Ten frames. Knock them down.

## Throw

There is no keyboard. Use a finger or the mouse.

1. **Slide** left and right to line the ball up.
2. **Flick up** the lane, then let go.

The flick has to travel **up** (more vertical than sideways), at least a short stroke, and finish within a second. A slow drag or a sideways swipe will not throw — that is how it is meant to work, not a stuck ball. While you pull, a bar shows how hard the flick is. Harder flicks send a faster ball. A little sideways motion as you release adds a bit of drift.

Wait until the ball and pins settle. Then you bowl again, or the next person does.

## Scoring

Ordinary ten-pin rules:

- **Strike (X)** — all ten on the first ball. That frame is done. The next two throws add on.
- **Spare (/)** — the rest of the pins on the second ball. The next throw adds on.
- A miss is **-**. The number is how many fell.
- **Tenth frame:** a strike or spare earns extra throws in that frame, up to three balls.
- A perfect game is 300.

Banners call **Strike!**, **Spare!**, **Gutter**, or the pin count.

♪ in the corner mutes the tones.

## Solo, two here, a friend

- **Bowl** — you play all ten frames on this device.
- **Two on this device** — pass the screen. You and Player 2 alternate after each finished frame.
- **Invite** (the bar above this app, not inside it) — a friend bowls on their own phone or computer. You take turns. Whoever has finished the fewest frames goes next; if someone has started a frame, they finish it. You watch their ball while they throw. Each person keeps their own score. Highest total after ten frames wins.

**New game** appears when the sheet is full. In a shared game it starts a fresh sheet for everyone.

If your friend leaves, you land back at the menu.

## What is saved

Only whether sound is on. The score sheet is not kept after you close the app. A live friend’s scores live in the room for as long as the room does.
