# Offline Neural Text to Speech

This is a **voice for your whole computer**, not a one-off talker. Other apps ask it to speak. The words stay on this device.

## Set it as your voice

1. Keep its icon in the **Providers** folder on your Home Screen. Anywhere else it wears a red ✕ and will not answer.
2. Open **Settings → AI models**.
3. Set **Text → speech** to **Offline Neural Text to Speech**.

From then on, Reader and anything else that talks uses this voice. You do not press Speak in those apps — they ask this one for you.

## Try it here

Type in the box, pick a voice, press **Speak**.

- **Bella, Luna, Rosie, Kiki** — four women.
- **Jasper, Bruno, Hugo, Leo** — four men. Leo is the default.

It thinks before it talks. The first sound can take several seconds, longer on a phone, longer if the box is full. That wait is the voice being made, not a freeze. This page starts playing the first sentence while it still makes the rest.

The first time you open it, a **24 MB voice** downloads once and stays on this device. After that it does not need the network.

## What other apps hear

A link or another app can ask this computer to say a line out loud. You see the words and confirm before it speaks. Nothing is sent anywhere. If your browser blocks sound until a tap, press **Tap to hear it**.

Apps that already pick cloud names (`nova`, `shimmer`, `fable`, `echo`, `onyx`, `alloy`) still work — they map onto these eight voices.

## Private vs shared

- **Private.** The try-box text is not saved. Close the app and it is gone. The voice file stays on this device so the next app can speak without downloading again.
- **Invite** in the bar above does not share a conversation. There is no live friend mode. This app serves this computer.

## Honest limits

- English only.
- It is slower than the robotic offline voice. That other voice starts instantly; this one sounds like a person and has to think.
- A very long passage still pauses between sentences. Ask for shorter pieces if you want it to keep up.
- If Speak fails with a missing-voice message, open this app once so the 24 MB file can finish landing, then try again.
