# shapez.io

Build a factory that cuts, rotates and paints shapes, then belts them into the hub in the middle of the floor.

## The goal

The hub asks for a shape. Deliver enough of that exact shape and it asks for the next one — and unlocks a new machine. Level 1 is whole uncolored circles. Later you will need halves, quarters, and painted colours.

Circles sit west of the hub, rectangles east, red colour north. Zoom out if you cannot see a patch.

## Build

- **Extractor** — only on a resource patch. Faces the way it dumps.
- **Belt** — drag to draw a line. Items travel the way the arrows point.
- **Cutter** — splits a shape into a left half and a right half. Both sides must be able to leave or it clogs. **Trash** eats whatever you belt into it.
- **Rotator** — turns a shape 90° clockwise.
- **Painter** — shape in the back, colour on the right-hand side, painted shape out the front.

There is no cost and no limit. Wrong shapes still vanish into the hub; they just do not count.

## Controls

- **Computer:** click to place, drag a belt (it will not overwrite an extractor), **R** rotate the held machine or the one under the cursor, **Q** the other way, **X** delete, wheel zoom, WASD or arrows pan, middle-drag pan, **H** recentre on the hub, space pause, **+** / **−** speed.
- **Phone:** the strip at the bottom is the machines. Drag with the Move tool to pan; pinch to zoom. With Belt selected, a drag draws belts. A short tap places.

## A friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. You both place on the same floor. The person who opened the app runs the belts; everyone can build.

## What is saved

The factory — every machine, every belt — and which level the hub is on. Close it, come back, it is still there. Items on the belts are not kept; extractors start filling them again.
