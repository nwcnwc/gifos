# Connect Four

Drop a disc. First to **four in a line** — across, down, or diagonal — wins. The board is **6 rows by 7 columns**. Red always goes first.

## How to drop

- **Phone / finger:** tap anywhere in a column. The whole column is the target, not just a hole. The disc falls with gravity.
- **Mouse:** click a column. Hover shows a preview disc on the rail above the board.
- **Keyboard:** keys `1` through `7` drop in that column, left to right.

A full column will not take another disc. If the board fills with no four, it is a **draw**.

## On your own

From **New game**, pick **Computer** or **Two here**, then **Start game**.

- **Computer** — you are red. Blue thinks on this phone. There is no game server. **Undo** takes back your last drop *and* the computer’s reply.
- **Two here** — pass the device. Red, then blue. **Undo** takes back the last disc.

**New game** returns to the setup screen. A gold line marks the winning four. The two coloured seats show whose turn it is.

## A live friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. There is no game server.

The first two people sit red and blue. Anyone else **watches**. You only drop on your own turn. Extra people in the room do not play until a seat opens.

- **Resign** appears after the first disc. The other colour wins.
- If your opponent leaves mid-game, you win.
- After a result, the next game starts on its own in a few seconds.

**Leave** puts you back on the setup screen.

## What is saved

A local game in progress lives in this file — the mode and the drops so far. Close it, open it again, and you are still in that game.

Finished local games are not restored. A live friend’s board lives in the room for that invite, not in the file.
