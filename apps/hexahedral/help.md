# Hexahedral

Push **every raised (pink) block down** before you run out of moves. Your piece is the green cube. Pink tiles are up. Blue tiles are already down. Beige tiles are broken — you cannot stand on them.

Stepping onto a tile **toggles** it: pink becomes blue, blue becomes pink. You must step onto a neighbour (no diagonals, no jumping). Use all the pinks up and the level is done. Hit the move limit with pinks still up and the level restarts.

## Controls

- **Tap** a block next to you.
- **Swipe** / drag toward a neighbour.
- **Arrow keys** on a laptop. **R** restarts the level.

Easy, medium, and hard are ten levels each. Finish one and the next loads. You can jump to any level you have already reached.

## What is saved

The furthest level you have opened, and the level you were on, come back with this copy of the app.
