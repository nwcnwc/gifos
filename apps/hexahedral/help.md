# Hexahedral

Push **every raised (pink) block down** before you run out of moves. You are the green cube. Pink tiles are up. Blue tiles are already down. Beige tiles are broken — you cannot stand on them.

Stepping onto a tile **toggles** it: pink becomes blue, blue becomes pink. You must step onto a neighbour (no diagonals, no jumping). Use all the pinks up and the level is done. Hit the move limit with pinks still up and the level restarts.

## Controls

- **Drag** the green cube toward a neighbour — it slides along the isometric faces.
- **Tap** a block next to you.
- **Arrows** follow the cube faces: up is the upper-right face, right is down-right, and so on.
- **U** undo. **R** restarts the level. The **Undo** / **Retry** labels do the same.
- On a phone a diamond pad appears after the first touch (↗ ↘ ↙ ↖), matching the four faces.

Easy, medium, and hard are ten levels each. Finish one and the next loads. You can jump to any level you have already reached.

## What is saved

The level you were on, the furthest you have opened, and your **best move count** on every level you have cleared. They come back with this copy of the app.
