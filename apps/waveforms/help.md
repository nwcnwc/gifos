# Waveforms

An **explorable** of sound waves. No prior knowledge needed. Nothing is uploaded.

## Walk through

**Next** and **Back** move through the guide. The graph on top is displacement over time — the same graph the original site used, drawn slow enough to see.

Phone Back also goes one step.

## Hear

**Hear** unmutes a bass tone. Tap again to mute. The graph is slow on purpose; the sound is about 100 times faster. The Hz badge is the audible pitch.

Volume starts muted. **M** mutes on a keyboard. Arrow keys step the guide.

## Controls

- **Amplitude** — how far the line moves from the middle. Loudness. 0 is silence.
- **Frequency** — how many times the shape repeats in the second the graph shows. Higher is a higher pitch.
- **Shape** — sine (pure), triangle, square, saw. Sine has no extra frequencies. The others add **harmonics**.
- **Harmonics** — how many extra sines to stack on a non-sine shape.
- **Converge** — blend those extras into the target shape. Turn it up to hear the square (or saw) assemble.

Tap the graph to pause it.

## Air

One step shows a grid of dots. Each dot is an air molecule wobbling in place. The wobble travels; the molecules do not fly across the screen.

## What is saved

Your step, sliders, shape, and volume stay in this app file.
