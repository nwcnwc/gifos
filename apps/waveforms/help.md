# Waveforms

An **explorable** of sound waves. No prior knowledge needed. Nothing is uploaded.

## Walk through

**Next** and **Back** move through the guide. The graph on top is the same waveform the original site used: displacement over time.

## Controls

- **Amplitude** — how far the line moves from the middle. That is loudness, more or less. 0 is silence.
- **Frequency** — how many times the shape repeats in the second the graph shows. Higher is a higher pitch.
- **Shape** — sine (pure), triangle, square, saw. Sine has no extra frequencies. The others add **harmonics**.
- **Harmonics** — how many extra sines to stack on a non-sine shape. Turn **converge** up to hear them become that shape.
- **Volume** — unmutes the tone. The graph is slow on purpose so you can see it; the sound is about 100 times faster (still a low bass). Press **M** to mute.

## Air

One step shows a grid of dots. Each dot is an air molecule wobbling in place. The wobble travels; the molecules do not fly across the screen.

## Phone

A thumb works on the sliders. Landscape gives the graph more room.

## What is saved

Your step, sliders, shape, and volume stay in this app file.
