# Vintage Poker

Texas Hold'em at one table. **Toy chips. No cash.** Nothing is wagered and nothing is paid out.

Each seated player gets **two hole cards**. Five community cards come out in three streets: the **flop** (three), the **turn** (one), the **river** (one). Best **five of those seven** wins the pot. Aces play high, and also low in a wheel straight (A-2-3-4-5).

## Blinds and streets

The dealer button moves each hand. The two seats left of the button post the **small blind (5)** and **big blind (10)**. Heads-up, the dealer posts the small blind and acts first before the flop.

After the blinds: **preflop**, then flop, turn, river. On each street you may **fold**, **check** (if nothing to call), **call**, or **raise**. A raise has to at least double the last bet. Going all-in is a call or a raise of whatever you have left.

Hands, strongest first: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair, high card.

## Play on this device

Two bots sit across from you. You start with **1000** chips. **Deal** starts a hand when it is idle. When it is your turn, the action buttons light up.

## Play with friends

Press **Play with friends**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app. One invite is the table. Two people have to be seated before the host can deal. The host (the first person) is the dealer. You act on **your** hole cards; everyone sees the board.

Up to six seats. Close the tab and your seat goes empty.

## What is saved

Your chip pile comes back with this copy of the app. A live table lives in the room while people are connected.
