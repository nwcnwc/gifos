# Vintage Poker

Texas Hold'em at one table.

Each seated player gets **two hole cards**. Five community cards come out in three streets: the **flop** (three), the **turn** (one), the **river** (one). Best **five of those seven** wins the pot. Aces play high, and also low in a wheel straight (A-2-3-4-5).

## Blinds and streets

The dealer button moves each hand. The two seats left of the button post the **small blind (5)** and **big blind (10)**. Heads-up, the dealer posts the small blind and acts first before the flop.

After the blinds: **preflop**, then flop, turn, river. On your turn you may **fold**, **check** (if nothing to call), **call**, or **raise**. A raise has to at least double the last bet. Going all-in is a call or a raise of whatever you have left. Unmatched chips come back; side pots pay the best hand that can claim them.

Hands, strongest first: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair, high card.

## Play on this device

Two bots sit across from you. You start with **1000** chips. **Deal** starts a hand when it is idle. Fold, Call, and Raise sit in the bar at the bottom — large enough for a thumb. If your stack is gone, **Top up 1000** restores your chips.

## Play with friends

Press **Play with friends**. Friends who open the invite sit automatically. Two people have to be seated before the host can deal. An empty table tells you so; a waiting table is waiting on the host, not on a lobby code. The host (the person who owns this copy) is the dealer. You act on **your** hole cards; everyone sees the board.

Up to six seats. Close the tab and your seat goes empty.

## What is saved

Your chip pile comes back with this copy of the app. A live table lives in the room while people are connected.
