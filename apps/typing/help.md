# Typing

A passage appears. Type it. Speed is the letters you got right, not the ones you mashed.

## Objective

Finish the line. **WPM** uses only correct letters (five letters count as one word). **Accuracy** sits beside it. A mash of wrong keys does not look fast.

**Practice** — English or Code, toggle at the top. **New** draws another passage.

**Lessons** — home row (left, right, both), then top row, bottom row, punctuation, numbers, mixed, and **Random keys**, the original never-ending drill of mixed letters, digits, and signs.

A lesson with **90%** accuracy or better is marked done.

## Controls

**Computer** — click the passage and type. **Backspace** fixes a miss. Enter and Tab do not insert a character.

**Phone** — tap the passage. The real keyboard opens. There is no fake QWERTY. The caret stays on the letter you are on, above the keys.

The clock starts on the first letter.

## Play a friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. You both get the same passage and a short countdown.

First to finish wins. If you both finish, the higher WPM takes it. Keep going after they finish — a clean fast line can still beat a sloppy first place. The next passage starts a few seconds later.

The shared passage follows the host's last English / Code choice from Practice. You cannot switch kinds mid-race.

## Scoring

Live WPM, accuracy, and time sit above the passage. At the end you see misses and how much of the line you got right.

## What is saved

Your last **20** runs and your best WPM stay in the file, on this device. Lessons you have cleared stay marked. A race does not. Nobody else keeps a copy.

An unofficial port of [Programmer's Typing Practice](https://github.com/climech/typing-practice). The original is a stream of random characters with no score. This version adds passages, lessons, a live result, and a race.
