# Slots

Five reels. A payline across the middle. Toy credits, **never cash**. Nothing is wagered and nothing is paid out.

Tap **Spin** (or the big lever) to pull. The reels take a second each, left to right. Autoplay keeps pulling after each stop.

## The payline

Only the **middle row** pays. Three of the same symbol in a row:

- any three — **10×** the 10-credit stake
- three **7**s — **50×**
- five of a kind across the whole row — **100×**

A miss costs the stake. Credits cannot go below zero: a broke machine still lets you pull, and a win starts you again from the stake.

## Play with friends

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Everyone who opens the link sees the **same reels**. Anyone at the table can pull. Credits stay on this device — the room shares the spin, not the purse.

## What is saved

Your credit pile and the last symbols on the reels come back with this copy of the app. A friend game’s live spin lives in the room while people are connected.
