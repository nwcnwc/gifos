# Slots

Five reels. A gold payline across the middle.

Tap **Spin** (or the lever, or the space bar) to pull. The reels take a second each, left to right. **Auto** keeps pulling after each stop. Back turns Auto off.

## The payline

Only the **middle row** pays. Matching symbols from the left:

- any three — **10×** the stake
- any four — **25×**
- five of a kind — **100×**
- three **7**s — **50×** · four 7s — **100×** · five 7s — **250×** (jackpot)

A miss costs the stake. Credits cannot go below zero: a broke machine still lets you pull, and a win starts you again. **Top up 1000 credits** appears whenever the pile dips under 100. Stake is 10, 25, 50 or 100.

## Sound

**Sound** / **Muted** toggles the clicks and the ding. Mute is saved in the file.

## Play with friends

Everyone who opens the link sees the **same reels**. Anyone at the table can pull. Credits stay on this device — the room shares the spin, not the purse. A line under the payline names who pulled.

## What is saved

Your credit pile, the stake, mute, and the last symbols on the reels come back with this copy of the app. A friend game’s live spin lives in the room while people are connected.
