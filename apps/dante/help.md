# Dante

You are a little red devil. Lucifer dropped a crate of evil souls in a corner of Hell that is still being built. Catch all **thirteen** and take them to the eighth circle.

## How you win

Walk the platforms, pull levers, ride the boats. Each soul you touch is caught. The counter in the corner reads **Souls: I / XIII** through **XIII / XIII**. When you have all thirteen, pull the boat lever and leave. There is no high score — thirteen is the whole game.

You cannot die. Fall in the lava and you come back at the last lever you pulled.

## On a computer

- **WASD** or the **arrow keys** walk.
- **E**, **Space**, **Enter**, or a **click** on the world pulls a lever (and calls a boat).
- **Esc** or the **☰** button opens the menu.
- **Play** is third person (camera behind you). **Play first person** looks with the mouse; a click on the canvas locks the pointer so you can look around. Esc unlocks it.
- A gamepad works too: left stick walks, right stick looks in first person, any face button pulls a lever, Start opens the menu.

## On a phone

- The **left half** of the screen walks (slide your thumb).
- A **tap** on the world pulls a lever.
- In first person, the **right half** looks around.
- After the first touch, a **LEVER** button sits at the bottom — same as pressing E.

## The menu

**Play** / **Play first person** start. **Restart** wipes this run and drops you back at the boat (it asks first). **Music: true/false** mutes Ryan Malm’s Beethoven. The opening lines on that screen are the original game’s own.

## Invite a friend

Playing alone is the original game.

**Invite** is in the bar **above** the app, not inside it. Anyone who opens the invite appears in the same Hell as a **ghost** — you see them on the platforms. Tap **N in Hell** in the corner for a roster of souls caught.

Each devil hunts **their own** thirteen. Levers you pull, souls you catch, and the boat you ride are yours. A ghost cannot collect for you, and you cannot collect for them. It is a race to thirteen, not one shared crate.

## What is saved

Your souls, levers, boat, and place in Hell stay **inside this app** on this device. Close it and open it later: you are where you left off. Restart from the menu is the way to begin again. Ghosts are not saved.
