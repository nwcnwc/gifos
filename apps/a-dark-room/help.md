# A Dark Room

You wake in a dark room. Light the fire. Keep it alive. A stranger will come who knows how to build. From there the village grows, a dusty path opens, and the world is a map of letters you walk with your thumbs.

This is an unofficial port of A Dark Room by Michael Townsend and doublespeak games.

## The room

- **light fire** costs 5 wood. **stoke fire** costs 1. The fire cools if you wait.
- When the builder arrives, **gather wood**. Buildings and crafts unlock as you go.
- The log on the left (above, on a phone) is the story. Read it.

## Later

A compass opens **A Dusty Path**. Pack meat, water, a weapon. **embark**. On the map you are `@`. Landmarks have letters. Walk with the **arrows** or **WASD**, or tap the map in that direction, or use the pad under the map on a phone.

Combat is a row of buttons. Eat meat. Use medicine. If you die you wake back in the room, supplies from the trip gone.

## Menu (bottom right)

**sound on/off**, **lights on/off**, **hyper** (double speed), **restart**, **save** (export or import a code from the original game).

## A shared fire

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Whoever opens the link sits at **your** fire. They can stoke, gather, and build. You keep the clock. Their copy of the village is the same one.

## What is saved

The fire, the stores, the village, the map, the ship — all of it stays in this copy of the app. Close it. Come back. Still stoking.
