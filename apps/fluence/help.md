# Fluence

A speech coach. You talk off the cuff for a minute or two, then you get numbers and honest notes on **how you actually spoke** — pace, fillers, pauses, hedges, restarts, and how varied your words were.

## What it is for

Practice speaking without a script. Use it before a talk, a meeting, or any time you blank mid-sentence. It is not a language course and it is not a live conversation partner.

## Before you record

You need two things in **GifOS Settings** (the OS, not a button in this app):

1. **Third-party APIs** — a Deepgram key, so the take can be turned into words with timings.
2. **AI models** — **Smartest text** to coach you. **Cheapest text** to invent drills. **Text → image** only if you pick a picture-description drill.

Keys stay in Settings. This app never sees them. If something is missing, Record pops a setup sheet instead of guessing.

The microphone is the OS recorder. This app never holds the live mic. Allow the mic when asked.

## Practice

The card is the drill. Default is **Freestyle** — a prompt, 90 seconds, no extra rules. **Choose a drill** opens nine types:

- **Freestyle** — open prompt, your baseline.
- **Semantic fluency** — name as many items in a category as you can in 60s.
- **Letter fluency** — words starting with one letter, 60s, no proper nouns, no same-root repeats.
- **Forced substitution** — speak without a list of banned words.
- **Bridging** — start on a given sentence, end on another.
- **Recast** — re-say a stumble from a past take, cleanly.
- **Bullet points** — four bullets, ~2 minutes, in order (you can type a topic).
- **Picture description** — describe a generated scene for 90s.
- **Topic switching** — three topics, 30 seconds each. Study them **before** you hit Record; the recorder covers the app while you talk.

**New prompt / Regenerate** rolls a fresh one. **Record a take** starts the OS recorder for that drill’s length; tap the recorder to stop early.

Study timed drills first. After the take you get a transcript, a score, strengths, work-ons, timestamped moments, and often a **suggested next drill**.

## Stats, History, Benchmarks

**Stats** — trends for fillers/min, words/min (a comfortable band is about 140–170), how varied your words are, long pauses, hedges. Windows: 7 / 30 / 90 days / all. Listing drills (letter / semantic) are left out so they do not distort the charts. **Review my week** looks across the last 7 days for patterns and one focus.

**History** — every take, by month. Open a row to read the transcript.

**Benchmarks** — the same prompt, re-recorded over time (aim for about once a month). Seed three starters, add your own, or save a take’s prompt from its coaching card. Each card charts fillers/min across runs.

## Private vs shared

Takes, weekly reviews, and benchmarks stay **on this device, in this app**. **Invite** in the bar above does not share them. A friend needs their own copy, their own keys, and their own mic.

## What is saved

Every finished take (prompt, transcript, numbers, coaching), each weekly review, and your benchmark list. Closing the app keeps them. There is no cloud copy of your speech.
