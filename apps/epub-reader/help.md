# EPUB Reader

Open an EPUB on this page. The last book you opened comes back the next time you open the app (if it fits — about 8 MB). Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Open a file

Press **Open**, or drag a `.epub` onto the page. A short public-domain book is already here the first time, so you can try turning pages before you pick a file of your own.

## Move around

- **Computer:** the arrows, Page Up / Page Down, and Home / End turn pages. `[` and `]` skip a chapter. `+` and `−` change the type size. `C` opens Contents. `F` opens Find.
- **Phone:** swipe left or right to turn pages. The buttons at the top do the same jobs with a thumb.

**Contents** lists every chapter. Tap one to jump. **A−** / **A+** make the type smaller or larger.

## Find

Press **Find** (or `F`). Type a word. Matches light up on the page. **Next** / **Prev** jump between them in the chapter you are in.

## A live friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. A friend who opens it sees the same book and the same page. When you turn a page, they turn. Press **Point**, then hold on a line — a pointer appears on theirs.

The person who opened the app is the one whose book is shared. A guest who opens a different file sees it only on their own device.

The host can switch the room between *everyone turns pages* and *only the host drives* using the control in the bar above.

## What is saved

The last book (when it fits), the page you were on, and the type size. Close it, come back, they are still there. Sharing the app file shares that saved reading too.
