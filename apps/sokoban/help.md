# Sokoban

You are in a warehouse. Push every box onto a marked gold spot.

## Objective

Park all the boxes. There are **fifty** rooms, easy first. A tick appears on a room you have already cleared.

## Controls

**Keyboard**

- **Arrows** or **WASD** — step (and push, if a box is in front)
- **U**, **Z**, or **Backspace** — undo one step
- **R** — restart this warehouse
- **N** — next warehouse (after you clear one, or skip ahead)

**Mouse** — the floor is not click-to-move. Use **Undo**, **Restart**, and the level list in the bar. **Next warehouse** is on the clear sheet.

**Phone / touch** — **swipe the floor**. A swipe that keeps going steps again every short distance. A **pad** of arrows appears the first time a finger lands; hold an arrow to keep walking.

## Rules

You can **only push**, never pull. You cannot push two boxes at once. You cannot walk through a wall or a box.

A box on a spot turns gold. The HUD counts **moves**, **pushes**, and **boxes on spots**. A move is any step; a push is a step that shoved a box.

Undo walks the history back, including boxes. Restart puts the room back to its first layout and zeroes the counts.

If you shove a box into a corner that is not a spot, that room may be impossible until you Undo or Restart. That is the puzzle.

## Scoring

Moves and pushes are the record for that clear — fewer is better, but any clear counts. There is no time limit and no stars.

## Solo vs a friend

On your own it is the original puzzle. Pick a level from the list whenever you like.

**Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar **above** the app (not inside it). You both get the **same warehouse**. Each of you pushes on your own floor — you do not see their boxes, only their **move counts** as they go. **First to park every box wins.** If two people finish in the same moment, fewer moves wins the tie, then fewer pushes.

**Next warehouse** (on the friend strip) starts the next room for everyone. **← Solo** puts you back on your saved game.

You can keep pushing while you wait for someone to open the link.

## What is saved

The warehouse you are in, the boxes as they sit, move and push counts, and which rooms you have cleared stay **in the file**. A live race is not. Close mid-race and solo picks up where you left that puzzle.
