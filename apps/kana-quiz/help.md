# Kana Quiz

Drill hiragana and katakana on this device. A kana, four sounds — tap the right one. Or flip it: the sound first, pick the kana.

## Start a drill

1. Pick **Hiragana**, **Katakana**, or **Both**.
2. Pick **Kana → sound** or **Sound → kana**.
3. Tap the rows you know. You see the actual あ か さ, not a list of names. Shortcuts: **Vowels**, **あ か**, **All 46**, **None**.
4. Dakuten, yōon, look-alikes, and extra katakana sounds sit behind **Dakuten, yōon, extras**.
5. **Start drill**.

If you left a solo drill unfinished, **Continue** picks up where you were. **New drill** shuffles a fresh deck from the same rows.

## Answering

Four huge choices. Right or wrong lands at once, with the correct pairing shown. A miss comes back later in this drill so it has to stick.

On a computer you can press `1` `2` `3` `4` for the four buttons, left to right. There is no typing box — it is always multiple choice. Mouse and finger are the same tap.

## Scoring

Each correct answer is one point. The bar is how far you are through the deck. At the end you see `score / cards`, and every miss as the kana plus its sound.

**Review misses** is a new deck of only those cards. They wait here until they stick.

## A live friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar **above** the app — that link is the room. You both get the **same** shuffled deck.

- **First to 10** — first to ten right wins (or fewer if the deck is smaller).
- **Whole deck** — whoever has more when the cards run out.

You only write your own score. Waiting for a friend shows in the lobby until someone opens the link. Misses do **not** requeue in a race — same cards for both of you, one pass.

Invite is OS chrome. This quiz does not draw its own share button.

## What is saved

Your row picks, direction, an unfinished solo drill, and the miss list live in this file. Close the app and come back: **Continue** is still there. A race lives in the room, not in the solo save.

Tables are the ones that shipped with [Kana Quiz](https://github.com/anzzstuff/kanaquiz) by anzzstuff. `し` is **shi** (and **si** still counts).
