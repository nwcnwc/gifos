How to play

  • Choose hiragana, katakana, or both, then the rows — あ-row, dakuten, yōon.
  • A kana appears. Tap the romaji. Or flip it: romaji first, pick the kana.
  • Right or wrong lands at once. Misses wait in Review.
  • To race a friend, use Invite in the bar above the app. Same shuffled deck — first to ten right, or whoever has more when the cards run out. You only write your own score.
