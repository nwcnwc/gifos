# Mykonos

A small island in the sun. White houses, a chapel with a blue dome, a windmill, olive trees, and a path down to the water. You land on the cobbles and walk.

This is a **walk**, not a builder. You cannot place or erase houses. The village is already here.

## Start

Press **Walk**. Sound is the ♪ in the corner; it remembers on this device.

## Keyboard and mouse

- **W A S D** or **arrow keys** walk.
- **Q** and **E** spin the island.
- **Drag** on the island to look around (yaw and tilt).
- **Scroll** the wheel to zoom.

Space does nothing. You cannot jump. Water is not a path — use the bridge.

## Phone

A real finger brings up two pads (a laptop with a touchscreen keeps the keyboard until then):

- **Left thumb** — the stick walks.
- **Right thumb** — drag to look around.
- **Pinch** on the right side to zoom.

## A friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Whoever opens the link appears on the same island, walking the same paths. There is no score and no lobby. The room is the link.

Each of you starts on a different cobble so you do not land on top of each other. Names show when more than one person is here.

## What is saved

Only whether sound is on. Your place on the island is not — close it, and you walk from a spawn again. Friends leave when they close the invite.

An unofficial port of [Mykonos Island Voxels](https://github.com/boona13/mykonos-island-voxels). The original is a builder. This copy is the walk around that village.
