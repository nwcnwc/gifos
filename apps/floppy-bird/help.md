# Floppy Bird

Tap to flap between the pipes. Last as long as you can. With a friend it is a race on the **same pipes**.

## Objective

Fly through each gap without hitting a pipe or the ground. One touch and that run is over — there are no lives.

## Controls

**Phone / touch**

Tap anywhere to flap. The first tap on the splash starts the run. After you crash, tap the replay badge to try again.

**Mouse**

Click anywhere — same as a tap.

**Keyboard**

**Space** flaps. On the scoreboard, **Space** is replay.

Do not pinch or try to pan. The course fills the window. The bird always flies forward; you only control height.

## Rules

Each flap kicks you up. Gravity pulls you down. The ceiling stops you from flying off the top — it does not end the run. Pipes and the ground do.

A run ends on the first hit. Then you see this score, your best, and a medal if you earned one.

## Scoring

Each pipe you pass is **1 point**.

Medals on the scoreboard:

- **10** bronze
- **20** silver
- **30** gold
- **40** platinum

Below 10 there is no medal. Your **best** is the highest solo score on this copy of the app.

## Solo vs a live friend

Alone this is the original tap-flap game. Close it and come back: the best score is still here.

To race: press **Invite** in the bar **above** this app, then send the link. There is no extra button inside the game. When someone is in the room:

- You both fly the **same pipes**.
- The other bird is a **ghost** — a tinted bird, a little ahead or behind.
- First to hit a pipe is losing; the one who flies **farthest** (most pipes) wins. A tie is a tie.
- If you crash first, tap to chase — they may still be in the air.

The race HUD along the top only appears when a friend is actually here. Playing alone never waits on a room.

## What is saved

Your high score stays in this app on this device. A live race is only while the room is open. The current run is not saved.
