# SkiFree

Point down the mountain. Dodge the trees. Crash, get up, go farther. Five skiers; when they are gone, that is how far you got.

## Objective

Ski as many metres down the slope as you can. Trees, rocks, jumps, thick snow, and the occasional snowboarder are in the way. Hit something and you eat snow — one skier gone. After about **2000 m**, something hungry starts hunting you.

Farthest distance wins. There is no clock and no finish line.

## Controls

**Mouse** — move the pointer where you want to go. The skier turns toward it.

**Keyboard**

- **Left / A** — turn (or step) west
- **Right / D** — turn (or step) east
- **Down / S** — point straight down
- **Up / W** — stop
- **F** — speed boost
- **T** — try a trick in the air (after a jump)
- **Space** — ski again after you wipe out

**Phone / touch** — drag on the snow to point. **Tilt** the phone left or right to steer (a finger on the slope always wins over tilt). **Double-tap** or hold **BOOST** to go faster. Space is a tap on the wiped-out sheet.

The BOOST button appears the first time a finger lands, so a laptop with a touchscreen is not cluttered until you actually touch.

## Rules

You start still, facing the camera. Point down to move. A crash spends one of five skiers and you keep going from there. When the last skier is gone, the run is over.

Jumps send you into the air. A trick is extra style, not extra metres.

The yeti only shows up once you have gone far enough. If you have not seen it, you have not gone far enough.

## Scoring

Metres down the mountain. The HUD shows the current run and **Best**. A ghost of that best run skis with you next time, so you can beat yourself.

## Solo vs a friend

On your own it is SkiFree, plus that ghost of your best.

**Invite** lives in the bar **above** the slope, not inside the game. A friend opens the link and you both ski **your own run**. They show up as a tinted **ghost** a little ahead or behind. Same trees are not shared — each slope is yours. **Farthest when everyone is out wins.** If you wipe out first, wait: the race is not over until the last skier is gone.

There is no account and no game server. The race is the link.

## What is saved

Your **best distance** and a short tape of that best run stay in the file. Lives, the current run, and a live race are not.
