# Backgammon

Fifteen checkers, two dice, a wooden table. Play the computer on this device, pass the phone, or send a link.

## New game

The first screen is setup, not the board.

- **Computer** — you pick white or black. White always goes first. The computer thinks **on this device**.
- **Two here** — pass the device. White goes first.
- **Play a friend** — then press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link.

**Start game** opens the table. A game you left unfinished comes back on its own.

## How a turn works

1. **Roll.** Two dice. Doubles are **four** moves of that number.
2. **Tap a checker**, then **tap where it goes**. Legal spots light up. Tap a die if you want to spend one of them first.
3. **Confirm** the turn. Until you do, **Undo** takes the checkers back. A roll with no legal move still needs Confirm.

Checkers move toward home (white's home is the bottom-right quadrant on the unflipped board). If you play black, the table **flips** so home is still in front of you.

A single checker on a point is a blot. Land on it and it goes to the **bar**. You must re-enter from the bar before anything else. Once every checker is in home, you **bear them off**. First to bear all fifteen wins.

There is **no doubling cube**. This is the general table the original shipped — not match play.

## Play a friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite**. The first two people take white and black (white still first). Anyone else who opens the link **watches**.

Roll, tap, confirm — same as solo. **Resign** appears after the opening. When the game ends, the next one starts on its own after a short pause. If someone leaves mid-game, the one who stayed wins.

## What is saved

The **local** game in progress (computer or two-here), including whose turn it is. Close it, come back, the checkers are where you left them. A live game with a friend lives in the room, not in the file.
