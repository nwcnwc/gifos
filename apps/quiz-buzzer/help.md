# Quiz Buzzer

A host puts a question on the big screen. Everyone else's phone is four huge coloured answers. Fastest **correct** buzz scores.

## Solo

**Drill the pack** — forty questions in science, geography, words, and numbers. Twelve seconds each. Tap one pad (or press `1`–`4` / `A`–`D`). Right or wrong, you see the answer, then **Next**.

**Type a question** — four answers, tap which colour is right. Tick **Keep in this file** if you want it next time. **My questions** drills only those.

## With friends

1. Press **Play with friends**.
2. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app — not inside this game. The people who open the link are the room.
3. Put the **host** device where everyone can see the question. The host is whoever the room picks (usually the person who opened the app). Their name in the list says **hosts**.
4. Host picks 5 / 10 / 20 / all 40 questions, and 10 / 15 / 20 seconds.
5. **Start the pack**, or type a custom question.

You need at least two people in the room to start.

Guests tap one of the four pads — that lock is their buzz. You cannot change it. The host does **not** buzz; their screen is the board.

When everyone has buzzed, or the clock hits zero, the answer lights up (the host can also press **Reveal**). First legal correct answer scores **one**. Wrong, late, and early are recorded and do not score. **Next**. At the end, the board.

## Scoring

One point per question, to the first correct buzz only. Same-instant ties go to the earlier player id. A round is the number of questions the host picked.

## What is saved

Pack progress (asked / correct / streak) and the questions you typed stay in this file, on this device. A live room is not saved.

Without a GifOS room, the solo pack still runs.
