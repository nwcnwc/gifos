Field

Press Invite (GifOS menu) to send a friend the link. You both get the same field. Change the recipe and press Apply — they see it.

    This toy needs a canvas.

    Tap a named field. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Edit the recipe and press Apply. Particles follow the arrows at every point.

    Unofficial port of Field Play by anvaka (MIT). Playing alone is the original toy. A shared field is the same recipe — nobody writes on anybody else’s row.
