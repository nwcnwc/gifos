# Cube Composer

Line up little **functions** until the coloured cubes match the picture on the right. You never type code. You tap functions.

## The loop

The big canvas is your cubes. The small canvas labelled **Goal** is the picture they should become.

- Tap a function under **Functions** to add it. The cubes change at once.
- Tap it again in **Your program** to take it out.
- Drag a function in **Your program** to change the order.

Each function may be used **once** per puzzle. Order matters: `stack` then `reject` is not the same as the other way round. Watch the cubes after every tap.

**Reset** empties your program. **Next level** (or **Next puzzle** in a race) moves on when you have a match. A **✓** in the level list marks a puzzle you have already matched.

Open **Tip** for a hint on this puzzle. Early chapters explain each new function there.

## Keyboard

- `R` — reset this puzzle
- `N` or `→` — next level
- `P` or `←` — previous level

On a phone there is no extra gesture: tap and drag are enough. There is no zoom; pinch does nothing. The canvases scale with the window.

## Chapters

Six chapters, **25 puzzles**. Skip around with the level menu — you are not locked in order.

Functions **map** every column, **filter** or **reject** some of them, **stack** a colour on top, **reverse** a column, merge equal neighbours, or **partition** the wall into two groups. Later chapters add a wildcard cube and number-stacks. The Tip names the one you need.

Difficulty is marked Easy / Medium / Hard on each puzzle. Hard ones have no Tip.

## A live friend

On your own this is the original puzzle. Progress lives in the file.

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. You both get the **same picture**. First to match it wins. If two people finish in the same moment, **fewer steps** wins.

You never see each other’s functions — only who finished, and how many steps they used. The level menu is locked for the race. **Next puzzle** starts the next round. **← Solo** returns you to your own save.

## What is saved

This file keeps the current puzzle, your program on every level, and which puzzles you have marked solved. A race scoreboard lives in the room for that invite, not in the file.
