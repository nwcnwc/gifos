# Crossword

Fill every white square. Across and down clues sit beside the grid. A letter that belongs to both a across and a down clue is the same letter.

## Controls

- **Tap** a square to select it. Tap it again to switch between across and down.
- **Type** a letter on a computer keyboard.
- On a **phone**, use the letter pad at the bottom.
- **Arrows / tab** move. Backspace clears.
- **Check word** marks wrong letters on the current clue.
- **Reveal letter** fills the current square.
- **Reset** clears the grid.

The baked puzzle is a 4×4: SAND / AREA / NEED / DADS, the same words down.

## A friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Everyone fills the **same** grid. Letters you type show up for them.

## What is saved

Your letters stay in the file. Close it and come back: the same squares are still filled.

Unofficial port of [crosswords-js](https://github.com/dwmkerr/crosswords-js) by Dave Kerr.
