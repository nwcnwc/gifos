# Crossword

Fill every white square. A letter that belongs to both an across clue and a down clue is the same letter.

There are **three** puzzles in this file:

- **Heart** — a 5×5 word square. The same five words read across and down.
- **Racecar** — a 7×7 of palindromes, with black squares.
- **Sand** — the original 4×4 (SAND / AREA / NEED / DADS).

## Controls

- **Tap** a square to select it. Tap it again to switch between across and down.
- **Type** on a computer keyboard, or on a **phone keyboard** (tap a square and the letters appear).
- A **letter pad** sits under the grid on a phone if you would rather tap keys there.
- The current clue is the line above the grid.
- **Arrows / tab** move. Backspace or **DEL** clears.
- **Check word** marks wrong letters on the current clue.
- **Check puzzle** marks the whole grid.
- **Reveal letter** fills the current square.
- **Reset** clears the grid you are on. The other puzzles keep their letters.

## A friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Everyone fills the **same** grid. Letters you type show up for them.

## What is saved

Your letters stay in the file, per puzzle. Close it and come back: the same squares are still filled.

Unofficial port of [crosswords-js](https://github.com/dwmkerr/crosswords-js) by Dave Kerr.
