# WiFi Card

Print or show a card with your WiFi name and password. A phone points its camera at the code and joins. Nothing is uploaded unless you press Invite.

## Make a card

1. Type the **Network name**.
2. Type the **Password** (at least 8 characters for WPA).
3. The code updates as you type.

**Encryption** is usually WPA/WPA2/WPA3. Pick **None** for an open network, **WEP** for an old one, or **WPA2-EAP** if you have a username (Identity) as well as a password.

## Options

- **Rotate** — tall card instead of wide. On a narrow phone the card starts this way.
- **Hide password** — the letters leave the card; the code still has them.
- **Hidden SSID** — for a network that does not broadcast its name.
- **Hide tip** — drops the “point your camera” line.
- **Number of cards to print** — 1 to 10 on one sheet.
- **Print** — opens this device’s print dialog.

## Invite

The last card stays on this device. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app to show the **same card** in a meeting. People who join see it and can point a phone at it. They do not type their own — the host’s card is the one shown.

The password rides with that card, so only Invite people you would hand the fridge card to.

## What is saved

Network name, password, and the options live in this file on this device. They come back the next time you open it. Nothing is sent anywhere until you Invite.

Unofficial port of [wifi-card](https://github.com/bndw/wifi-card) by bndw. English only.
