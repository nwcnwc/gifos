# Hextris

Rotate a hexagon so falling coloured blocks land on matching stacks. Match **three or more** of the same colour and they vanish. Keep every stack inside the outer grey hexagon — a stack that grows past that ring is the end of the game.

## Objective

Score by clearing blocks. Combos pay extra if you clear again before the coloured lines on the outer hex run out. Last as long as you can. On your own that is the whole game. With a friend it is a race from the **same sequence of falling blocks**, each of you on your own hex.

## Controls

**Keyboard**

- **Left / A** — rotate the hexagon one step counter-clockwise.
- **Right / D** — rotate one step clockwise.
- **Down / S** — hurry the falling block in (hold).
- **Enter** — start, or play again after a game over.
- **P** or **Space** — pause / resume.

**Mouse**

Click the left half of the field to rotate one way, the right half the other way. Click **Play!** in the middle to start. The pause and restart hexes sit in the corners.

**Phone / touch**

Tap the left or right side of the screen to rotate. **LEFT**, **RIGHT**, and **FAST** sit under your thumbs on a phone (or after a finger lands). There is no zoom and no pan.

## Rules

Blocks fall in from the six directions toward the centre hex. Rotate so a block lands on a stack of the same colour. Three or more touching blocks of one colour (across neighbouring sides as well as up a stack) clear.

The coloured lines on the outer hex are your **combo timer**. Clear again before they run out and the next clear is worth more.

The outer grey hexagon is the wall. If any stack sticks out past it, that game is over.

## Scoring

Each clear scores the square of how many blocks vanished, times the combo multiplier. A 3-block clear is 9; a 4-block clear is 16. Combos show as **x2**, **x3**… over the hex.

**HIGH SCORE** at the top is your best on this copy of the app. The game-over screen keeps your top three.

## Solo vs a live friend

Alone: tap **Play!** (or Enter). Your high scores stay.

To race: press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar **above** this app, and send the link. Invite is not a button inside the game. You can play while you wait — when they join, you both get the same blocks.

- Each of you plays **your** hex. Nobody rotates the other one.
- **Last one still stacking wins.** If both hexes fill, highest score wins. A tie is a tie.
- **Play again** starts a new round with a new sequence. **Solo** leaves the race.

This is friends, not a ladder.

## What is saved

Your **top three scores** stay in this app on this device. A live race is only while the room is open. Closing the app does not keep a game in progress.
