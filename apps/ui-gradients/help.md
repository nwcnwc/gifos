# uiGradients

A gallery of colour ramps. Flip through, copy a colour or the whole recipe, and keep favourites on this device.

## Browse

- The screen is the current ramp, full-bleed.
- **‹** and **›** step through the list. Arrow keys do the same: left and right to flip, up or down to turn the direction.
- **Show all** opens a grid. Type in **Find a name** to search. Colour chips along the top filter by family (Red, Blue, Hearts, and so on).
- Tap a card in the grid to make it the current ramp.

## Copy

- Tap a colour chip to copy that hex.
- **Copy recipe** copies CSS for the whole ramp, including a fallback colour. Enter does the same.
- **Turn** cycles the direction: right, left, down, up.

## Favourites

**♡ Favourite** hearts the current ramp. Hearts stay on this device — nobody else sees them, and they come back the next time you open this copy. In Show all, pick **♥ Hearts** to see only those.

## Share this pick

Playing alone is the original gallery. To look at the same ramp with a friend:

1. Press **Share this pick**.
2. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link.

You both start from this ramp. When anyone picks another, or turns it, everyone sees it. **← Solo** puts you back on your own gallery. Hearts never leave this device.

If you arrived through an Invite, you are already sharing.

## What is saved

The last ramp you were looking at, and your hearts, live in this file on this device. A shared pick lives only while the room is open.

Unofficial port of [uiGradients](https://github.com/ghosh/uiGradients) by ghosh (MIT).
