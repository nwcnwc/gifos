# Pixel Paint

A **32×32** pixel canvas. Draw a tiny sprite. Draw together. This is the small grid, not an animation studio — there are no frames.

## Draw

Tap or drag on the grid. On a phone, a finger paints; the page will not scroll while you draw. A mouse click-drags the same way.

Toolbar:

- **Pencil** (`P`) — paint the active color
- **Fill** (`B`) — flood a connected region
- **Eraser** (`E`) — back to empty
- **Selection** (`S`) — drag a box, then drag to move; Alt-drag copies; `Del` / Backspace deletes; `Esc` drops the selection
- **Color picker** (`V`) — tap a pixel to steal its color
- **Grid** (`G`) — show or hide cell lines
- **Undo** (`Z`) / **Redo** (`X`)
- **Save PNG** — download a picture of the canvas

Two color wells: tap one to make it active, tap a palette chip to set it. **C** swaps them. Palettes: **NES** or **Game Boy**. Hover **Shortcuts** (bottom of the stage) for the original key list.

**New picture** clears the canvas. When you are drawing together, it clears the **shared** board for everyone.

## Draw together

Press **Draw together**, then **Invite** in the GifOS menu, and send the link. Everyone paints the same 32×32. Strokes land live. **← Solo** leaves the room and restores the sprite you had before you joined.

Until a friend opens the link, you can still paint. Invite shares only the live board, not your private sprite.

## What is saved

Playing alone, the sprite stays on this device. Close the app, open it later, the pixels are where you left them. Draw-together does **not** overwrite that private picture.

There is no animation export. For frames and an animated GIF, use [Piskel](https://gifos.app/store/piskel).

## Phone vs mouse

The original stage is a fixed desktop layout; on a small screen it is scaled down so the tools still fit. Prefer a mouse for selection, copy, and paste. A finger is fine for pencil, fill, and eraser.

## Private vs Invite

Solo pixels stay here. Invite shares the 32×32 in the room. **Save PNG** is always a download to your files.
