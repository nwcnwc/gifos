# Math Race

An equation lands. Type the answer. First correct scores, and the next one is already there. Beat the clock.

## New race

Pick a time (**30s**, **60s**, or **90s**) and a level, then:

- **Practice** — you against the clock, on this device.
- **Play a friend** — then press **Invite** in the bar above the app. The link is the room. The host starts the round; the same equation lands on every phone at once. First correct scores.

Invite is in that bar, not inside this game.

## Levels

- **Easy +/−** — two numbers from 0 to 20, plus or minus (answers can be negative).
- **Medium ×** — times tables, 2 through 12.
- **Hard mix** — plus, minus, and times.

A wrong answer flashes and **stays on the same equation**. It does not skip you. Try again.

## Controls

**On a phone:** tap the number pad. `−` is the minus sign (only at the start of an answer). **DEL** rubs out. **GO** sends.

**Keyboard:** digits, minus, Backspace, Enter. Same rules.

As soon as you have typed enough digits for the answer, it sends itself — you do not always need **GO**. If the answer is `8`, the first digit you type is the whole answer.

## Versus

The host (the first person in the room) picks time and level and presses **Start round**. Live scores sit under the equation. When the clock hits zero, the round is over. The host can start another.

You only score if you are first and correct. A miss on your phone does not help or hurt anyone else.

## What is saved

Best scores for each time × level, and your last few runs, stay in this file. Mid-practice is not saved — leave, and that race is over. A friend round lives on the invite.

An unofficial port of [math-race](https://github.com/iloire/math-race).
