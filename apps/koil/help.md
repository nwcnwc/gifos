# Koil

A small tiled hall: mint walls, a checkerboard floor, a line of keys, a bomb. Walk, pick things up, throw the bomb. There is no score, no health, and the keys do not open anything — that is the game.

## Walk in

Press **Walk in**. The first tap also unlocks sound.

## Computer

- `W` / `↑` — forward
- `S` / `↓` — back
- `A` / `←` — **turn** left (not strafe)
- `D` / `→` — **turn** right (not strafe)
- `Space` — throw a bomb, if you are holding one

There is no mouse-look. Turn with the keys.

## Phone

After a real touch, on-screen sticks appear:

- **Left thumb** — walk: forward, back, **and** strafe
- **Right thumb** — drag to look
- **Throw** — same as Space

## Items

Walk into a floating **key** or the **bomb** to pick it up. Keys do nothing else. You hold one bomb at a time; **Throw** / `Space` tosses it in the direction you face. It bounces, rings off walls, and bursts into sparks. Bombs do not hurt anyone.

The hall is tiny — a handful of wall blocks, six things on the floor. Walk the corridor. That is the map.

## A live friend

**Invite** in the bar **above** the app is the room. Other people appear as silhouettes down the same hall. They pick up what you have not reached yet and throw bombs of their own. First pickup each client sees keeps the item. There is no lobby and no account.

Invite is OS chrome. This game does not draw its own share button.

Alone, the HUD says **Solo**. With a link open it counts the people in the hall.

## What is saved

Nothing. Keys and the bomb reset when you open the app again. A room lasts as long as people stay on the link.

An unofficial port of [Koil](https://github.com/tsoding/koil) by tsoding. The original talked to a game server on your machine. This copy does not: the people you invited are the whole network.
