# Yahtzee

Five dice and a scorecard. Thirteen turns. Fill every line. Highest total wins.

Unofficial. Not a Hasbro product.

## How a turn works

1. Press **Roll** (or tap the deck of cards) to throw all five.
2. Tap a die to **keep** it. Tap again to throw it next time.
3. You get **three rolls** a turn. After the last, pick a line on the card.
4. A line can be filled with 0 if the hand does not match — that is how you dump a bad roll.

On a computer, click the dice and the scorecard the same way. On a phone, use **Roll** and the hit targets on each die — the fan of cards is easy to miss with a finger.

## The card

**Upper** — Ones through Sixes, the sum of those faces. Reach 63 for a **+35** bonus.

**Lower**

- 3 of a kind / 4 of a kind — sum of all five, if you have that many alike
- Small straight 30 / Large straight 40
- Full house 25
- Chance — sum of all five, always
- Yahtzee (five alike) 50

A second Yahtzee, after you have already scored 50, adds **+100**. If the upper box for that face is still open, you must put it there (joker); otherwise any open lower box is legal.

## Solo vs a table

On your own, fill the card at your pace. **Best** in the corner is your high score.

**Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app. You each have your **own** card. You both roll **now** — nobody waits for a turn. Highest total when every remaining card is full. **Play again** starts the next round. **Solo** puts you back on the game you had before the table.

A friend who arrives mid-round starts a fresh card on that round. They are playing, not watching.

## What is saved

The solo game in progress and your best score live in this file. A live table is the room.
