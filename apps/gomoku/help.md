# Gomoku

Five in a row on a wooden **15-by-15**. Black and white take turns putting a stone on an empty **intersection**. First to get five in a line — across, down, or diagonal — wins. **Black goes first.**

## Play here

On the setup card pick:

- **Computer** — sit **Black** or **White**, at **novice**, **medium**, or **expert**. Expert thinks for a moment on its turn; wait for it.
- **Two here** — pass the device. Black still goes first.

**Start game** opens the board.

- Tap or click an empty point to place. Stones sit on the crossings, not in the squares.
- On a computer, hovering a point previews the stone.
- A mark on the last stone shows where it just landed.
- **Undo** takes back the last stone. Against the computer it also takes back the computer’s reply.
- **New game** returns to setup.

No capturing. Nothing is taken off the board. If every point is filled with no five, it is a draw.

## Phone

The board fills the width. Aim for the **dots where lines cross**. A tap that lands in a square, between lines, does nothing.

## A live friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the **bar above the app**. They sit the other colour on the same board. Extra people watch.

- You only place on your turn.
- **Undo** of the stone you just put down if they have not answered yet. Further than that, both of you have to tap Undo.
- **Resign** ends that game; the next one starts on its own.
- **Leave** returns to setup.

## What is saved

A local game in progress stays in the file — mode, colour, level, and every stone. Close it and come back: the board is still there. A finished game is not restored. A live match is the room, not the file.

Unofficial port of [HTML5 Gomoku](https://github.com/yyjhao/HTML5-Gomoku) by yyjhao. The computer is the same one that shipped with that game.
