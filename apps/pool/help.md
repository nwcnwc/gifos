# Pool

Eight-ball. Pot your colours, then the black.

## Objective

Fifteen coloured balls, the black in the triangle, the white on its spot. The first colour you pot legally is yours for the rest of the frame — **red** or **yellow**. Clear those seven, then pot the black to win. Pot the black too soon, or on a foul, and you lose.

## How to shoot

The white goes the way you **pull away from it**.

1. Press near the white.
2. Drag **away** from the shot you want. Farther back is more power. The stick follows.
3. Let go to shoot.

A dashed **ghost** line is the path. A ring marks the first ball you will hit. A second faint ghost shows where that ball wants to go. The power bar fills as you pull.

**On a phone:** same pull-back. Let go to shoot. After a foul, drag the white somewhere legal, then let go to place it. On a tall phone the table turns so the long side fills the screen — you stand at the white, the rack is up the felt.

**On a computer:** pull back with the mouse the same way, or hold `W` to add power and `S` to take it off, then click. After a foul, move the white and click to place it. `Esc` returns to the menu.

A tap with no pull does nothing. You have to draw the stick back.

## Whose turn

The name that lights up at the top is the person who shoots. Once colours are set, the hint says **You are red** or **You are yellow**. In a room, it says **Their turn** when it is not you — wait. You cannot aim while the balls are moving.

## Rules

- Once you have a colour, the white must hit one of yours first. Otherwise it is a foul.
- Pot one of yours and you stay on the table.
- Miss, pot the white, hit theirs first, or put the black in too soon: foul, and the other player places the white.
- Pot one of theirs and they get that ball, and it is their turn.

## Solo vs a friend

**Play the computer** — then Easy, Medium, or Hard. It tries a handful of shots and picks the best.

**Two on this device** — pass the screen and take turns.

**Invite** in the bar above the app — a friend takes the other turn on their own phone or computer. You go first (the host, who opened the app). They join, a fresh frame starts. They leave, you are back at the menu. A third person can watch; they do not get a stick.

There is no game server. The host runs the balls; the friend sends the shot.

## What is saved

Whether sound is on stays in this file. Frames, wins, and the table are not.
