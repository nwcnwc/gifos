# Longwave

One of you sees a mark between two opposites and gives a clue. The other slides the needle to where that clue belongs. Closer is better.

## Two ways to play

- **Two here** — pass this device. The psychic looks at the mark, types a clue, then hands the phone over. The guesser never sees the mark.
- **Play a friend** — press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app. The link is the room. One of you is the psychic, the other is the guesser. Extra people can watch.

Invite is in that bar, not inside this game.

## A round

Seven cards. After each card you swap seats.

1. The psychic sees a gold **4** sitting somewhere on the line. They type one thought that lives right there — a word or a short phrase. No numbers. No pointing at the rail.
2. The guesser sees the two poles and the clue, not the mark. They slide the needle, then lock it.
3. The mark is revealed. You score, then go on.

**Skip this card** redraws the line if the psychic cannot think of a clue.

## The line

The rail is twenty-one ticks, from the left pole to the right. Drag the knob with a mouse or a finger. There are no arrow keys for the needle; type the clue in the box (Enter sends it).

The gold band labelled **4** is the mark. The **3** and **2** bands sit one and two ticks either side.

## Scoring

You score together.

- Dead on the **4**: you count **3** points and **keep the card** (it does not spend one of the seven).
- One tick off (**3**): three points, spend a card.
- Two ticks off (**2**): two points, spend a card.
- Further: nothing, spend a card.

Seven spent cards and the game is over. Best cooperative score stays on this device.

## What is saved

Your best score, and an unfinished **Two here** game, stay in this file. Close it and **Continue this game** is waiting. A live friend game is the invite — leave, and that board is gone.

An unofficial port of [Longwave](https://github.com/cynicaloptimist/longwave).
