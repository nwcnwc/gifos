# Hanzi Writer

Trace Chinese characters in stroke order, on this device. A square, your finger (or a mouse). Draw the strokes in the right order.

## Start

1. Pick **HSK 1**, **HSK 2**, or **HSK 3**. Review is only the characters you missed.
2. **Outline** shows a faint ghost of the character. Turn it off to make it harder.
3. **Pinyin first** shows the reading before you finish. Otherwise it appears after the last stroke.
4. **Start tracing**. **Jump to** a character you type, if it is in this file.

If you left a solo drill unfinished, **Continue** picks up. **New drill** shuffles a fresh deck.

## Tracing

Draw each stroke. Right or wrong is judged as you lift. Miss the same stroke three times and it highlights the correct one.

**Watch** plays the character, then you trace. **Retry** starts this character over. **Skip** leaves it for review.

On a computer, a mouse drag is the same as a finger. There is no typing box.

## Scoring

A **clean** trace is every stroke first time. The chip is how many clean traces you have. At the end: clean / completed, and every miss as the character plus its reading.

**Review misses** is a new deck of only those.

## A live friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar **above** the app. You both get the **same** shuffled deck and the **same** character at the same time. First to ten clean traces wins.

You only write your own score. Waiting for a friend shows in the lobby until someone opens the link.

## What is saved

Your level, outline, pinyin setting, an unfinished solo drill, the miss list, and how many times each character was clean. Close the app and come back: **Continue** is still there.

HSK 1–3 (618 characters) plus 永. Stroke data from Hanzi Writer / Make Me a Hanzi.
