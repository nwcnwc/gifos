# Mini Photo Editor

Crop, rotate and filter a photo on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Load a picture

- **Take photo** — one still, not a live camera.
- **Open** — pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP from this computer.
- Put a picture on the window, or paste one.

## The loop

1. Drag a **corner** of the white box to crop. Drag inside the box to move it. The rest of the picture stays dimmed, so you can see what you are cutting.
2. **1:1 / 4:3 / 3:4 / 16:9 / 9:16** lock the box. **Free** lets you pull any corner.
3. **Rotate ←** and **Rotate →** turn it in 90° steps. **Flip** mirrors it.
4. Tap a look: **None**, **Grey**, **Vintage**, **Polaroid**, **Kodak**, **Browni**.
5. Turn **Bright**, **Contrast**, **Sat**, **Warmth**, **Vignette**.
6. **Download** writes a JPEG on this device. **Reset** clears the recipe and the crop.

This is not a drawing app (that is Paint) and not a compressor (that is Squoosh). It is the crop / rotate / filter loop.

A red note appears if the file is not a picture, or if taking a photo is cancelled or refused.

## What is saved

The last picture and the last recipe (look + sliders + crop) stay in this file on this device. Close it, open it later, they are still there.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app to show the same look, read-only, in a meeting. They get your sliders and look, not your photo. Each person opens their own picture.

## Phone

Thumbs work on the corners and the sliders. **Take photo** is the easy path.

Unofficial port of [mini-photo-editor](https://github.com/xdadda/mini-photo-editor) by xdadda. The looks Polaroid / Kodak / Vintage / Browni are their MTX filters.
