# Mini Photo Editor

Crop, rotate and filter a photo on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

## The loop

1. Press **Open** and pick a picture from this computer.
2. Drag on the picture to crop. The box you draw is the keep.
3. **Rotate ←** and **Rotate →** turn it in 90° steps. Crop resets after a turn.
4. Tap a look: **None**, **Grey**, **Vintage**, **Polaroid**, **Kodak**, **Browni**.
5. Turn **Bright**, **Contrast**, **Sat**, **Warmth**, **Vignette**.
6. **Download** saves a JPEG on this device. **Reset** clears the recipe and crop.

You can open another picture at any time. The last recipe (look + sliders) comes back the next time you open the app. The photo itself is not stored in the file — open it again.

This is not a drawing app (that is Paint) and not a compressor (that is Squoosh). It is the crop / rotate / filter loop.

## A live friend

Working alone is the original tool.

Want a friend using the same recipe? Press **Play together**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app. They get your sliders and look, not your photo. Each person opens their own picture.

**← Solo** puts you back on the original tool.

## What is saved

The recipe lives in this file. The picture stays in memory on this device until you open another one. A live share is the room for that invite, not a second save.
