# Civiclock

You are mayor of a small land that **ticks**. Paint zones, keep the lights and the taps on, and people show up because the city works. They leave when it does not. The city lives in this file. There is no account and no server.

## Land

The river is on the west. You cannot build on water. **Pumps** must sit on a tile that touches the river.

## Tools

Scroll the bar at the bottom.

- **Pan** — drag the map. Two fingers pan and pinch on any tool. Mouse wheel zooms.
- **Look** — tap a lot. It tells you why it will not grow, or why people left.
- **Road** — people and services follow roads. A zone only grows if a road touches it.
- **Homes / Shops / Works** — paint lots. Homes want jobs. Works want workers. Shops want customers. The three bars under the date are that demand.
- **Plant** — 80 power. Power follows roads, lines, and buildings from the plant.
- **Pump** — 55 water, only next to the river. Water follows the road.
- **Park** — raises land value around it (homes like this; works next door do not).
- **Line** — carries power across grass. Roads already carry power.
- **Clear** — bulldoze back to grass.

Drag to paint a strip. Keyboard: `R` road, `H` homes, `S` shops, `W` works, `P` plant, `U` pump, `B` clear, `I` look, `V` pan, space pause.

## Time and money

▶ is one month about every second. ▶▶ is faster. ❚❚ freezes the months (the clock of the day still holds still too). Each month: buildings grow or abandon, people are counted, rates come in, plants and pumps and parks and roads are paid. **Budget** sets the tax (0–20%). High tax empties lots. A negative treasury that lasts will shut the plants off.

Day turns to night on its own. Occupied, powered buildings light their windows. Dark windows with a red pip mean no power.

## How to wreck it (and how to read it)

Bulldoze the plant. Raise tax to 20%. Cut the road between homes and works. Watch the population fall and lots go boarded. **Look** is how you learn: no road, no power, no water, no demand, low land, abandoned.

## With a friend

Press **Invite** in the bar **above** this app — Civiclock does not draw a share button. Whoever opens the link is co-mayor on the **same** land. The owner’s device runs the months; everyone can paint. Cursors of other mayors show on the map.

## What is saved

The whole city (streets, money, month, tax) stays in this file, on this device. Share the GIF and the city goes with it. A live invite is a second way to share: only while people are connected.

Without GifOS, the city still ticks in the tab, but a refresh starts a new land.
