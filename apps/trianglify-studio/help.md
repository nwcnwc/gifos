# Trianglify

A low-poly triangle wallpaper generator. Pick a palette, type a seed, download a PNG or SVG.

## The wallpaper

The picture fills the top of the app. It is made of triangles, coloured from a named palette. The same **seed** always paints the same triangles — type a word you can remember, or press **New seed** to roll one (a word and a number, like `harbor-17`).

**Shuffle palette** jumps to a different named palette and keeps your seed, so the mesh stays and the colours change.

## Controls

- **Mesh** — how chunky the triangles are. Left is finer, right is coarser.
- **Scatter** — how far the points wander. Zero is a regular grid; higher is more organic.
- **Linear / Sparkle / Shadows** — how colour is laid across the mesh. Linear is a smooth wash. Sparkle jitters neighbouring triangles. Shadows darken some cells.
- **Wire** — outline only, no fill.
- **Size** — the pixel size of a download. What you see is the same picture, fitted to the window.

There is no extra keyboard command. On a phone, tap; on a computer, click. Drag the sliders live.

## Download

**PNG** and **SVG** write the wallpaper at the size you picked — HD, QHD, 4K, phone, square, or wide. Nothing is uploaded. You own the picture you make.

## Share the wallpaper

Playing alone is the original generator. The last palette and seed stay in this file.

Want a friend looking at the same wallpaper? Press **Share the wallpaper**, then **Invite** in the bar above the app, and send the link. You both start from this seed. When anyone rolls a seed, picks a palette, or drags a slider, everyone gets the new picture.

**← Solo** puts you back on your own copy with the wallpaper you left.

## What is saved

The last palette, seed, mesh, scatter, look, wire, and size live in this file. Close it, come back, the wallpaper is still there. A live share is the room for that invite, not a second save.
