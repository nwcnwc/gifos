# Racer

A sunset highway. Drive. Do not leave the road.

## Objective

Stay on the tarmac, pass traffic, finish a lap. Speed is on the dash; so is this lap, and the best you have done on this device.

Off the road you slow down. Curves pull you sideways — lift or steer into them.

## Computer

- **← →** or **A** / **D** steer
- **↑** or **W** accelerate
- **↓** or **S** brake

A key is a snap left or right. Tap the start card, or press a key, to dismiss it.

## Phone

After the first touch:

- **Left** thumb on the pad steers (slide for how much — it is analog)
- Hold **GO** on the right to accelerate
- Hold **BRAKE** to slow down

## Driving vs a race

On your own you are just driving. Traffic already takes the racing line. Finish a lap and your time can become Best.

A race with a friend is a separate thing:

1. Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. The same road has everyone else on it as cars you can see and pass.
2. Only the **host** (who opened the app) sees **Start race**. They tap it.
3. A three-count, then you all leave the line. **One lap.** Finish times land on the board.

Until the host starts a race you are just driving, even if a friend is already a car on the road. A guest cannot start the race.

Comfortable for a handful of friends, not a stadium.

## What is saved

Your best lap on this device stays in this file. A live race is not.
