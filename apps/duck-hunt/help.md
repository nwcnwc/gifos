# Pond Hunt

Click the ducks before they fly away. Three shots a wave. Miss too many and the dog laughs.

This is an unofficial remake based on DuckHunt JS. It is not a cabinet or a licensed console game.

## Objective

Waves of ducks. Shoot the quota (about six in ten) to go on. Later waves are faster and thicker. Fail a round and the run ends.

## Controls

- **Click** or **tap** a duck to shoot. The cursor is the zapper. On a phone a tap has a little slop so a thumb still counts.
- You have **three shots** each wave (more on later waves). Empty them and anything still up flies off.
- **Pause** and **Mute** sit in the corner — or `P` and `M` on a keyboard.

## Play with a friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. A friend who opens the link sits their own pond on their device — two zappers, two dogs. Each best score is written onto the pond board.

## What is saved

Your **best** and **last** run stay in this file. A live wave does not. Close it, come back, Play.
