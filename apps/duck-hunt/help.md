# Duck Hunt

Click the ducks before they fly away. Three shots a wave. Miss too many and the dog laughs.

This is an unofficial remake of the 1984 zapper game, based on DuckHunt JS. It is not the original cabinet or console game.

## Objective

Waves of one or two ducks. Shoot the quota (about six in ten) to go on. Later waves are faster. A round you fail ends the run.

## Controls

- **Click** or **tap** a duck to shoot. The cursor is the zapper.
- You have **three shots** each wave. Empty them and anything still up flies off.
- **P** pauses. **M** mutes. **F** tries to fill the screen.

On a phone it is the same tap. There is no aim-assist and no gyro.

## Play with a friend

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. A friend who opens the link sits their own pond on their device — two zappers, two dogs. Scores stay on each device. There is no game server.

## What is saved

Your best run stays in this file. A live wave does not.
