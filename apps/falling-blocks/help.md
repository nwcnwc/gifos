# Falling Blocks

Stack falling shapes in a 10-wide well. Fill a row and it vanishes. The pile grows; you lose when a new shape has nowhere to sit.

## Objective

Clear rows. Last as long as you can. On your own that is the whole game. With a friend it is a race from the **same sequence of shapes**, each of you on your own board.

## Controls

**Keyboard**

- **Left / Right** — slide. Hold to keep sliding.
- **Down** — nudge down (soft drop). Hold to fall faster.
- **Up**, **Z**, or **X** — turn the shape.
- **Space** — drop it all the way.

**Mouse**

The well is not a click game. Use the keys. Click **New game**, **Play a friend**, **Play again**, or **Solo**.

**Phone / touch**

The first tap on the well shows a pad along the bottom: slide, turn, nudge down, **Drop**. You can also swipe the well itself: sideways to slide one cell at a time, a short swipe down to nudge, a long swipe down to drop, a tap or swipe up to turn.

There is no zoom and no pan. The well is the whole playfield.

## Rules

Seven shapes. A faint outline shows where the current one will land. **Next** shows the shape after this one. There is no hold / swap piece.

Every **10 lines** you go up a level and pieces fall faster.

## Scoring

Clearing 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 rows at once scores **100 / 300 / 500 / 800**. Soft drop does not add points. There are no lives — one filled well is the end of that game.

**Best** is your highest solo score on this copy of the app.

## Solo vs a live friend

Alone: **New game** starts a fresh well. Your **Best** stays.

To race: press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar **above** this app, and send the link. Invite is not a button inside the game. You can play while you wait — when they join, you both get the same shapes.

- Each of you plays **your** board. Nobody moves the other well.
- **Last one still stacking wins.** If both wells fill, highest score wins. A tie is a tie.
- **Play again** starts a new round with a new sequence. **Solo** leaves the race.
- Someone who joins mid-round starts from the **opening** shapes of that round, on an empty well — they are racing, not watching your pile.

This is friends, not a ladder. There is no pause.

## What is saved

Your **Best** score stays in this app on this device. A live race is only while the room is open. Closing the app does not keep the current well.
