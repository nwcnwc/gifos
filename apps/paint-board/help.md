# Paint Board

A cream page and a strip of wild brushes. Draw on your own, or press **Draw together** and paint on one board with a friend.

## Brushes

Tap a brush, a colour, and the **Width** slider, then drag on the page.

- **Basic** — a plain round stroke.
- **Rainbow** — the hue walks as you go.
- **Stars** — a scatter of filled stars.
- **Crayon** — several shaky, faint lines.
- **Pixels** — a spray of little squares.
- **Stripe** — parallel bands of your recent colours.
- **Web** — a line with threads back to earlier points.
- **Mesh** — a net that knits nearby parts of the stroke.
- **Dots** — clusters of dots, linked along the path.
- **Wave** — alternating arcs.
- **Thorn** — stretched petals along the line.
- **Eraser** — punches holes in what is already there.

Colours are the swatches. Rainbow, stars, pixels and stripe also use the last few colours you picked.

## Controls

- **Mouse:** click and drag on the paper. Left button.
- **Phone / touch:** one finger paints. The page will not scroll while you draw.
- **Keyboard:** none for drawing. Use the buttons.
- **Undo / Redo** — step through strokes **while you are solo**. In a shared board they do nothing; wait for the next line or **New picture**.
- **Save PNG** — downloads a picture named `paint-board.png` to this device.
- **New picture** — clears the page.

## Solo vs a live friend

Alone, every stroke is kept in **this file**. Close the app, open it later, the picture is still there.

Press **Draw together**, then press **Invite** in the bar **above** the app (GifOS menu) and send the link. Invite is not a button on this page.

- Everyone paints on the same board, at the same time. There are no turns.
- Each person draws their own strokes; they land for everybody.
- You can paint while you wait for a friend.
- **← Solo** leaves the room and puts your private picture back.
- **New picture** in a shared board wipes it for everyone.

The shared board is not your solo save. Leaving does not copy the group picture onto your private page.

## What is saved

The solo picture lives in this file (private). A PNG you export is a snapshot you keep wherever your browser downloads.

A live board lives in the room. Oldest strokes drop off after a few hundred, so a very long jam will forget the beginning.

An unofficial port of [paint-board](https://github.com/LHRUN/paint-board) by LHRUN. Bugs go to GifOS, not upstream.
