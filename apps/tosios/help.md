# TOSIOS

A stone dungeon from above. A round fighter with a staff. Three hearts.

## Objective

Walk, aim, shoot. Red bottles on the floor give a heart back if you have room. Bats patrol the halls while you are alone. Send the link and the bats scatter — the other people are the fight.

You drop in looking down. There is no lobby and no round clock. A life lost respawns you in a few seconds. Kills and deaths sit on the scoreboard.

## Controls

**Computer**

- **W A S D** or the arrow keys to walk
- Mouse to aim
- Click or **Space** to shoot
- Hold **Tab** for scores

**Phone**

- Left thumb walks (the stick)
- Right thumb aims; holding that side of the screen fires
- **FIRE** also shoots
- Tap the tally at the top for scores

Shots take a moment to recharge. Walls stop you and stop bullets.

## Rules

Three hearts. A hit takes one. At zero you go down, then come back with three. Names sit over heads. Each person has their own colour.

Three red bottles sit in the dungeon. Walk onto one to drink it. Once taken, it stays taken for everyone. They do not come back.

The dungeon is the same every time you open the app.

## Solo vs a friend

Alone, three bats hunt you. Killing a bat counts as a kill.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. Everyone who opens it lands in the same dungeon. The bats leave the moment a second person is present — that is the game, not a glitch. There is no team mode. The scoreboard is kills and deaths, and it does not end a round.

## What is saved

Nothing lasting. Close the app and you start again, three hearts, empty scoreboard. The room is the people who still have the link open.

An unofficial port of [TOSIOS](https://github.com/halftheopposite/TOSIOS). The original needs a game server. This copy does not: the meeting is the room.
