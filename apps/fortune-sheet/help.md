# Fortune Sheet

A spreadsheet on this device. Cells, formulas, formatting, freeze, sort, filter, comments, charts, and as many sheets as you need. Your numbers stay on this device.

## What it is for

Budgets, rosters, grade books, trackers — anything you would put in a grid. It opens on a small **demo sheet** the first time so you can see a formula working (`=SUM(...)`). After that it is yours.

## Jobs

- Type in a cell; press **Enter** or click away.
- Formulas start with `=`. Use the formula bar under the toolbar, or type in the cell.
- Toolbar: font, fill, borders, merge, align, wrap, number formats, freeze, sort, filter, comments, charts, and more.
- Sheet tabs along the bottom: add, rename, colour, reorder.
- **New workbook** (top right) wipes the current grid and starts blank — it does not uninstall the app. Confirm first.

## Moving around

**Mouse** — click a cell, drag to select, scroll the grid. Double-click to edit.

**Keyboard** — arrows move; **Enter** confirms; **Tab** next cell; type to overwrite; `=` to start a formula.

**Phone / touch** — tap a cell to select, tap again to edit. Scroll the grid with one finger. Zoom is the toolbar’s zoom control, not a pinch of the page. The toolbar is dense on a small screen; landscape helps.

There is no separate pan tool. If a sheet is larger than the window, you scroll it.

## Private vs shared

This workbook is **private**. **Invite** in the bar above does not show your numbers to a guest. To give someone a sheet, they install their own copy and type (or you hand them a copy of this app file). There is no live co-editing.

There is also no Excel / CSV import or export in this copy. What you type lives here.

## What is saved

Every edit is kept a moment after you make it. Close the app and come back: the grid, sheets, freeze, filters, comments, and zoom are where you left them. **New workbook** replaces that saved grid. The OS **Save** in the bar above is the same file — you do not have to press it for ordinary typing.
