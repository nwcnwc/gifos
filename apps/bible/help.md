# Bible

Read the Bible in **139 translations across 63 languages** — every one in the
public domain, every one yours to keep. Two English translations (the World
English Bible and the King James Version) are inside the app from the first
moment; any other translation downloads once when you first open it and then
works offline forever.

## Reading

- **The reference at the top** opens the books — Old Testament, New Testament,
  and the Apocrypha where a translation carries it. Tap a book, tap a chapter.
- **The name beside it** picks translations. Put a second or third translation
  **beside** the first to read them in parallel columns — even across
  languages. Translations that number chapters differently (Greek and Hebrew
  psalters) are lined up for you, and the app says so when the numbering
  differs.
- **‹ and ›** at the bottom turn chapters. On a keyboard, the arrow keys do.
- The **magnifier** searches the whole text of the current translation, or
  jumps straight to a reference — try `John 3:16`, `Ps 23`, `1 Cor 13:4-7`.

## Studying

Tap any verse:

- **Highlight** it in six colours — and rename the colours to match your own
  system (Reading settings → Highlight names).
- **Note** — write in the margin. **Voice note** — speak into it instead; the
  recording is kept with the verse.
- **Compare** — the same verse in every translation you have on this device,
  side by side.
- Small **\*** and **†** marks in the text are the translators' own footnotes
  and cross references — tap them.

## Reading plans

The plan button at the bottom starts a reading plan and tracks your days.
Miss a day and the plan simply offers the next unread day — no guilt.

## Read aloud

"Read aloud from here" on any verse reads the chapter to you, following along
verse by verse. It uses the text-to-speech voice set up in GifOS — an offline
voice from the App Store works and nothing leaves your device.

## Reading together

Invite someone and you read the same page: when either of you turns a chapter,
the other's banner offers to follow. Your highlights and notes stay yours —
only the place you are reading is shared.

## Your data

Highlights, notes, voice notes and plans are saved on this device, inside the
app. There is no account and no cloud. **Save** keeps them in the file itself —
hand that file to anyone and your whole marked-up Bible opens on their screen.
