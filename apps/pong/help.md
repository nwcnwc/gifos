# Pong

A table-tennis rally. Keep the ball in play. First to **nine** wins.

## Controls

**Keyboard, left paddle:** `Q`, `W`, or ↑ to go up. `A`, `S`, or ↓ to go down.

**Keyboard, right paddle** (two of you on this screen): `P` up, `L` down.

**Mouse:** drag on the court. The paddle follows, and a moving swipe puts english on the ball the way a moving paddle does.

**Phone:** drag your paddle on the court, or hold ▲ / ▼ under the table. Those arrows sit in the letterbox so they never cover the rally.

Tap the court, or press `1`, Space, or Enter, to start. `Esc` quits. The ♪ button mutes the beeps.

## Rules

The ball speeds up as the rally goes on. It bounces off the top and bottom. Miss your end, and the other side scores a point. First to nine is the winner.

## Solo vs a friend

On your own, the right paddle is a computer. It aims better when it is behind and sloppier when it is winning.

Two of you on **this** screen: press `2` before the rally. You take left (`Q`/`A`), they take right (`P`/`L`).

A friend on **another** phone or computer: press **Invite** in the bar above the app. They take the right paddle. You stay left. Names at the top tell you who is who. A third person who opens the link can watch; they do not get a paddle.

When a friend joins, the score resets. When they leave, the computer takes the right paddle back. There is no game server.

## What is saved

Wins against the computer, and whether sound is on, stay in this file. Close it, come back: the count is still there. A live rally is not saved.
