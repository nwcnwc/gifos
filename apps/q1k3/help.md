# Q1K3

A tiny Quake. You wake in a brown stone hall with a shotgun. Two maps. Clear them.

## Objective

Find keys, open doors, kill what is waiting around the corner — grunts, ogres, a hound that runs at you. Pick up a **nailgun** and **grenades** if you live long enough. Two halls, then the end.

## Computer

- **WASD** walk
- **Mouse** look (the pointer locks to this window)
- **Click** fire
- **Right-click** or **Space** jump
- **Q** / **E** or the **scroll wheel** switch weapon
- Hold **Tab** for the scoreboard when friends are in
- **Enter** or **Space** on the start card to begin

Mouse speed and invert sit on the start card. If this window cannot lock the pointer, the view will not turn with the mouse.

## Phone

- Left thumb **walks** (a half-tilt is a walk)
- Right thumb **looks** (drag)
- A quick tap on the look side **shoots**
- **FIRE**, **JUMP**, and **GUN** on the right
- The game fills the screen in landscape

## Continue

Close it mid-map and come back: **Continue** starts in the hall you reached. **From the start** begins again.

## Solo vs friends

On your own this is the original campaign.

Press **Invite** in the bar above the app. Extra people appear in the same halls. They look like the monsters already in the game, and you **can shoot them** — they can shoot you. Hold **Tab** (or tap the “in the halls” tally on a phone) for kills and deaths. This is not a co-op campaign and not a tournament. There is no game server.

## What is saved

Look speed, invert, and which map you reached stay in this file. Health, ammo, and a live fight do not.
