# SVG-Edit

A vector drawing editor. What you draw is real SVG — paths, shapes, text — saved on this device inside this app. Send **Invite** and everyone who opens the link draws on the **same picture**.

## Draw

Tools sit on the left:

- **Select** — click a shape to move, resize, or rotate it. Drag a box to grab several.
- **Zoom (Z)** — click to zoom in, Shift-click to zoom out. The mouse wheel zooms too.
- **Pencil (Q)** — freehand. Becomes a path you can edit later.
- **Line (L)** — click and drag. Hold **Shift** for a straight 45° line.
- **Path (P)** — click for corners, drag for curves. Double-click or Enter to finish.
- **Rectangle (R)** / **Square**, **Ellipse (E)** / **Circle** — click and drag. Shift keeps them regular.
- **Text (T)** — click, type, then click away. Font, size, and bold/italic sit in the top bar once the text is selected.
- **Image** — place a picture from this device. It is embedded in the SVG.

Fill and stroke colours, width, opacity, blur, and alignment sit along the top once something is selected. **Layers** open from the right-hand handle: add, rename, hide, reorder.

**Undo** / **Redo** are in the top bar. **Ctrl/Cmd+Z** undoes. **Delete** removes the selection. **Ctrl/Cmd+A** selects everything on the current layer.

## File

The strip above the editor:

- **Open** — an `.svg` from this device (you can also drop one on the canvas).
- **Save SVG** — download the current drawing as an `.svg` file.
- **PNG** — a snapshot you can keep or send.

The SVG-Edit menu (logo, top-left) still has **Export**, document size, and editor preferences.

## Invite

**Invite** is in the bar **above** the app. Whoever opens the link sees **this drawing** and can edit it live. There is no account. Last change in wins if two people grab the same shape.

A guest who cannot write will see a message — the person who opened the app has to stay connected.

## Phone

Tap a tool, then drag on the canvas. Pinch or use **Zoom** to get closer. The tool strip is tight; rotate to landscape if the hits feel small. There is no right-click: use the top bar for fill vs stroke.

## What is saved

The drawing auto-saves **inside this app** on this device. Close it, open it later: the picture is where you left it. Sharing the GIF shares the drawing. A few embedded photos are fine; a page full of photographs makes the app heavy.
