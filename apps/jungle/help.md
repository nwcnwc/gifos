# Jungle

Jungle (Dou Shou Qi) is animal chess. Walk one of your animals into the other den to win. Stronger takes weaker — except the rat, who can take the elephant.

## The board

7 columns by 9 rows. Each side has a **den** in the middle of its back rank and three **traps** beside and in front of it. Two **rivers** sit in the middle.

Blue sits the far side and **moves first**. Red sits nearest you. Each piece shows its animal and its rank (8 elephant down to 1 rat).

## How to move

Tap (or click) a piece, then tap where it goes. Legal squares light up. Tap the same piece again to cancel. Pieces move one square orthogonally — no diagonals, no jumping over land.

There is no keyboard: mouse and finger do the same two taps. On a phone, the board is the whole control.

## Ranks

Elephant 8, Lion 7, Tiger 6, Leopard 5, Wolf 4, Dog 3, Cat 2, Rat 1.

- Higher rank captures lower. Equal rank captures equal.
- **Rat captures Elephant.** Elephant cannot capture Rat.
- A piece sitting in an *opponent* trap has rank 0 — anything can take it. Your own traps do not weaken you.
- You may never step into your **own** den.

## Water

Only the **Rat** may enter the river. A rat in the water can only fight another rat in the water, and cannot take the elephant from the water.

**Lion** and **Tiger** jump straight across empty water, onto land. A rat of either colour sitting on the jump path blocks it.

## How you win

- Enter the opponent’s den, or
- Capture their last animal, or
- Leave them with no legal move.

Captured animals show above the board. There is no score beyond that.

## Computer

On **New game**, pick **Computer** and **Blue** or **Red**. The computer thinks on this device. Blue always goes first, so if you pick Red, the computer moves first.

**Undo** takes back your last move and the computer’s reply. **New game** returns to the setup screen.

## Two here

**Two here** is hotseat: pass the device. Blue goes first. Same tap-then-tap.

## A live friend

Press **Play a friend**, then **Invite** in the bar **above** the app — that link is the room. First two people sit Blue and Red; anyone else watches. You only move your own colour. **Resign** gives the game to the other seat. After a win the next game starts on its own.

Invite is OS chrome. This game does not draw its own share button.

## What is saved

A local computer or hotseat game auto-saves in this file. Close the app and come back: the board is still there. A live friend game lives in the room, not in the save.
