# Hex Chess

Hexagonal chess on 91 hexes. Checkmate the other king. This is Gliński's game (1936), not the square board.

## How to move

Tap a piece, then tap a hex. Legal hexes light up. Tap the same piece again to put it down. On a computer that is a click; on a phone it is a tap. There is no keyboard, and you do not drag.

White sits at the near side. If you play Black against the computer, the board flips so your pieces are near you.

A pawn that reaches the far end of any file asks you to promote: queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

## The pieces (where it differs)

Each side has 18: king, queen, two rooks, two knights, **three** bishops, nine pawns.

- **Rook** — any distance through the edges of a hex (six directions).
- **Bishop** — any distance through the corners. Each bishop stays on one of the three colours; they never defend each other.
- **Queen** — rook plus bishop.
- **King** — one hex any way (twelve neighbours in the middle). **No castling.**
- **Knight** — a leap: two hexes along an edge, then one at 60°. In the middle it has twelve landings.
- **Pawn** — one hex straight forward. It captures one hex forward-left or forward-right through an **edge**, not a corner. From its starting hex it may step two. **En passant** is in. There is no double-step after that.

## Ending the game

Check, then checkmate, wins. Stalemate is **not** a draw: the side that left the other with no legal move wins. There is no 50-move rule and no threefold repetition.

**Undo** takes back your last move. Against the computer it takes back the computer's reply as well.

## Play

- **Computer** — you choose White or Black. It thinks on this device. It looks a little way ahead and never cheats.
- **Two here** — pass the device. White goes first.
- **Play a friend** — you sit one colour, they sit the other. After you press Play a friend, press **Invite** in the bar above the app and send the link. The first two people to join take the seats; anyone else watches. **Resign** is there once the game has a move on it.

Invite is not a button inside this game. It lives in the bar above.

## What is saved

The local game (computer or two-here) lives in this file. Close it, come back, the position is where you left it. A finished game is not restored. A live friend game lives in the room, not in the file.
