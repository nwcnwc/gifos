# Tuner

Record a **note**, then see its **pitch**. Nothing is uploaded. There is no live microphone inside the app.

## Record a note

1. Press **Record a note**.
2. Play or sing one pitch — a guitar string, a whistle, a hummed tone.
3. Stop when the recorder finishes (a couple of seconds is plenty).

The app then names:

- The **note** (A, C#, …) and octave
- The **frequency** in Hertz
- **Cents** sharp or flat of that note (0 is in tune)

A wave of the take is drawn so you can see whether the clip was loud enough. If it says there was no clear pitch, get closer, play louder, or try a simpler tone (whistle works well). Strong extra frequencies can throw the reading off — that is the same limit the original detector has.

## Not a live tuner

This is a take, then a reading. The needle does not move while you play. Record again for a new reading.

## Phone

A thumb on Record a note is the whole flow.

## What is saved

The last reading — note, Hertz, cents — stays in this app file.

Unofficial port of [PitchDetect](https://github.com/cwilso/PitchDetect) by Chris Wilson.
