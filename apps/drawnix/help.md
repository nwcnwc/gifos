# Drawnix

An all-in-one whiteboard: mind maps, flowcharts, and pencil sketches on **one infinite canvas**. The board auto-saves on this device.

## The jobs

- **Mind map** — grow topics from a centre. Double-click a node to type. **Enter** adds a sibling, **Tab** a child.
- **Flowchart** — boxes, diamonds, terminals, notes, and arrows (straight, elbow, or curve).
- **Freehand** — the pen, for scribbling next to the diagram.
- **Paste a Markdown outline** (Extra tools) and it becomes a mind map. **Paste Mermaid** and it becomes boxes and arrows (flowcharts, sequence, class; other diagram types land as a picture).

## Tools

Bottom (or side) bar:

- **Hand (H)** — pan the board. On a phone, drag with this, or pinch to zoom.
- **Selection (V)** — tap a shape to move, resize, recolour.
- **Mind (M)** — place a mind-map centre.
- **Text (T)**, **Arrow (A)**, **Shape** (rectangle **R**, ellipse **O**, triangle, diamond, and more).
- **Pen (P)** and **Eraser (E)**.
- **Image (Cmd/Ctrl+U)** — drop a picture on the canvas, or pick a file.
- **Extra tools** — Mermaid to Drawnix, Markdown to Drawnix.

The **app menu** (top) is Open, Save, Save As, export **PNG / SVG / JPG**, language, theme, and Clear Board. Undo and redo sit with the board; **Cmd/Ctrl+Z** and **Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z** work too.

A first-run tutorial (title, where the tools live, that data stays on this computer) is Drawnix’s own — not a second Help.

## Zoom, pan, select

Scroll or pinch to zoom (**Cmd/Ctrl +** and **−**, Fit to Screen, 100%). Hand tool pans. Two fingers pan on a phone. Click-drag with Selection to move. There is no separate “space to pan” like some boards — use **H**.

## Invite

**Invite** is in the bar **above** the app. It does **not** share this board. A guest who opens it gets their **own** empty canvas. There is no live collaboration and no cloud link. To show someone your work, **export** an image or a Drawnix file.

## What is saved

Everything you drew, the viewport, the current tool, theme, and language auto-save **inside this app** on this device. Close it, open it later: the canvas is where you left it.

Open / Save As also write a file you can keep or email. Pictures you drop live **in the board** — a few photos are fine; a board full of photographs makes the app heavy.

## Themes

Default, Colorful, Soft, Retro, Dark, Starry — from the toolbar. Language: English, 中文, Русский, عربي, Tiếng Việt.
