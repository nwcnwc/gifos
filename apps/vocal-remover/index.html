Good to know before you press Separate:

  • It’s slow. The app works through the song in six-second pieces, and each piece takes real effort.
  • On a computer without a usable graphics chip, expect about 10× the length of the song. On a phone, three to four times longer still — a four-minute song can take a couple of hours.
  • A graphics chip speeds this up a lot.
  • The app times itself on the first piece, then shows a live estimate. You can stop whenever you want.
  • Try “First 30 seconds” first to hear whether you like the result.
  • The first Separate also downloads the models it needs (67–120 MB, one time only).

Built on Ultimate Vocal Remover, the open-source separator by Anjok07 & aufr33 (MIT licence) — this app is its MDX‑Net method rewritten to run right here, on the ONNX Runtime Web engine (MIT). The models are UVR’s own, downloaded once and checked byte-for-byte against a fingerprint built into this app. Your audio is never uploaded — the app has no internet access of its own.
