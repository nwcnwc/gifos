3 · Settings

Separation is slow: the model listens to the track in six-second pieces and each piece is real work. On a device with a GPU it runs at roughly the speed of the music; on the processor alone, expect several times the track's length. Try 30 seconds first.

Stems

These are held in memory only — nothing this big is written into the app's GIF. Download what you want before you close it.

A port of Ultimate Vocal Remover (Anjok07 & aufr33, MIT) — its MDX-Net separation path, transcribed to JavaScript and run in the sandbox with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Model weights are UVR's own, downloaded once from the UVR model repository and checked against a SHA-256 pinned in this app. Nothing is uploaded, and the app has no network of its own.
