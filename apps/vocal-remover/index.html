Separation is slow. The model listens to the track in six-second pieces and each piece is real work. On the processor alone it takes roughly ten times the length of the track on a computer, and three to four times that again on a phone — where a four-minute song is a couple of hours. A device with a GPU is much faster. Either way the app measures the real rate from the first piece and tells you what is left, and you can stop at any point — but try 30 seconds first. UVR weights download the first time you separate, not at install — only the models that job needs.

A port of Ultimate Vocal Remover (Anjok07 & aufr33, MIT) — its MDX-Net separation path, transcribed to JavaScript and run in the sandbox with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). Model weights are UVR's own, downloaded once from the UVR model repository and checked against a SHA-256 pinned in this app. Nothing is uploaded, and the app has no network of its own.
