Ultimate Vocal Remover GUI — licence and credit
===============================================

This GifOS app is a port of the MDX-Net separation path of

    Ultimate Vocal Remover GUI
    https://github.com/Anjok07/ultimatevocalremovergui
    by Anjok07 and aufr33, with contributors credited below.

and it runs that project's own trained model weights, downloaded from the UVR
model repository.

WHAT THIS PORT TOOK, AND WHAT IT DID NOT
----------------------------------------
Taken and transcribed to JavaScript:

  separate.py            SeperateMDX.demix / .run_model / .initialize_model_settings
  lib_v5/tfc_tdf_v3.py   class STFT
  gui_data/constants.py  the DEFAULT_DATA settings the port runs at
  models/MDX_Net_Models/model_data/model_data.json
                         the per-model n_fft / dim_f / dim_t / compensate values

Not ported: the VR Architecture and Demucs paths, MDX23C, ensembles, secondary
models, denoise/deverb, pitch shifting, and the desktop GUI. This app is the
MDX-Net path at UVR's shipped defaults, and nothing else.
